# Making a character

The first time you play, as a guest or on your own, you make your character before you arrive in the world. You choose a name (one is suggested for your kind, and the button beside it suggests another), what kind of creature you are, how you look, and what your body, mind and spirit are like. For now each player has one character.

## Kind and looks

Every kind that stands in one place can be played: birdfolk, dwarves, elves, goblins, halflings, humans, kobolds, lizardfolk, minotaurs and orcs. Each kind has its own looks to choose from, such as hair, skin, coat or feathers, and height. Looks never change what you can do.

## Attributes

You have nine attributes, in three rows:

| Row | Power | Finesse | Resistance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Body | Strength | Agility | Toughness |
| Mind | Intellect | Perception | Will |
| Spirit | Presence | Empathy | Aura |

- **Strength:** force and carrying.
- **Agility:** precision, balance and moving quietly.
- **Toughness:** health, and standing up to injury, poison and disease.
- **Intellect:** reasoning, learning and arcane magic.
- **Perception:** noticing things, reactions and aim.
- **Will:** standing up to fear, confusion and sleep.
- **Presence:** commanding, persuading and frightening.
- **Empathy:** reading people and creatures, and divine magic.
- **Aura:** standing up to magic, curses and possession.

For now only some of them do anything: Strength decides how much you can carry and how hard you hit, Strength and Agility how well you fight, Agility and Perception how well you defend, Toughness your Health and Will your Mana. See [Fighting](fighting).

## The triangles

Each row is a triangle with its three attributes at the corners. Where you put the dot in it shares that row between them: in the middle all three are even, and at a corner that one is as high as your kind allows, while the other two are at their lowest.

The three triangles turn together round the middle. The row at the top gets an extra point, which raises all three of its attributes. Two rows level near the top share it, and rows below the middle get none of it, so every character leans one way.

The triangles stay the same way up as they turn, so each attribute keeps its corner: power at the top, finesse at the lower right, resistance at the lower left. The arrows on the ring show they turn.

Behind each triangle is a chart of its three attributes. Its inner layer, in gold, is what your kind adds. Every kind has the same bonus points in all: some put more into a few attributes, and the rest is shared evenly; the coloured layer round it is what your choices give. The values are listed beside the triangles as you go.

## Controls

| Action | Mouse | Touch | Keys |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Move a dot | Press on its triangle and drag | Slide from its triangle | 1, 2 or 3 picks a triangle; the arrow keys move its dot |
| Turn the triangles | Press beside them and drag round | Slide round beside them | Q and E |

When you have a name, press **Begin**.
