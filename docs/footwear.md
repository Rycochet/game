# Footwear

Footwear is one item for both feet: shoes, boots, sandals, clogs or slippers. It goes on the Middle layer of the feet, over socks, and [foot armour](armour/feet) goes over it. Leatherworkers sell shoes, boots and sandals, tailors sell slippers and carpenters sell clogs.

Every pair is made in its own way, and that shows in how it looks: how it is made, how high it comes, the shape of its toe and heel, how it is held on and how it is decorated. A pair that matches a style from history takes that style's name, such as Oxfords or Geta. Any other pair gets a plain name such as "Calf Boots". A plain name puts an unusual material first, as in "Silk Slippers".

## Kinds

| Kind | Covers | Layer | Made as | Named |
| ---- | ------ | ----- | ------- | ----- |
| Shoes | Feet, below the ankle | Middle | Sewn, soft, rawhide, woven | By their make (below) |
| Boots | Feet and ankle, up to the calf or knee | Middle | Sewn, soft, felted, wooden | By their make and height |
| Sandals | Feet; sandal boots lace up the shin | Middle | Strapped, woven | By their straps |
| Clogs | Feet | Middle | Wooden | By their sole and fastening |
| Slippers | Feet, below the ankle | Middle | Sewn, soft, felted | By their back and toe |

How each is made:

| Make | What it is |
| ---- | ---------- |
| Sewn | An upper sewn to a separate sole, which may have a heel |
| Soft | Sole and sides from one piece of leather or fur wrapped up round the foot |
| Rawhide | One piece of untanned hide gathered round the foot with thongs |
| Woven | Straw, bast, rope or grass, plaited or coiled |
| Felted | Felted wool in one piece |
| Strapped | A sole held on by straps, loops or a thong |
| Wooden | Carved wood, whole or as the sole |

### Shape

| Part | What it can be |
| ---- | -------------- |
| Height | Low (below the ankle), ankle, calf, knee; only cavalier boots go over the knee |
| Toe | Round, almond, pointed, long point (reaching well past the toes), square, broad (wider than the foot), upturned, or open (on sandals and wooden soles) |
| Heel | Flat, low, riding (stacked and angled), Louis (curved), or wooden teeth under the sole |
| Fastening | Slip-on, laces (closed or open), laces through loops, buckled straps, a plain strap, buttons, toggles, elastic sides, thongs round the shin, a toe thong or knob, a drawstring |
| Top of a boot | Plain, turned-down cuff in another colour, wide bucket, V notch at the front, scalloped, fur, or higher at the front |
| Decoration | Broguing (punched holes), embroidery, beads, slashing showing the lining, stitching, a pompom on an upturned toe, tassels, fringe, carving on wood, jewels (only on fine pairs), plaited straps |
| Under the sole | Nothing, hobnails, or irons at the heel and toe |

Long points are only made on sewn shoes and ankle boots, and broad toes only on sewn shoes, both with flat heels.

## Materials

- Uppers: leather, suede and buff leather; silk, wool, cotton and linen; fur.
- Rawhide shoes and some sandal straps: rawhide.
- Woven pairs: straw, rattan or coir rope.
- Felt boots and slippers: felt.
- Sewn and strapped pairs stand on leather soles; clogs on wood. Soft pairs have the upper's own sole, or leather under fur and wool.
- Leather, suede and cloth come in the usual dyes. Wood is bare or painted. Hide, fur and straw keep their own colours.

## Weights

| Piece | Weight (a pair) |
| ----- | --------------- |
| Woven shoes and sandals | about 0.2 to 0.25 kg |
| Sandals, soft and rawhide shoes, slippers | about 0.3 kg |
| Sewn shoes | about 0.6 kg |
| Wooden sandals (geta, nalins) | about 0.5 to 0.65 kg |
| Clogs | about 1 kg |
| Ankle boots | about 1 kg |
| Calf boots | about 1.4 kg |
| Felt boots | about 1.2 kg |
| Knee boots | about 1.8 to 2 kg |
| Cavalier boots | about 2.7 kg |

Heels, hobnails, irons and wide tops add a little, and cloth is lighter than leather. Bigger pairs weigh more.
