# Underclothing

Underclothes are what you wear next to the skin: shirts and chemises, braies, breast bands, loincloths, hose, socks, petticoats, linen coifs and wimples. They all go on the Skin layer, under your clothes, [armour](armour/) and [footwear](footwear), so they are mostly hidden. They are what people wore in the Middle Ages and before, with the same pieces from other parts of the world. Tailors sell them.

On Hard, underclothes stay on you when you die, while everything else you wear is dropped.

Every piece is made in its own way. One that matches a garment from history takes its name, such as Chemise or Fundoshi. A name puts an unusual material or fine linen first, as in "Silk Chemise" or "Lawn Shirt".

## Kinds

| Kind | Covers | Goes with |
| ---- | ------ | --------- |
| Shirt | Torso and arms (torso only if sleeveless), from the hip to the ankle | Braies, hose, a petticoat |
| Braies | Waist, hanging to the thigh, knee, calf or ankle | A shirt and hose, but not a loincloth or petticoat |
| Breast Band | Chest | Anything but a shirt |
| Loincloth | Waist and between the legs | Hose, but not braies or a petticoat |
| Hose | Legs and feet (footless and stirrup hose: legs only) | Braies, a shirt, a petticoat; socks only with footless or stirrup hose |
| Socks | Feet | Footless or stirrup hose |
| Petticoat | Waist, hanging to the knee, calf or ankle | Hose or stockings, but not braies or a loincloth |
| Linen Coif | Head | Not an arming cap |
| Wimple | Throat and chin | A linen coif |

Braies, loincloths and petticoats hang from the waist, so they count as worn on the waist alone. That is why hose go with them.

### Shape

| Kind | What it can be |
| ---- | -------------- |
| Shirt | Hip, thigh, knee, calf or ankle length; sleeveless, short, elbow, wrist or full sleeves; a slit, round, square, gathered, collared or wrapped neck; plain, widened with gores or gathered full. Long ones may be made for the night, or with stays over them |
| Braies | Thigh, knee, calf or ankle length; fitted, loose or full; on a belt or a drawstring; legs open, tied at the calf or tucked into the hose |
| Breast Band | Wound flat or twisted, a wide wrap to the waist, tied at the back, a panel tied round the neck, or with shoulder straps |
| Loincloth | A long strip, an apron on a cord, a narrow strip with strings, a triangle with strings, tied strings with a hanging end, or sewn briefs |
| Hose | Footed, with a strap under the foot, or footless; to the knee, the thigh or joined at the waist (sometimes with a codpiece); cut cloth, leather or knitted; tied to the braies belt, pointed to a doublet or gartered; plain, striped or with each leg a different colour; some have leather soles |
| Socks | Sewn, nålbound, felt, a wrapped cloth or knitted; whole or split toes; to the ankle, calf or knee |
| Petticoat | Knee, calf or ankle length; fitted, full or very full; plain, quilted or horsehair; on a drawstring, ties or wrapped |
| Linen Coif | A coif tied under the chin, an undercap, a hairnet or caul, or a nightcap |
| Wimple | A wimple round the throat and chin, or a pleated barbe |

## Materials

Mostly linen, as it washes well. Shirts, braies and coifs are also made in hemp, cotton, silk, wool and ramie; hose and socks are usually wool; petticoats wool or linen. Fine linen is called lawn. Hair shirts and horsehair petticoats are haircloth, some loincloths and hose are leather, and some socks are felt. Linen underclothes are usually undyed or white; hose, socks and petticoats come in more colours. Some are embroidered or edged with lace, and cauls of gold thread are only found on fine pieces.

## Weights

Typical figures for an ordinary-sized piece in its usual material. Size and material change them: silk is lighter, wool and haircloth heavier.

| Kind | About |
| ---- | ----- |
| Shirt | 200 to 500 g; stays add about 400 g |
| Braies | 150 to 350 g |
| Breast Band | 50 to 100 g |
| Loincloth | 30 to 150 g |
| Hose | 150 to 400 g a pair |
| Socks | 60 to 250 g a pair |
| Petticoat | 300 g to 1.5 kg; quilted wool ones up to about 2 kg |
| Linen Coif | 15 to 70 g |
| Wimple | About 60 g |
