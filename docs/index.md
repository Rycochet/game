# Hall of Worlds

Hall of Worlds is a multiplayer roguelike set in an infinite, procedurally generated world. Each world comes from a seed, with its own land, climate, weather, suns and moons, and places to explore: towns, farms, caves, buildings and more.

You can play alone on your own computer, or join a server with other people.

> **This game is at an early stage.** It changes often, much of it isn't there yet, and saves may be wiped between versions. These pages describe what exists now and leave out the details that are still moving.
