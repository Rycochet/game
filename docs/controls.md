# Controls

Everything can be done with a mouse or by touch, and on touch screens a tap is a click.

The keys below are the ones the game starts with. To change them, open Settings, Controls: each action can have two keys, and a key can be held with Ctrl, Alt, Shift or Super (Cmd, or the Windows key), as Shift + G. Esc can't be changed.

## Moving

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Touch |
| ------ | ------------------ | ----- |
| Walk | W, A, S, D, or 8, 4, 2, 6 on the number pad (hold to keep walking; two together walk diagonally), or hold an arrow on the movement pad | Hold an arrow on the movement pad |
| Travel somewhere | Click the place | Tap the place |
| Enter a place, take stairs, leave a site (in the wilderness, the hatched ground round a place you know leads back to the world map) | Click it, and walk there | Tap it |
| Up / Leave: up the stairs you stand on, or leave for the world map: the wilderness or a place's ground level from anywhere out in the open, a cave only from the edge of its map (or a Dev Area, from anywhere in it) | Shift + , (`<`), or . on the number pad, or the movement pad's Up ladder | The movement pad's Up ladder |
| Down / Explore: down the stairs you stand on, or explore the wilderness where you stand on the world map (going into a place you know of whose circle your own overlaps by about a fifth) | Shift + . (`>`), or 0 on the number pad, or the movement pad's Down ladder | The movement pad's Down ladder |
| Open or close doors: closes the open doors beside you, or if none are open, opens the closed door you face (or else one beside you) | O | Not yet |
| Talk to someone in a town: their quests, and a shop's wares (a "!" over a door has a quest for you, a "?" one to hand in, a grey "?" one of theirs you are still doing, a grey "!" one for later) | Click their door, and walk there | Tap the door |
| Turn to face a place | Right-click it | Hold a finger on it |
| Turn left or right (a tap turns an eighth, holding keeps turning) | Choose keys for Turn Left and Turn Right in Settings, Controls (none to begin with), or the movement pad's curved arrows | The movement pad's curved arrows |

The movement pad is the small panel at the bottom right, open when the game starts; the d-pad button in the corner opens and closes it, and its top edge drags it somewhere else. The game remembers whether it is open and where you left it. Its arrows sit round the middle like a d-pad, with Turn Left and Turn Right (curved arrows) either side of the up arrow, and Down (a ladder narrowing towards the bottom) and Up (a ladder narrowing towards the top) either side of the down arrow. The curved arrows turn you, never the camera, and are greyed out on the world map. The arrows go the way the keys do, by the screen, however the camera is turned, and keep walking as long as they are held, stopping when you let go or slide off them. The ladders are greyed out when they would do nothing; hold the pointer or a finger on one to see what it does here.

In the wilderness you walk the land between places on foot, 16 steps to each step on the world map, and find places by walking up to them. Going into a place from the world map, by clicking it or by Down beside it, puts you in the wilderness at its way in, or where you last walked out of it, with the place's ground level set into the land round it: a town is known whole as soon as you step inside it. You find a place, and it goes on your map, when you see its way in or step inside it. Stairs down lead into the rest of a place, and back up into the wilderness. You can't explore from water, or leave the wilderness from inside a building or cave or into water.

You can't change floors in a fight: for 10 seconds after you last attack or are attacked, stairs, Up, Down and the edge of a map do nothing. Walking onto stairs then leaves you standing on them, and Up or Down takes them once the fight is over.

How fast you go depends on your kind of creature, what you carry, and the ground: the heavier the load, the slower everything is.

In places, the wilderness and the Hall of Worlds you walk freely, any way at all: the keys walk the way they point on the screen, however the camera is turned, and walking into a wall at an angle slides along it. Walking straight at water or a hole jumps it. Clicking somewhere walks there by the shortest way, cutting across open ground at any angle. On the world map you still go a step at a time.

## Facing and sight

You face the way you walk, the way you turn, or towards whoever you use an ability on. In places, the wilderness and the Hall of Worlds you see in a cone ahead of you (about a third of a turn for people; animals that are hunted see almost all round), and only a step around you behind: what is outside it is shown as you remember it. Creatures see the same way, so you can creep up behind one without it noticing, but they turn towards a fight they hear and towards whoever strikes them. Observation (under Survival) widens your cone and lets you see a little further, and grows as you spot creatures. On the world map you see all round.

You can't walk onto an enemy. Walking onto anyone else swaps places with them.

Doors are open, closed or broken. Walking into a closed door opens it, away from you, and clicking somewhere beyond one walks you there, opening the doors on the way; clicking a closed door itself walks up to it and opens it. Closed doors stop sight and light. You can't close a door while anyone or anything is in its doorway, and a broken door can't be closed at all. A secret door opens towards you, once you have found it.

Some doors have a lock, which you can see. Whether one is locked you only find out by trying it. With its key anywhere in your pack, walking into a locked door unlocks it and opens it; without, "Locked" shows and it stays shut. Keys are carried by the creatures of the place, so a locked door's key is always somewhere you can get to without going through it.

Anyone with legs can kick: put Kick on your action bar from the Abilities page. Kicking your target shoves it a step away, or hurts it more if it has nowhere to go; with no target in reach, you kick the closed door beside you. Enough kicks break a door open for good.

## Targeting

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Touch |
| ------ | ------------------ | ----- |
| Target a creature | Right-click it | Hold a finger on it |
| Clear your target | Right-click empty ground | Hold a finger on empty ground |
| Target the next enemy further out, or the nearest | Tab | |
| Target the next enemy further in | Shift + Tab | |

A black ring turns under your target. Tab and Shift + Tab go through enemies from nearest to furthest, or through everyone in sight if no enemies are. Your target stays your target when it goes out of sight. If you have no target in sight, the first enemy to come up to you becomes it, and when your target dies the nearest enemy you can see takes its place.

## Camera

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Touch |
| ------ | ------------------ | ----- |
| Turn and tilt | Drag with the right button | Twist two fingers to turn, move both up or down to tilt |
| Turn the camera left or right | 7 or 9 on the number pad | |
| Zoom | Mouse wheel, = and -, or + and - on the number pad | Pinch |
| Back to the starting view | Home, or 5 on the number pad | Hold the minimap (with the mouse or a finger) |

A camera key moves the camera a little each time you press it, and keeps it moving for as long as you hold it.

## Typing

Click or tap a box to type in it. While you type, the line showing where your typing goes stays solid; when you stop, it fades in and out. These keys can't be changed. On a Mac, Cmd takes the place of Ctrl, and Option moves by words.

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Touch |
| ------ | ------------------ | ----- |
| Move along the writing | Left and Right; with Ctrl, a word at a time | Tap the place |
| Go to the start or the end | Home or End | Tap there |
| Select | Shift with any of the moves above, drag across it, or Shift + click | Drag a finger across it |
| Select a word, or all of it | Double-click, or triple-click | Double-tap, or triple-tap |
| Select all of it | Ctrl + A | Triple-tap |
| Delete | Backspace or Delete; with Ctrl, a word at a time | The on-screen keyboard |
| Cut, copy and paste | Ctrl + X, Ctrl + C, Ctrl + V | |

Typing or pasting with something selected writes over it. A box you leave keeps what was selected in it, in a paler colour; clicking back into it first only lets you type there again, and a second click puts the line where you clicked.

## While the system menu is open

The menu takes all the keys, the wheel and the gestures, so nothing behind it reacts. In single player the game is paused too, unless Online is on (and, if you turn on "Pause When Not in Focus" under Settings, Gameplay, while another window is in front). On a networked server it isn't, and a journey you clicked on carries on: see [Pausing](client#pausing).
