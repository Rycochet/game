# Controls

Everything can be done with a mouse or by touch, and on touch screens a tap is a click.

The keys below are the ones the game starts with. To change them, open Settings, Controls: each action can have two keys, and a key can be held with Ctrl, Alt, Shift or Super (Cmd, or the Windows key), as Shift + G. Esc can't be changed.

## Moving

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Touch |
| ------ | ------------------ | ----- |
| Take steps | Arrow keys or W, A, S, D | |
| Travel somewhere | Click the place | Tap the place |
| Enter a place, take stairs, leave a site | Click it, and walk there | Tap it |
| Explore the wilderness where you stand on the world map | Shift + . (`>`) | The Explore button |
| Leave the wilderness, from anywhere out in the open | Shift + , (`<`) | The Leave button |

In the wilderness you walk the land between places on foot, 16 steps to each step on the world map, and find places by walking up to them. A place you walk into from the wilderness leads back out into it; one you enter from the world map leads back to the world map. You can't explore from water, or leave the wilderness from inside a building or cave or into water.

How fast you go depends on your kind of creature, what you carry, and the ground: the heavier the load, the slower everything is.

You can't walk onto an enemy. Walking onto anyone else swaps places with them.

## Camera

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Touch |
| ------ | ------------------ | ----- |
| Turn and tilt | Drag with the right button | Twist two fingers to turn, move both up or down to tilt |
| Zoom | Mouse wheel | Pinch |
| Back to the starting view | Home | Reset View in the [debug menu](debug-menu) |

## Maps and menus

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Touch |
| ------ | ------------------ | ----- |
| Open or close the full map | M, or click the minimap | Tap the minimap |
| Move and zoom the full map | Drag, mouse wheel | Drag, pinch |
| Open the system menu | Esc, or click the gear in the top-right corner | Tap the gear |
| Windowed or full screen (native only) | Alt+Enter, or Settings, Video | Settings, Video |
| Close what is on top | Esc, or click outside it (not the startup menu: press Play) | Tap outside it |
| Open or close your character, clothes and inventory | C or I, or click the person at the bottom left | Tap the person |
| Pick up an item, and put it down | Press on it, drag, let go; or click it, then click where it goes | Hold a finger on it, drag, let go |
| Turn an item you are holding | Right-click or R | Tap with a second finger |
| Put back an item you are holding | Esc, or drop it where it can't go | Drop it where it can't go |
| See an item's name | Point at it | Tap it |
| Open a bag | Double-click it | Double-tap it |
| Put on or take off clothes | Drag them onto the clothes panel, or off it into a grid | The same |
| Give something to someone near | Drag it onto their name in the Nearby panel | The same |
| Throw an item away, with all that is in it | Drop it on the bin under your inventory. Take it back until you throw away something else, which destroys it | The same |
| Open the [debug menu](debug-menu) | Backtick (`` ` ``), or click the warning button at the top left | Tap the warning button |

Esc goes to whatever is on top first: a text box, a dropdown, the debug menu or the full map, and only then the system menu.

## While the system menu is open

The menu takes all the keys, the wheel and the gestures, so nothing behind it reacts. In single player the game is paused too, unless Online is on (and, if you turn on "Pause When Not in Focus" under Settings, Gameplay, while another window is in front). On a networked server it isn't, and a journey you clicked on carries on: see [Pausing](client#pausing).
