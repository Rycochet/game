# client: widgets

The interface pieces, so everything looks alike. Use these rather than styling Bevy's `Node`s directly. How they should look and what is planned: `docs/context/ui-design.md`. Not what any screen shows or does: that stays with the module using them.

## Public API

- `Spinner`: give a sized node it to show a turning spinner while it is on show.
- `OverInterface(bool)` (resource, `PreUpdate`): the pointer is on a panel, a node that `stops_presses`, a button or an element of markup this frame (`dom::Hit` has a target); for what the pointer does to the map behind (`target`: a right click on nothing clears the target).
- `WidgetsPlugin`; `WidgetSystems` (system sets, `Presses`, `Front`, then `Modal`): anything covering the screen reads presses in `Modal`, or `Front` to be ahead of those (the system menu); anything else taking clicks in `PreUpdate` runs after both.
- `INTERFACE_PERCENTS`: the sizes Settings offers after Auto. `TouchOnly(bool)` (resource): the device has only a touch screen (browsers say, with `any-pointer: fine`; natively never), so touch is on whatever the setting.
- `INK`, `PARCHMENT`, `CLOSE_PIXELS`, `BUTTON_PADDING`, `PANEL_EDGE`.
- `TextSize` (`Small`, `Medium`, `Large`, `Title`), with `.font()`: all writing is one of these. `WARNING_TEXT`: the colour of words saying something can't be done.
- `panel()`, `panel_title(text)`: a parchment panel with a raised edge (give its `Node` a border of `PANEL_EDGE`) and a soft shadow, placed by absolute position, and the writing for its title (drawn `TITLE_LIFT` above its place by a `UiTransform`, as Lexend leaves more room above capitals, so callers may still add their own `Node`).
- `HoverTip(words)`: on a node, its words show in the tooltip when the pointer (or a finger held down) stays anywhere on it or what is in it for half a second, even over a disabled part, unless something else is named. For more than a button's name: Settings' rows. Tooltips wrap at 420 interface pixels, are in front of everything (full-screen layers too), and wait by the real clock, as Bevy's `Time` stops while single player is paused (button looks and ripples go by it too).
- `raised_box()`, `spawn_tooltip(holder, marker)`: a box standing up from what is behind it; a dark box naming something, with `marker` on its text. `tooltip_box(node)`: the same dark box, for more than a name (a `popup`). For markup's own tooltip box (`dom`'s `Look::tip`): `tooltip_colours()`, `TOOLTIP_BOX_EDGE`, `tooltip_trim()` (its edge colour and shadow).
- `spawn_button(parent, state, label, size, node)`, `ButtonState` (`Normal`, `Selected`, `Active`, `Disabled`, `DisabledSelected`; a component, change it to restyle), `spawn_icon_button(parent, state, icon, node)`, `spawn_small_icon_button(parent, state, icon, node)` (22 px across, for controls on the edge of something, such as a grid), `spawn_check_button(parent, state, checked, label, size, node)` (`None` leaves the checkbox's space, so words line up), `spawn_close_button(parent, node)` (a normal button, with a letter X). `ButtonStyle` (`Plain`, `Main`, `Quiet`, `Folder`, `Danger`: red with light words, for destroying what can't be had back): put it on a button for how much it stands out. `ButtonTooltip(words)`: its name, shown after a short wait. Hovered and pressed looks and the ripple need nothing from the caller: anything with a `ButtonState` gets them, including buttons with their own hit tests.
- `Pressable` (put it on a button): clicks on it become `ButtonPressed(entity)`, unless disabled, and go no further.
- `spawn_moveable_panel(commands, title, at)`: returns the panel and the entity to put its contents in. It fits its contents, has lighter stripes either side of its title to tell it from a static panel, is moved by its title bar and despawned by its close button; it opens in front of other panels; a press on it brings it to the front, as does `RaisePanel(entity)`. `MoveablePanel` (on the panel) has `start_at(place)`, which changes where it first goes until it has been placed, and its title's writing carries `PanelTitleOf(panel)`: both for `dom`, whose `panel moveable` follows its element's `title`, `left` and `top`. Only `dom` and the debug menu's panels spawn these now.
- `spawn_grip_panel(commands, PanelPlace)`: the same with no title or close button (owner, 2026-11-08), moved by a narrow grip along its top: the stripes thinner (1 px), closer together and nearer the edge, the room round them gripping too, for fingers. Hide it with `Display::None` to keep its place. `PanelPlace::TopLeft(at)`, `BottomRight(gap)` (its corner that far from the window's) or `Share(share)` (its top-left at that share of the window, as saved), worked out once it is first laid out. `PanelMoved { panel, share }` (message, from `place_panels`): a drag let go of a moveable panel, and where its top-left corner is now as a share of the window, for an owner keeping its place between runs. The movement pad; another kind of widget later.
- `HoldButton`: put it on a button (not `Pressable`) for it to count as pressed only while held: by the mouse button that went down on it while the pointer stays on it, or by the finger that landed on it while that stays on it. Sliding off or letting go lets it go (coming back on with the mouse still down holds it again); the tap a lifting finger makes doesn't hold it again. `held()` says whether it is; becoming held sends `ButtonPressed` once. Disabled, it is never held. It shows the pressed look while held, by a finger too. Presses on it stop at its panel, so they never reach the map. Debug API kind `hold_button`, set true or false.
- `spawn_switch(parent, state, on, node)`: a switch 52 × 32 (its size is its own), with a tick and a cross in its track and a thumb covering one. A press flips `Switch` and sends `SwitchFlipped(entity, on)`; setting `Switch` sends nothing. Disabled by `ButtonState::Disabled` (or `DisabledSelected`). Give it a `UiId` so its slide survives the page being built again. No ripple: the thumb slides instead. Every on/off setting in Settings is one.
- `spawn_colour_picker(parent, ColourPicker { swatches, names, selected }, size, node)`: square swatches edge to edge, wrapping onto more lines, in a box drawn as an active button (give `node` its width); each is a line of `size` tall, so one line stands as tall as a dropdown. A `Swatch` is a `Colour` or a `Picture` stretched to fill it (a pattern, a gradient). Swatches are buttons (hover, press, ripple; tooltip with the name); the chosen one is `Active` with a white edge inside. A press sends `ColourChosen(picker, index)`; setting `selected` only restyles. Debug API kind `colour_picker`, set by index or name. `SAMPLE_GRADIENT`: a gradient picture for samples.
- `spawn_slider(parent, value, track, width)`: a track (a picture, or plain) with a mark along it. Values are `0..=1`. `SliderMoved(entity, value)` says the player moved it; add a marker to tell which. The mark sits on an invisible rail half a mark in from each end, so it stays inside the frame; the pointer is read along the same rail, keeping the mark's centre under it.
- `field_label(words)`: small words to go above a box. `spawn_text_box(parent, TextBox { text, prefix, digits_only, takes_keys, error, suffix }, size, node)` (`suffix`: grey words after the text and caret, a `TextSpan` of the label): sunken, its edge showing `error`, then the keyboard, then the pointer; a click or tap gives it the keyboard. The caret (a thin line, solid for half a second after moving, then fading in and out twice a second; always solid with Reduce Motion) goes anywhere: Left/Right (Ctrl or Option by word, Cmd to the ends), Home/End, Shift to select, Backspace/Delete (Ctrl by word), Ctrl+A, cut, copy and paste; Up and Down are left to the owner. A click puts the caret, a drag or Shift+click selects, a double click takes a word and a triple click everything. A box keeps its selection without the keyboard, paler; a click giving it the keyboard back leaves the selection, and a second click puts the caret. Writing too long for the box slides along to keep the caret in sight, cut at the padding; it never widens the box (the label is placed absolutely, so the box is as big as its `node` and min height say). An owner changing `text` puts the caret at the end. `accepts_items`: items written in (`${item:<uuid>}`) show as their pictures, framed in their quality's colour and named on hovering, and count as one place for the caret, selection and deleting (copied whole); an item from the inventory, a bag, a shop or the ground let go on the box is written in at its caret, and goes back where it was (`common::TakesItemText`, `DroppedOnText`). In other boxes such text is plain.
- `spawn_item_text(parent, text, size, colour, node)`, `ItemText(text)`: writing to show, not edit, with any items in it shown as in a box that takes them; change `ItemText` to change it. Show Widgets has one repeating its sample box. It is at least a line of its text size tall (plus its border) unless `node` sets `min_height`, as empty writing has no height. `takes_keys` keeps all keys from the game while it has them (Return, Esc or a click elsewhere gives them up); off for a box in something that reads keys itself, which sets `TextFocus` to its box and reads `TextEdited(entity)` and `SoftKeyPressed`.
- `spawn_key_box(parent, KeyBox { binding, warned }, state, size, node)`: a box holding a `common::Binding`, sunken like a text box (`state` `Normal` or `Disabled`). A click or tap makes it wait for a key (one at a time; a text box with the keyboard gives way), taking every key from the game meanwhile, Esc included: the first key that isn't a modifier, with the modifiers held (Shift + G), or a modifier pressed and let go alone (Shift). Esc or another press cancels; Backspace empties it. The box shows the choice itself and sends `KeyChosen(entity, Option<Binding>)`; its owner may change `binding` again (a key moved elsewhere) and sets `warned` for the error edge. Keys not in `common::controls`' list are ignored. Debug API kind `key_box`, set by the saved name (`Shift+KeyG`, or `""`).
- `ROW_TINT`: the background of every other row of a table (Settings, Controls).
- `spawn_dropdown(parent, Dropdown { options, selected, searchable, icons, greyed }, size, node)`: `DropdownChosen(entity, index)` says what the player chose. With `searchable`, the open list has a text box with the keyboard, narrowing it as letters are typed. `icons` (one per option, or none) go beside each option in the list; options marked in `greyed` show disabled and can't be chosen. A blank option is drawn as a space, so it stands a line high like the rest. `spawn_icon_dropdown(parent, dropdown, node)`: the same as a small icon button showing the chosen option's icon (the inventory's filters); `icons` may be filled in after spawning.
- `spawn_toggle_row(parent, labels, selected, size)`: joined buttons of which one is chosen, to pick an option. `spawn_tabs(parent, labels, selected, size)`: the same as folder tabs, to switch views. Both return the row; `TabChosen(row, index)` says the player chose another. `ChooseTab(row, index)` chooses one from code (a key), without `TabChosen`. `set_tabs(world, row, labels)` gives a row other words, the first chosen (`dom`'s `Dom::set_options`).
- `spawn_ability(parent, AbilityLook { icon, name, passive, usable, colour })`: an ability, `ABILITY` (1.5 cells) across: its white icon with a 1 px black outline (the icon in black nudged each way behind it) over `colour`, square if active, round if passive, greyed on grey with a dull red frame if it can't be used now, named on hovering (`HoverTip`). Returns the frame.
- `attribute_colour(leans, Shade)`: the colour of something leaning on these attributes: the rows' colours (`row_rgb`: Body red, Mind blue, Spirit green, the creation screen's triangles') mixed by how many of them are in each row, then shaded (owner, 2026-11-07): `Shade::Active` darkened to 0.4, `Shade::Passive` 0.55 of the way to white (pastel), `Shade::Skill` in between, the rows' own colours, as a skill just is and serves either kind of ability; `Shade::of_ability(passive)`. Grey-brown for nothing. For abilities, and skills next.
- `spawn_progress_bar(parent, full, (track, fill), node, words)`, `Progress(full)`: a bar with writing over it, in whatever colours suit where it is.
- Grids of items are drawn by `dom` (`GridShown`), from `covering()` nodes and `at_cell(x, y)`. `spawn_item_cells(parent, ItemLook, shape, corner, pressable, on_each)`: an item's cells, with `on_each(x, y)` giving what goes on each and on its label. `ItemLook` (with `turned`, and `dimmed` for something a filter fades: the grid's colour over its cells, `Floating`, so it can still be picked up and named) carries the picture, if the kind has a model: it then goes behind transparent cells, with an edge only round the outside of the shape, and no colour or label. `item_picture(look, pixels, node, rotation)`, `PICTURE_PIXELS_PER_CELL`, `item_colour`, `item_label`, `item_edge`, `item_edge_width`, `GRID_COLOUR`, `GRID_LINE`, `ITEM_EDGE`, `ALLOWED`, `REFUSED` (these colours are in `theme`), `ITEM_LABEL_FONT`, `HELD_ITEM_Z`.
- `ItemTooltip(Option<ItemTip { words, quality, at }>)`: set it in `Update` to show a name beside the pointer this frame, on a dark box, in its quality's colour. `ItemName { words, quality }`: on an item's cells and label, for its name to show on hovering, unless one has been asked for. `Floating`: on what follows the pointer, or lies over a grid's cells, so it doesn't count as being under it.
- `AutoScroll` (component): on a scroll area, it waits 2 s, scrolls down slowly, waits 4 s at the bottom, goes back up, and again, while there is more than fits. Any scrolling by hand removes it; insert it again (and set `ScrollPosition` to the top) to restart.
- `UiId::parts()`: its form's name and its own, for code matching on them; `index()`: its place in a list.
- `spawn_wide_scroll_area(parent, max_height)`: a scroll area as wide as what holds it (markup's `scroll`).
- `UiId::new(form, name)`, `UiId::of(form, id, name)` (or `.in_one(id)`; `.which()` reads it back), `.at(index)`: a widget's name, as HTML's forms and ids, written `form/name`, `form#<id>/name` (one of several forms alike, as bags) and `name.<index>` (one of a list). Insert it on what a `spawn_*` returns; the form is only a name each widget carries, not a node round them. Every input widget should have one, unique among those on show (a warning is logged when two have the same). Forms in use: `hud` (corner buttons), `menu`, `quit`, `going_online`, `going_offline`, `online_failed`, `refused`, `settings` (tabs, close), `settings.gameplay`, `settings.controls`, `settings.interface`, `settings.graphics`, `settings.keep_graphics`, `credits`, `connect`, `connect.certificate`, `connect.forget`, `server_options`, `debug.menu`, `debug.submenu`, `debug.<panel>` (each debug panel), `inventory`, `bag#<id>`, `ground`, `split`, `nearby`, `trade#<who>`, `planet`, `abilities`, `skills`, `step_pad`. Moveable panels are `<form>/panel`. The minimap's buttons have none: they keep their own hit tests, so pressing them by id wouldn't reach them.
- Scroll areas with a `UiId` come back to their place when built again (a page rebuilt after a change, a panel opened again), for the rest of the session. Every area, with an id or not, keeps its place as a share of how far it can go, so it stays at much the same place when the window, the interface size or its contents change size; a hidden one keeps its share until shown. Credits has none: it restarts at the top.
- `spawn_filling_scroll_area(parent)`: the same, but taking all the room left in a column whose height is set (its width too), as Settings' Graphics page does.
- A scroll area's bar shows only while there is more than fits, unless the area has `dom::AlwaysBars` or the player has Always Show Scroll Bars on (`Preferences::always_show_scroll_bars`).
- `spawn_scroll_area(parent, max_height)`: returns the entity to put contents in. As tall as they are up to `max_height`, then they scroll by the wheel (which then doesn't reach the camera), a finger, or the bar beside it. What is scrolled out of sight still counts as being there: looking for what is under the pointer in one, use `shown_at(at, node, transform, clip)`. `logical_rect(node, transform)`: where a node is on the screen.
- `Aura::of(item)`, `aura_glow(aura, mask, node)`: a glow never past the item's frame (owner): round a picture's black edge, behind it, or in from the edges of plain cells, over them. It pulses gently, with a band of light drifting across. Blessed and cursed are colours in `Aura::colours`; magical with one of them fades between the two. `spawn_item_cells` does this itself, so only something drawing an item its own way needs it.
- The material givers (`give_rings`, `give_set_edges`, `give_auras`) use `try_insert`: a node may be despawned before its material lands (the Skills page once crashed on that), and that is skipped, not a crash.
- `set_edge(quality, border, out)`: a node drawn as a metal edge on the sides `border` has, standing `out` beyond its parent's inside on just those sides, so a shape's cells join into one frame (each cell passes its own item edge's sides); `spawn_item_cells` and `ItemTip { set }` use it, so only something drawing an item its own way needs it.
- `queued_stripe(width, inset)`: yellow and black diagonal stripes `width` wide round the inside of the parent, `inset` in from its edges (inside an automatic slot's metal edge on the action bar), chasing round clockwise; not what the pointer is on. The action bar's queued abilities and a sample in Show Widgets.
- `skill_ring(SkillRing)`: a node covering its round parent, drawing a band at its edge filled clockwise from the top by `fill` in `quality`'s colour over a dark track, and, if `specialised`, a second, thinner band inside it by `special` as a set item's metal edge in the Artifact colour (owner, 2026-11-07). `SkillRing::of(value, specialised)`: full at 100 (`FULL`), a sliver once anything is learned, the whole ring in Common to Legendary by fifths; a specialised one fills its second ring from 100 to 120 (owner, 2026-11-07). The Skills page and a row in Show Widgets.
- `stops_presses(entity)`: put it on a node for presses on it to go no further.
- `spawn_fullscreen_layer(commands, depth, content)`: a hidden, centred full-screen panel, 90% of the window for depth 0 and 75% for depth 1 (`layer_share`), darkening everything outside it. Returns the layer (show and hide it; a press outside the panel is its `ButtonPressed`, which should close only that layer) and the panel to fill.
- `spawn_dialog(commands, depth, title)`: `Dialog { layer, content, buttons }`, a panel fitting its contents with its buttons along the bottom on the right.
- `spawn_sized_layer(commands, depth, width, height, content)`: the same with a panel of this size rather than its depth's (`Val::Auto` fits its contents).
- `darkening(depth)`, `leaves_presses(entity)`, `layer_z(depth)`: for a panel placing itself, as the full map does: the darkening alone, with presses on it hidden from widgets underneath but left for its owner to read from the mouse.

## Notes

- Interface size: Auto is the smaller of width ÷ 1920 and height ÷ 1080 of the window's logical pixels (owner's choice, so a maximised 1080p screen is today's size); a percentage otherwise; always 50% to 300%, and at least 100% with touch on. Everything built from `Node`s grows with it except the minimap and full map (see there). Hit tests, panel places and anything at the pointer are in interface pixels (`UiPointer`, `logical_rect`, `shown_at`); window sizes and `TouchGesture`'s positions and drags are converted with `in_interface` / `interface_area`. The soft keyboard's places go back to window pixels for the browser.
- Sliders by touch: a tap moves the mark there, and a drag starting on one moves it (`drag_sliders`, before `take_presses`, which would otherwise take a drag on a panel).
- Dropdowns: an open list takes all input, in `Presses` so nothing else sees it. Up and Down move the highlight, Return chooses it (or the first), Esc or a click outside closes it, and the wheel or a finger scrolls. Only a window of `ROWS` (10) rows is drawn rather than clipping more, as a clipped node still takes presses here. Used by Settings (interface size), Item Pictures' pages and, as icon dropdowns, the inventory's filters; plain ones also in Show Widgets.
- On-screen keyboard: browsers only allow focusing an input during the tap, so a `pointerup` listener on the canvas focuses the hidden input if the tap is on a box, or a dropdown with search, whose places are sent each frame. Untested on a real iPad.
- By touch: a tap arrives as a click (`touch`), so buttons and close buttons just work. A title bar moves by `TouchGesture::drag`, using where the finger landed (`drag_start`), since by the time a drag is known the finger may have left a thin bar. A drag starting anywhere else on a panel is taken and does nothing.
- Moveable panels stay hidden until first laid out and placed, so they never flash at the top left. They keep their share of the window as it changes size (`in_new_window`), and don't move at all while it is minimised.
- Close buttons on moveable panels have a margin that also counts as on them, for fingers; above and below, it overlaps the panel's padding rather than making the title bar taller. The panel's padding is smaller at the top (`TOP_PADDING`), so the room above the stripes matches the room beside them (owner).
- The minimap's close and floor buttons look like widgets but keep their own hit tests from its layout constants (and the tests built on them), so they aren't `Pressable`.
- Layers: maps 10, warning button 15, moveable panels from 30 up, full-screen layers from 1,000,000 (debug menu, full map, View Planet at depth 50), open dropdown lists at depth 100 (above all of them), loading screen above all. Outside a layer's panel, each layer's darkening adds up (owner's design), so nothing under one can be used, whatever is behind it.
- Only the front-most thing under the pointer gets a press: a button under another panel gets nothing. What is in front comes from `dom::Hit`, one search a frame that markup shares: `track_pointer` lights up, and `take_presses` presses, the nearest button or panel part to the target, going outwards through what holds it, so nothing covered by a panel or by markup reacts. `Pressable`, `ButtonState` and panel parts require `dom::Solid`. Tests of either run `dom::find_now` first, and build their panels as the game does, the parts as children of the panel.
- Shader uniforms must be a multiple of 16 bytes, or WebGL 2 (the web build) refuses the pipeline and the game quits on its first frame; native is fine either way, so nothing else catches it. Pad a short struct with unused `f32`s, the same in the Rust struct and the shader (see `project.md`).
- A text box's label is made again by a command that leaves a label that has gone alone (`queue_silenced` in `text_box/show.rs`): its panel may be closed in the frame its words change, which used to stop the game.
- A scroll bar's thumb is never more than the whole bar: with "Always Show Scroll Bars" on and everything fitting, it used to stick out below its area.
