# client: debug_menu

The debug menu: backtick or the warning button top left opens a full-screen layer (`widgets`, 90% of the window) listing actions, filtered by typing, chosen by click, tap, arrow keys and Return. Entries with a submenu end in "..." (`DebugAction::label`, by `offers_choice`), and their plain name is the submenu's title (owner's convention); so does Teleport to Gate, whose panel is only a choice of gates (owner's name for it). Replaces the old demo keys. Works in native and web builds.

## Public API

- `DebugMenuPlugin`.
- `DebugAction` (message): do this, as if chosen. Used by `dev_tools` (`HOW_DEBUG_PANEL`, and the debug API's `client.debug_action`, which finds it with `DebugAction::named`; `DebugAction::names` lists them).
- `DebugMenu` (resource): `open`, `filter`; `open_with` (native, for `HOW_DEBUG_MENU`).
- `AutoWalk(bool)` (resource): the player walks east one step per frame on the world map.

## Notes

- Ids (`widgets::UiId`): each panel's form is `form_of(action)` in `panels/open.rs` (`debug.give_item`, `debug.item_pictures`, ...), with the panel itself `<form>/panel`. Give Item's widgets are named in `give.rs` (`count`, `less`, `more`, `size`, `identical`, `quality`, `set`, `favour`, `magic`, `kind`, `seed`, `new_seed`, `give`) and its Form line by field (`give_option::ui_name`); Stairs, Fields and Herbs (`debug.stairs`, ...) have `previous` and `next`; World Time `back.N`, `on.N`, `time`, `rate.N`; Generate POI (`debug.generate_poi`) `ruin.N`, `seed`, `new_seed`, `generate`; Dev Area (`debug.dev_area`) `size`, `generator`, `mobs`, `less`, `count`, `more`, `seed`, `new_seed`, `generate`; Create Mob (`debug.create_mob`) `kind`, `attitude`, `gear`, `still`, `seed`, `new_seed`, `create`; Go to World `seed`, `new_seed`, `go`; Teleport to Gate (`debug.teleport_to_gate`) `gate.N` (N from 0); Item Pictures `page`, `quality`; Suns and Moons `colour.N`, `intensity.N`; Show Widgets each sample by its words. The menu's layers are `debug.menu` with `filter`, `close`, `previous`, `next` at the layer (`debug.menu/filter.0`); its entries have none, as `client.debug_action` chooses them by name. A test spawns each panel and checks its ids differ.

- Owner's design, after RimWorld's: choosing an entry closes the menu; a toggle shows its new state for `TOGGLE_SECONDS` (0.4, owner: just past the press's ripple) first. Disabled entries stay visible and do nothing. Columns fill top to bottom, then overflow into the next.
- Claude's choices: Return with nothing highlighted takes the first entry that can be used; arrow keys can highlight disabled entries; Left and Right move the filter's caret until Up or Down starts a highlight, and only then move it; Esc, its close button and a click outside it close it too; backtick does nothing over the full map, as the menu would be the bigger of the two (owner's rule: a layer above is smaller); turning Cycle Temperature off puts the snow line straight back; only one of Teleport and Change Cell Mode can be on. Reset View is here as tablets have no Home key (Home still works).
- While open it takes all input: `take_input` clears keys, wheel and gestures, and its layer stops presses, so nothing behind it reacts.
- With the filter box focused in a browser, the game's keys see nothing, so its Return and Escape come through `widgets::SoftKeyPressed`, and arrow keys on an attached keyboard don't work until tapping elsewhere is added.
- Panels stay open with greyed buttons where they can't act. Stairs, fields and herbs changes are only what is drawn; the next level goes back to its own, except herbs (`HerbGrowthShift` stays).
- Suns and Moons (`bodies.rs`): a row per sun, a gap, a row per moon: a swatch of its colour and a slider along its kind's colours (`shared::celestial::colour_along`), and for suns a slider for intensity (`MOST_INTENSITY`; above about 1.15 on flat ground `world_map`'s cap holds it). Only this client's drawing, until the world is joined again. A generated body can be paler than anything on its slider: the mark goes to the nearest, and the swatch shows it exactly until it is moved.
- World Time and Suns and Moons toggle their panels, and their entries have checkboxes saying whether they are open (`OpenPanels::toggle`, `forget_closed` for close buttons).
- World Time's panel (owner's design) asks the server to move the world's clock (`DevSetWorldClock`), on either map, for everyone in that world: small buttons -1d to -1m, a box, +1m to +1d, all one height and touching. The box shows `Day N HH:MM` (days from 1) and follows the clock unless it has the keyboard; giving the keyboard up by Return or clicking away sets that time (a bare `HH:MM` is on the day shown), Esc puts the real time back and sends nothing (the box takes the key, so the panel stays). What can't be read keeps an error edge until edited or a step is pressed. It stops at the world's beginning. A second row (`panels/world_rate.rs`) sets how fast the world's time runs, x1/4 to x600, the rate in use `Selected`.
- Change Creature (`panels/change_creature.rs`): a moveable panel with Permanent, Temporary and Illusion (always disabled: disguises aren't made yet) above a button per kind of creature (`DevSetCreature { kind, how }`, `how` from `ChosenChange`, Permanent at first; ids `debug.change_creature/permanent`, `temporary`, `illusion`). The kinds are disabled on the world map and in the Hall (`update_creature_buttons`), where the server refuses it as there is no ground.
- Delete Character (`delete_character.rs`): a dialog over the menu (layer 1, form `debug.delete_character`: `delete`, `cancel`), spawned hidden at start; Delete sends `DevDeleteCharacter`, after which the server asks for a new character and `character_create` shows its screen.
- Give Item (owner's design): a moveable panel reading like what it makes: "Give [- count +] [size] Size [Different/Identical]", then "Quality [quality] [blank/Set/Unique] [Unaligned/Blessed/Cursed] [Mundane/Magical] [kind, searchable]" in the order of the item's name, then "Seed [box]" with the new seed button, the item drawn as in a grid (`spawn_item_cells`, named on hover), and Give at the bottom right (`DevGiveItem`). `<target>` means the size of whoever gets it (owner, 2026-11-07): the target while it is in sight (`common::Target`, `common::Other`), else the character; the preview is made for them, "to the Wolf" or "to you" stands by Give, and Give sends `to`. With a target, room is the server's to judge (a mob takes anything given), so No space only shows for the character; Set and Unique both make a set piece for now; Different/Identical is greyed for one. The preview is rolled here with `shared::item_form::make_item` from the seed, as the server will roll it. The count is kept to `StackLimits::most_of` the item (identical) or `most` (different). Where there is no ground (the world map, the Hall), the panel tries them on a copy of `Carried` and, if they don't all fit, shows a red No space and greys Give. A Form line (owner, PR 2) has a dropdown per option of the kind's form (`item_form::fields`), "Any ..." first, listing what `item_form::choices` says each can be given the others (cached per kind, quality and request, as sets are slow); one no longer allowed goes back to Any, and the line hides for kinds without any. Blades' length is "at least N cells" (`Limits::at_least_long`), which may make another family's kind. The preview uses `try_make_item`: a request it can't meet for that seed (any of a Different batch) shows "No such ..." in place of No space and greys Give, as the server then gives nothing. The form starts afresh, with a random seed, whenever the panel opens; the seed is kept after giving, to make the same again.
- Item Pictures: a moveable panel with a dropdown of pages (`PicturePage`: Bodies, Hands, Shoulders, Arms, Torso, Waist, Legs, Feet, Footwear, Head, Neck, Sets, Weapons, Gems, Rings; the last chosen kept by name in `common::Preferences`, Gems if none or unknown), tabs for quality, and a scrolling grid that `fill_pictures` rebuilds with only that page's rows, so only those are drawn (the cut names head the Gems page only). The pages between them show a bare body of each kind on two legs at its usual height beside a person, in its first colours, then any animal drawn before it is a kind, then rows of hair styles and beards (bare and covered), a sample of gauntlets, demi-gauntlets and padded gloves as rolled (one row with a kobold's hands) and plate gauntlets in each style, a sample of each kind of shoulder piece as rolled and plate pauldrons in each style, a sample of weapons of each family, every gem in every cut and a sample of rings as the server would roll them (`item_art`, `give_form`); pressing one shows it large. It only draws here, so it needs no server. A new kind with models gets its own page.
- Truesight (`common::Truesight`, `DevTruesight`): the server sends items' secrets and secret doors, without them being found out; `remind_of_god_mode` tells a new connection too, `trade` asks again for what is open, and `ui` shows eyes. Later, anything else hidden should go by the same flag, on both sides. Give Item's tabs set `magical` and `favour`, as secret as on anything found.
- God Mode (`common::GodMode`, `DevGodMode`): the server has the rules (see `server`); `remind_of_god_mode` tells a new connection, `player` stops treating enemies as in the way, and `ui` shows a ghost. Further powers should go by the same flag, on both sides, rather than new entries.
- Kill Mob (`common::KillMob`, owner's design): only clicks change; movement doesn't, and it isn't saved. `entities` makes mobs clickable and sends `DevKillMob`, and `ui` shows a skull beside the ghost.
- Hurt, Spend Mana (5 each), Heal Fully and Vitals Bonus send `DevVitals`; the vitals circle shows the answer.
- Change Cell Mode, Change Creature, Create Mob, Delete Character, Delete Mob, Dev Area, Freeze Mobs, Inspect Mob, Regenerate Dev Area, Forget Map, Forget Map (Server), God Mode, Heal Fully, Hurt, Kill Mob, Reveal Map, Spend Mana, Teleport Mode, Teleport to Gate, Teleport to Hall of Worlds, Teleport to POI Showcase, Teleport to Start, Truesight and Vitals Bonus need the server's dev tools (`common::ServerDevTools`, from `Welcome`), and are disabled where it refuses them. The embedded server always allows them; a standalone one with `HOW_DEBUG_TOOLS=true`.
- Reveal Map and Forget Map are answered by the server sending the level again as if arriving where the player stands (`EnterLevel`), or for forgetting the world map `ForgetWorldMap` and the chunks in sight. Both are disabled in towns, which arrive whole. Forget Map on the world map also forgets what the character remembers of every site. Forget Map (Server) does the same and has the server forget the site or, on the world map, every site (`DevForgetMapOnServer`), so they are made anew with the current generators; it works in towns too.
- Generate POI... (owner's design): a second layer (75%) with the same grid and search, listing every kind alphabetically. Choosing one closes both layers and opens a moveable panel (`panels::GenerateKind`, `GenerateForm`): the kind as words, ruin 0 to 5 (grey for kinds that can't be), the seed box with a new seed button (a drawn round arrow, as the font has none), and Generate (`DevGenerateSite`). Claude's choices: the panel stays open to try more seeds, and choosing another kind replaces it; Esc, the close button or a click outside goes back to the first layer with its filter kept; backtick closes everything; a new seed has up to 9 digits, though any `u64` can be typed. Where columns don't fit, the grid shows a window of them, moved by the wheel, by following the arrow keys, or by the `<` and `>` buttons in the title row, which show only then.
- Dev Area (owner's design): a moveable panel with Size (16 × 16 to 128 × 128) and Generator (`DevLayout::all`: Empty, Moat, Cover, each biome, each kind of poi; searchable) dropdowns, Mobs (Default: whoever the generator would have; None; or any kind; searchable) with `- count +` beside it, live only for a kind (1 to `MOST_DEV_MOBS`), a seed box taking any words (`DevArea::seed_from_text`) with the new seed button, and Generate (`DevGenerateArea`), which goes straight in; its stairs lead back. `DevAreaForm` keeps the choices, and `kept_choices` keeps them between runs, so the panel opens as it was left (`start_dev_area_panel` sets the widgets from it, with a random seed if none).
- Create Mob (owner's design): a moveable panel with Kind (searchable), Attitude (Default, Friendly, Neutral, Unfriendly: how it stands with whoever made it), Gear (Rolled, None) dropdowns, a Still check button, a seed box with the new seed button, and Create, a toggle for `ClickMode::PlaceMob` (Mob Mode, which `ui` shows in red; only one click mode at a time): "Click to place" shows beside it while on, and `dungeon_map` then sends `DevCreateMob` for the cell clicked with `common::CreateMobOrder`, which the panel edits and keeps. Mob Mode goes off when the panel closes or anywhere but a site level (not the wilderness or the Hall), where Create is greyed.
- Inspect Mob, Delete Mob and Kill Mob (`common::InspectMob`, `DeleteMob`, `KillMob`): only one on at a time (`MobSwitches::toggle`); `entities/dev_clicks.rs` makes mobs clickable and sends `DevKillMob` or `DevMobs` Inspect / Delete; `ui` says which in red. Inspect's answer (`common::InspectedMob`) opens a moveable panel of lines (`debug.inspect_mob/panel`) that keeps asking while open. Freeze Mobs (`common::FreezeMobs`) sends `DevMobs(Freeze)`, for the whole world, and `ui` shows Mobs Frozen.
