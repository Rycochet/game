# client: system_menu

The system menu: Esc or the gear button top right (the way in on touch devices) opens a small panel in three sections: Continue, Online (native, with Server Options' gear) and Connect...; Settings and Credits; Logout and Quit. While it is open, single player is paused. The same menu, with Play for Continue, is the startup menu over the title, until Play is pressed (`common::Session`). Native and web.

## Files

- `crates/client/src/system_menu/mod.rs`: `SystemMenuPlugin`, `SystemMenu`, the gear icon (drawn in code), the panels, `take_input` (`PreUpdate`, `WidgetSystems::Front`, while `takes_input`: at the startup menu or once there is a world), `show_menu`, `show_buttons` (Play, Continue and Logout by `Session` and `ServerClock::local_server`), `ask_for_pause`, `show_ended_page`. Unit tests. Beside it: `state.rs` (`SystemMenu`, `Action`, `MenuButton`), `spawn.rs` (gear, layers, requester, Settings, `spawn_title_row`), `panel.rs` (the menu's sections, `SECTIONS`), `input.rs`, `show.rs`.
- `crates/client/src/system_menu/connect.rs`: Connect (startup menu only): example, address box (`AddressBox`), padlock (`PadlockButton`, native only), the remembered servers (`ServerList`, rows by `spawn_server_row` with a bin), Connect and Cancel. In `connect/`: `form.rs` (`ConnectForm`: a key pasted or cleared and the address it is for, the server being forgotten; `open_connect` fills the box with `ChosenServer` or the latest server, and after a refusal keeps the key tried; `fill_server_list`; `use_connect`, Connect's presses, which `SystemMenu::after` leaves to it), `show.rs` (`show_connect`: Connect and the padlock only for an address, the padlock shut while a key is kept, Keep only for a certificate), `popups.rs` (the certificate box and the forget requester), `check.rs` (`AddressCheck`, `check_address`: 0.6 s after typing stops, or at once when the box was filled another way (opening, picking a server), asks `connection::ServerProbe` for the ports `ServerAddress::ports_to_try` gives (the one typed, or 8081 then 443), again whenever the text or the key kept for it changes; `Checked::{Waiting, Checking, Found, Failed(reason)}`; a server of another protocol is a failure; the box's grey `suffix` is the port found when none was typed; `ready_for(text)` is what Connect connects to), `mark.rs` (`spawn_mark` inside the box's right end, which keeps room for it; `show_mark`: spinner while checking, tick (tooltip: the server's version) or cross (the reason)). Connect is only enabled while `ready_for` the box's text, and saves and joins the address with the port that answered, always written (owner). `icons.rs`: padlock shut and open, bin, tick, cross.
- `crates/client/src/system_menu/online.rs` (native): the Online row (`spawn_online_row`: the toggle, held down while on, with the gear attached), `use_online` (at once, or the Go online / Go offline requester while playing on the built-in server), `show_online`, `show_online_failure` (`Hosting` failed: Online off again, and the reason).
- `crates/client/src/system_menu/server_options.rs` (native): Server Options: name, public address, listen address, port, player limit (`OptionBox`: filled on opening, wrong ones marked, the right ones kept in `Preferences::online_settings` on closing, which reopens the server if online), and the certificate (the one in use, or `tls/certificate.pem` in the data folder) with a clipboard button beside its label (`widgets::spawn_copy_button`). Tests.
- `crates/client/src/system_menu/refusal.rs`: `show_refusal`: a refusal while playing goes to `Session::AtStart` and the "Can't connect" requester with the reason in red; its OK goes to Connect if a server was chosen there, else the menu.
- `crates/client/src/system_menu/credits.rs`: the Credits layer (`CREDITS`: what was done and by whom), a scroll area that `start_credits` restarts from the top with `widgets::AutoScroll` each time it opens. Test: every bold label on `docs/credits.md` is in it.
- `crates/client/src/system_menu/full_screen_key.rs` (native): Alt+Enter between windowed and `WindowPrefs::last_full_screen`, at once and saved; in `PreUpdate` before the widgets, following Alt by its own `KeyboardInput`s, as the open menu lets go of every held key (and forgetting it on `KeyboardFocusLost`). Not while a text box has the keyboard, or `KeepVideo` is up.
- `crates/client/src/system_menu/settings.rs`: Settings' tabs and pages (`spawn_pages`; hidden pages take no room): Gameplay (`settings/gameplay.rs`), Interface and Video (`settings/video.rs`). `settings/rows.rs`: what pages are made of: `spawn_sections(page, key, fill)` (one under another, each the page's whole width, scrolling in what is left of the page and keeping its place by `widgets::ScrollKey` when the page is rebuilt or Settings opened again; pages fill the rest of the panel), `spawn_section` (a heading and rows), `spawn_labelled` / `spawn_dropdown_row` (a label and a control with a `widgets::HoverTip` over the whole row), `tip(what, why_not)` (a setting's words, and why it is disabled). `settings/gameplay.rs`: "Pause When Not in Focus" (`Preferences::pause_unfocused`), disabled in a browser and while playing on another computer's server, with the reason in its tip. Interface has the interface size dropdown (Auto, then `widgets::INTERFACE_PERCENTS`) and "Enable touch" (on and disabled on a touch-only device, `widgets::TouchOnly`). `fill_interface_page` rebuilds it when `Preferences` change; `use_pages` switches pages and writes the choices into `Preferences`. Unit tests.
- `crates/client/src/system_menu/settings/video.rs`: the Video page: the Preset row, then sections: Window, Display, Lighting, World Map, Sites, Motion and Background (`video/sections.rs` the middle four; `video/options.rs` the rest and the rows' pieces, `dropdown`, `on_off`, `setting_row`, `slider_row`; `VideoControl` on each control; settings the browser decides are disabled with the reason in the tip). `video/choose.rs`: `use_video_options` puts choices into `Preferences::video` at once (a preset sets them all); `VideoShown` is what the page shows, so a change builds it again, except while a slider (Render Scale, Brightness) is held, which would drop it mid-drag (`stale` waits until it is let go). Window: Display mode and Resolution dropdowns ("Desktop (W × H)", then the monitor's from `common::resolutions`) and Apply at the right below them. `VideoForm` holds the choices until Apply; `fill_video_page` rebuilds it (dropping them) on opening Settings, when `DisplayInUse` changes and when the window is on another monitor; `use_video_page` enables Resolution only for Full screen and Apply only while the choices differ from what is in use (both always disabled on the web), and on Apply starts the trial and sets `DisplayInUse`. Tests. `settings/keep.rs`: the "Keep these settings?" requester (`KeepVideo`, above Settings) with its countdown; `VideoTrial` holds what to go back to; `run_trial` keeps it in `Preferences::window` on Keep (unless `RememberWindow` is off), and goes back on Go back, Esc, a press outside or after 15 s.

## Notes

- Ids (`widgets::UiId`): buttons by their `Action` (`Action::ui_id(form)` in `state.rs`: `menu/settings`, `quit/quit`, `connect/server.2`), big layers' close buttons `<form>/close` (`spawn_title_row`), Settings' tabs `settings/page`, Video's rows by `VideoControl` (`settings.video/frame_rate`), Server Options' boxes by `OptionBox`.

- Startup menu: while `Session::AtStart` the menu is always open, and Esc, outside presses and Continue do nothing; Play sets `Playing` and closes it. Logout (shown only while playing on a server that isn't built in: always on the web) sets `AtStart` and keeps the menu open, and `connection` hangs up. Credits are kept by hand beside `docs/credits.md` (the owner's name is only in the game's).
- Owner's design: no close button or title on the menu; "Paused" above the first button, taking no room while not paused (`Display::None`), so the menu is shorter in multiplayer. Quit asks "Are you sure?" in a small requester, whose "No" closes only that. Settings is a layer 50% of the window wide and 75% high, with tabs (owner's choice).
- Claude's choices: Esc or a press outside closes what is on top, one layer at a time (touch has no Esc, and the menu no close button). Settings closes back to the menu. The gear sits in the corner outside the round minimap, so a low sun or moon can pass behind it, and is hidden while connecting, as the warning button is.
- Esc: text boxes and dropdowns use it first (`Presses`). `take_input` runs before the debug menu and full map, leaving it to them if they are open. While open, the menu takes all keys, the wheel, gestures and right button, so backtick and M do nothing.
- Pausing: `ask_for_pause` sends `SetPaused` as the menu opens and closes, and as the window goes into the background and comes back with "Pause When Not in Focus" on, if the server said it can (`ServerClock::can_pause`). Only the server's answer stops anything (`connection`, see there), and "Paused" shows only then. In multiplayer nothing pauses: keys are blocked, but click travel carries on.
- Quit: `AppExit`, which `connection` hangs up on. In a browser `show_ended_page` also shows the `#ended` screen in `index.html`, a copy of the loading screen with a "Click to reload" button, over the stopped canvas. Its colours and sizes are shared with `#loading` there.
