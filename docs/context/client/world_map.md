# client: world_map

The world map: the surface as a lit 3D terrain mesh rendered by the GPU. A 400×400 slab of terrain around the player with flat earth faces on its edges (the prototype's composition, see the Legend entry in `decisions.md`) with modern lighting. Left-click travels to the clicked point; right-drag turns the view around the player, Home turns it back to north. Entities with a `common::Token` (including the player) are drawn as tokens.

## Files

- `crates/client/src/world_map/mod.rs`: `WorldMapPlugin`. `spawn_view` (Startup) spawns a `Camera3d` (tonemapping and debanding off), an east sun with shadows, ambient light, the terrain mesh and the edge faces, and inserts `HeightCache` and `TokenAssets`. `follow_player` (runs when `MapPosition`, clock, weather or style changes) moves the terrain to the player's grid point and rebuilds heights/edge faces; `token::resize_tokens` rebuilds the outline meshes in place when the window height changes; `token::sync_tokens` and `token::sync_icons` keep token views matching `Token`s and `Icon`s; `camera::turn_with_mouse` (in `PlayerSystems::Input`, before picking) turns the camera; `place_camera_and_tokens` (every frame) places the camera from its `OrbitCamera` around the player's `SmoothPosition`, writes `common::ViewFacing`, each token view at its owner's `SmoothPosition`, and turns icon anchors to face the camera; `pick_on_click` (in `PlayerSystems::Input`) sends `TravelTo`. Constants `HEIGHT_SCALE`, `HALF_SIZE` (200), `CAMERA_FOV_DEGREES` (20), `CAMERA_DISTANCE`, `CAMERA_PITCH_DEGREES`, `LOOK_AHEAD`, `SLOPE_SHADING`, `SUN_LUX`, `AMBIENT_BRIGHTNESS`.
- `crates/client/src/world_map/camera.rs`: `OrbitCamera` (component: `yaw`, `pitch`, `distance`, `look_ahead`; `transform(target)`, `quarter_turns()`), `turn_with_mouse` (right-drag turns every `OrbitCamera`, `RADIANS_PER_PIXEL` 0.005; Home resets yaw to 0). Nothing world-map specific, so other 3D views can reuse it. Unit tests.
- `crates/client/src/world_map/picking.rs`: `pick(ray, centre, ...) -> Option<(x, y)>`: walks the camera ray in 0.5-unit steps until it is below the drawn surface; misses if it passes over or comes in through an edge face. Works from any camera direction. Unit tests.
- `crates/client/src/world_map/token.rs`: how `Token`s are drawn. `TokenAssets` (resource: one body and one outline mesh per shape, the icon quad, one lit body material per attitude and one outline material, each in a normal and a completed version), `prism` (the shape extruded upwards: `Cylinder` for the circle, `Extrusion<RegularPolygon>` stood up for the others; `DIAMETER` 16, thickness 15%), `TokenView` (separate root entity per token: body, outline and `IconAnchor` children), `sync_tokens` (spawns views for new tokens, swaps mesh/material when a `Token` changes, despawns views whose owner or token is gone), `sync_icons` (puts the owner's `Icon` on the view's `IconAnchor` as an unlit, alpha-masked quad with nearest-pixel sampling; one material per icon file, cached in `TokenAssets`). Outline: the same prism grown by `OUTLINE_PIXELS` (2, halved by the owner) in grid units (from camera distance, FOV and window height; `outline_width`), `cull_mode: Front` (inverted hull, no custom shader); polygon corners grow by `outline / cos(π/sides)` so the rim is even.
- `crates/client/src/world_map/terrain.rs`: `HeightCache` (wrapping 512×512 grid of samples, so a step only samples the new edge), `new_terrain_mesh` (fixed 401×401 vertex grid centred on the entity), `update_terrain` (heights, smooth normals from neighbours, vertex colours with baked slope shading, all in place), `build_edges` (one unlit mesh with faces down all four edges, `EDGES`: water depth strip over earth down to `FRONT_BASE` × water level; each wound to face outwards, so back-face culling hides the far ones), `highest_shown_height` (max drawn height within a radius, for resting tokens).

## Public API

- `WorldMapPlugin`.
- `OrbitCamera` (component on the map camera): read or set `yaw` (radians; 0 looks north, positive moves the camera east). Used by `dev_tools` (`GAME_YAW`).

## Depends on

- `common` (`CurrentWorld`, `GameClock`, `ViewFacing`, `ViewStats`, `Weather`, `Token`, `Icon`), `player` (`MapPosition`, `SmoothPosition`, `TravelTo`, `PlayerSystems`), `surface_style`.

## Used by

- `dev_tools` (`OrbitCamera`).

## Notes

- World `x` → 3D `+X`, world `y` (south) → `+Z`. The terrain and edge faces sit at the player's grid point (`MapPosition`) and move a whole step at a time; the camera and tokens follow `SmoothPosition`, so travel looks smooth (the slab edges shift by under one unit relative to the camera).
- Camera: narrow field of view (20°) from far away (a "long lens"), so perspective is flat and the slab's sides stay near vertical. It looks at **sea level** (not the ground) `LOOK_AHEAD` units from the player towards the camera, so it doesn't bob over hills and the near face fits on screen. Only the yaw changes (owner's choice: rotate only, no tilt or zoom); the view stays where it is left. Turned diagonally, the slab is wider than the framing and its near corner goes slightly off the bottom of the screen.
- `ViewFacing` (common) is the camera's yaw rounded to a quarter turn; `player` uses it so arrow keys step towards the top of the screen. Written with `set_if_neq`. If the FOV changes, scale `CAMERA_DISTANCE` by `tan(old/2) / tan(new/2)` to keep the framing; `far` is set from the distance.
- Shading: real lighting alone looked too smooth, so the prototype's slope shading (height minus the western neighbour, very strong) is baked into the vertex colours, scaled by `SLOPE_SHADING`. On top, a harsh sun (from the east) with little ambient fill gives shadows and dark far sides. Both are fixed to the world, so they stay put when the view turns (looking south, the map is lit from the left and looks darker). Brightness was tuned for the default north view.
- Water is part of the terrain mesh, not a separate plane: points below the water are drawn at the surface (`SurfaceStyle::shown_height`) and coloured by depth, as the prototype did. This keeps shore sand (just below the water by definition) visible at the waterline. A translucent plane showed it tinted green, and lifting only the sand made spikes.
- Colours: `Tonemapping::None` on the camera; Bevy's default tonemapping made the palette pastel. With it off, sunlit faces can exceed full brightness and clip towards lime, so keep `SUN_LUX` low.
- Brightness was tuned to roughly match the prototype's renderer (mean luminance ~77, measured from `dev_tools` screenshots with Pillow). If colours or lighting change, re-measure.
- Frozen water vertex colours are multiplied by `ICE_BOOST` (1.41, above white) to cancel the sun's dimming of flat surfaces, so it shows at `#eee`. Re-measure if the lighting changes.
- Edge face bottom is `FRONT_BASE` (0.3) × the water level rather than 0 (shorter than the prototype's); where the ground is below that, the water strip stops at the base.
- Tokens rest on the highest drawn ground within their radius, so they never sink into slopes (they float a little over the low side). The outline stays 2 screen pixels wide across window resizes (`resize_tokens`); icons scale with the map instead.
- Token placement reads the owner's `player::SmoothPosition`. Only the player has a token so far; when other entities get one, their position component should move to `common` (or `shared`).
- Token views never rotate (shape turns are baked into the meshes), so an `IconAnchor`'s rotation copied from the camera makes it face the screen. The icon is a quad child of the anchor, so it always shows the same face whatever the camera does. Icons are sized relative to the token, not the screen (owner's choice, for a blockier retro look): the central 32×32 picture is `ICON_ART_SIZE` (1.5 × the token's diameter) wide, so art pixels aren't whole screen pixels; the anchor sits so the bottom of the central 32×32 picture, less `ICON_SHADOW_PIXELS` (3 art pixels: most pictures end in a shadow that far up), is `ICON_GAP_PIXELS` (1 art pixel) above the token, so characters stand on it (dividing by the pitch's cosine, since heights look shortened). Completed tokens don't dim their icon (yet). The current art (Gervais tiles) is planned to be replaced by commissioned artwork: retune `ICON_FILE_PIXELS`, `ICON_ART_PIXELS`, `ICON_ART_SIZE`, `ICON_GAP_PIXELS` and `ICON_SHADOW_PIXELS` to match it. Polygons have a corner pointing north, then turn: the square to line up with the grid, the hexagon (30°) and octagon (22.5°) to put a flat side towards the camera in the default north view (turning the view turns that side away).
- Completed tokens (`fade`): sRGB colour × `COMPLETED_SHADE` (0.7) and alpha `COMPLETED_OPACITY` (0.9, chosen by the owner) with `AlphaMode::AlphaToCoverage` (needs MSAA, Bevy's default), for body and outline alike. Ordinary blending showed the outline's inner faces (directly behind the body) through the token, turning it black; alpha to coverage still writes depth, so the body hides them. Coverage comes in coarse steps (measured: 0.85 and 0.9 look identical), so small opacity changes may do nothing, and it looks slightly dotted up close.
- Performance (native debug): first fill ~56 ms (161k samples); each grid step ~12 ms (the prototype's CPU renderer took ~38 ms). The vertex loops call `HeightCache::get` several times per vertex; a flat height array per step would be the first optimisation if needed.
- Terrain and edge faces use `NoFrustumCulling` because their heights change after the bounds are computed. `HeightCache` is inserted by commands at startup, so systems take it as `Option` for the first frame.
