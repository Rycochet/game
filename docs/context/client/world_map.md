# client: world_map

The world map: the surface as a lit 3D terrain mesh rendered by the GPU, with its features (caves, houses, castles...) as token entities. A 400×400 square window of terrain around the player with flat earth faces on its edges (the prototype's composition, see the Legend entry in `decisions.md`) with modern lighting. Left-click travels to the clicked point; clicking a feature's token travels to it and enters its site (`site`). The camera (right-drag turns and tilts, wheel zooms, Home resets) and the tokens come from `view3d`. The window stays lined up with the camera and keeps its size on screen: turning turns the world inside it, zooming shows less of the world in it. Shown while `GameView::WorldMap` is current.

## Files

- `crates/client/src/world_map/mod.rs`: `WorldMapPlugin`. `spawn_view` (Startup) spawns the east sun with shadows and the terrain and edge-face meshes (all marked `WorldMapScene`, hidden unless this is the current view) and inserts `HeightCache`; `TerrainGrid` is inited by the plugin. `show_scene` / `hide_scene` on entering/leaving the view (also sets the ambient light and `view3d::ViewGrid`: 1 unit per point, floor at sea level). Only while current: `update_terrain` (runs when `MapPosition`, clock, weather, style or the camera's yaw or zoom changes; changes made while another view was current are still noticed) moves the terrain to the player's grid point, fills the grid and cuts the window and edge faces for `view_square(orbit)`; `rest_tokens_on_terrain` (after `View3dSystems::Place`) lifts token views onto the highest ground under them; `hide_tokens_outside_window` (same time) hides token views not wholly inside the window (centre within `half` less the token radius) and `hide_scene` shows them all again; `pick_on_click` (in `PlayerSystems::Input` after `View3dSystems::Click`, sends `TravelTo` unless a token was clicked). Constants `HEIGHT_SCALE`, `HALF_SIZE` (200), `SLOPE_SHADING`, `SUN_LUX`, `AMBIENT_BRIGHTNESS`. `view_square` (the window for the camera: its ground axes, half-size `HALF_SIZE × zoom`).
- `crates/client/src/world_map/features.rs`: (towns show `l2_town` pictures) features as entities (`common::Portal::Feature(feature)` and `Clickable`, `MapPosition`, `SmoothPosition`, white octagon `Token` with `TokenSize::Tiny` icons (the pictures fill the whole tile), `Icon` `terrain/l2_terrainNNN.png` with NNN = 66 + `FeatureKind` index). `spawn_nearby` (after `PlayerSystems::Travel`, before `View3dSystems::Place`, while current: when `MapPosition` or `CurrentWorld` (new chunks from the server) changes, or the view was just entered) keeps entities for exactly the known features (`CurrentWorld::features`) within `RANGE` (the turned window's corners plus a token radius) of the player, in `SpawnedFeatures` (by position); `despawn_all` on leaving the view. Unit test.
- `crates/client/src/world_map/picking.rs`: `pick(ray, centre, square, ...) -> Option<(x, y)>`: walks the camera ray in 0.5-unit steps until it is below the drawn surface; misses if it passes over or comes in through an edge face. Allows for raised woods through its `lift` argument. Inside/outside uses the current `ViewSquare`. Unit tests.
- `crates/client/src/world_map/terrain.rs`: `HeightCache` (wrapping 640×640 grid of surface samples). `ViewSquare` (the window on show: `right`, `towards`, `half`; `contains`). `TerrainGrid` (resource: wrapping 640×640 store of per-point drawn height, ground height, normal, colour with baked slope shading and vegetation cover, keyed by world position; `fill` works out only points the window's bounding box needs that aren't kept yet, `redraw_all` clears it). `build_window` (the terrain mesh: whole triangles inside the turned square are copied, ones crossing its border are clipped by `clip` (Sutherland–Hodgman), so the outline is exact). `build_edges` (one unlit mesh with faces down all four window edges: water depth strip over earth down to `FRONT_BASE` × water level in the starting view, the depth below the water scaled by the zoom so the frame keeps its shape; columns at every grid line and triangle diagonal the edge crosses (`crossings`), heights from the triangles (`height_at`), so the face top meets the terrain exactly; wound to face outwards, so back-face culling hides the far ones). `highest_shown_height` (max drawn height within a radius, for resting tokens). Unit tests.

- `crates/client/src/world_map/rivers.rs`: `KnownRivers` (resource: chunks around the player and their rivers, traced once and kept until well out of reach; `open_at`: how much woods grow at a point, a wrapping store rebuilt on entering a chunk, cleared `CLEARED` to `CLEARED_TO` units round each river), `DrawnRiver` (smoothed course, temperature, and width per point: 1 px at the source plus 1 px per chunk run, up to 4 px; pixels at the starting zoom, so they scale with the map). `terrain::build_rivers` makes the meshes: the strips, and dark see-through banks either side (`BANK_WIDTH`, `BANK_ALPHA`) so they look set into the ground.
- `crates/client/src/world_map/sky.rs`: `spawn`, `update` (every frame: the time and window for the shaders; once a second, or at once after a teleport, samples `shared::weather` onto a 100�100 texture with 10-point spacing, about 1.8 ms: red cover, green rain, blue temperature. Two such textures, a second apart, are faded between, so the sky changes smoothly), `TerrainMaterial` (`ExtendedMaterial<StandardMaterial, TerrainSky>`), `CloudMaterial`, `PrecipitationMaterial`, `SkyAssets`, `SkyHourOffset`.
- `crates/client/assets/shaders/`: `terrain.wesl` (standard lighting plus cloud shadows, water ripples and painted vegetation), `clouds.wesl` (the sheet), `precipitation.wesl`. `struct Sky` in each must match `SkyParams` in `sky.rs`.

## Notes

- Sky (owner's design): clouds are one sheet at `CLOUD_HEIGHT` (170, may change), straight above the window and exactly its shape, drawn in chunky blocks (`BLOCK` in `clouds.wesl`, about 8 screen pixels), so they end in a hard edge and don't move relative to the map when the camera turns. Being high and nearer the camera, the sheet shows further up the screen and wider than the map. Clicks pass through. Under them the terrain is slightly darkened straight below (`SHADOW`), not along the sun's direction, and not real shadows. Water is marked by vertex alpha 0 (frozen water isn't), and flowing rivers count as water.
- Vegetation is painted, not modelled: `shared::vegetation` per grid point reaches the fragment shader in the terrain mesh's UV slots (`ATTRIBUTE_UV_0` trees and low growth, zero except on `SurfaceKind::Land`; `ATTRIBUTE_UV_1` how narrow and how lush), so clipping and interpolation carry it with no vertex shader of our own. `terrain.wesl` draws crowns on a jittered grid (`CROWN`, conical where narrow, thin woods losing crowns one at a time) and grass, changing the existing colour, so biome blends and hue rotation carry through, and tilting `pbr_input.N` for the bumps. Detail fades by `fwidth` before it reaches pixel size. Woods are also really raised: `terrain::canopy_lift` adds to each point's drawn height (`CANOPY_HEIGHT` 2.5, plus `CANOPY_LUSH` 1.5 in rainforest), fading to nothing towards the shore (`CANOPY_SHORE`, or the shore stretches up a wall) and round rivers (`KnownRivers::open_at`, which also thins the trees), and counting only a little in the baked slope shading (`CANOPY_SHADING`, or woods' edges go white). Normals, edge faces, rivers, token resting and click picking all follow the raised surface; `ground` stays the real ground, which is also how edge faces know land from water. Rivers sit in a fold, so low camera angles can hide stretches of them. Rivers use the same material without UVs (`@if(VERTEX_UVS_A && VERTEX_UVS_B)`). Woods are darker than open land, which lowers the map's mean brightness.
- Precipitation is `DROPS` quads placed by the vertex shader from random numbers in their positions, each at a fixed place on the map (a pattern repeating every `DROP_TILE`, so walking doesn't move them): how many show follows rain; temperature picks snow (below 0.2), sleet (below 0.3), otherwise rain, or hail where rain is above 0.8. Time comes from `common::ServerClock`, so clients agree to within their latency.

- World `x` → 3D `+X`, world `y` (south) → `+Z`. The terrain and edge faces sit at the player's grid point (`MapPosition`) and move a whole step at a time; the camera and tokens follow `SmoothPosition`, so travel looks smooth (the window edges shift by under one unit relative to the camera).
- Turning and zooming (owner's choice): the window is a square lined up with the camera, cut out of the world-aligned terrain, rather than the slab itself turning on screen. Zooming moves the camera in and shrinks the square by the same factor, so the square keeps its size on screen and shows less of the world (a true zoom: hills look taller). Each turn or zoom step re-cuts the window. Grid triangles are split along the north-east/south-west diagonal; the edge faces and `TerrainGrid::height_at` follow that same split.
- Shading: real lighting alone looked too smooth, so the prototype's slope shading (height minus the western neighbour, very strong) is baked into the vertex colours, scaled by `SLOPE_SHADING`. On top, a harsh sun (from the east) with little ambient fill gives shadows and dark far sides. Both are fixed to the world, so they stay put when the view turns (looking south, the map is lit from the left and looks darker). Brightness was tuned for the starting view.
- Water is part of the terrain mesh, not a separate plane: points below the water are drawn at the surface (`SurfaceStyle::shown_height`) and coloured by depth, as the prototype did. This keeps the shore (just below the water by definition) visible at the waterline. A translucent plane showed it tinted green, and lifting only the shore made spikes.
- Colours: `Tonemapping::None` on the camera; Bevy's default tonemapping made the palette pastel. With it off, sunlit faces can exceed full brightness and clip towards lime, so keep `SUN_LUX` low.
- Brightness was tuned to roughly match the prototype's renderer (mean luminance ~77, measured from `dev_tools` screenshots with Pillow). If colours or lighting change, re-measure.
- Frozen water vertex colours are multiplied by `ICE_BOOST` (1.41, above white) to cancel the sun's dimming of flat surfaces, so it shows at `#eee`. Re-measure if the lighting changes.
- Edge face bottom is `FRONT_BASE` (0.3) × the water level in the starting view rather than 0 (shorter than the prototype's), closer to the water when zoomed in; where the ground is below that, the water strip stops at the base.
- Camera (see `view3d.md`): looks at **sea level** (`ViewGrid::floor`), so it doesn't bob over hills. Tokens rest on the highest drawn ground within their radius (`rest_tokens_on_terrain`), so they never sink into slopes (they float a little over the low side).
- Token placement reads the owner's `common::SmoothPosition`. Features only exist as entities while the world map is current (other views draw every token), so the dungeon view never sees them.
- Features are only on dry land (decided in `shared::feature`). Their `Portal` carries the `SeenFeature` (kind, chunk, position: never the map seed); `site` asks the server to enter its chunk.
- The sun, terrain and edge faces are hidden (not despawned) in other views, so the terrain grid and cut are kept for coming back.
- Performance (native dev build, shown in the HUD): a step fills ~1 ms (only the new edge) and cuts the window in ~9–12 ms in the starting view (more at diagonal angles, less zoomed in: ~4 ms at 0.6); turning costs the same cut every frame while dragging. A full refill (new hour, weather or style, e.g. every frame of the temperature demo) is ~15 ms facing north and ~28 ms at 45°, plus the cut. The very first fill is ~100 ms (sampling everything). Measured before this change: ~12 ms per step, full update in place.
- Vegetation added about 0.4 ms to the cut while walking (13.4 to 13.8 ms, single readings); its cost on the GPU has not been measured.
- Terrain and edge faces use `NoFrustumCulling` because their meshes are replaced after the bounds are computed. `HeightCache` is inserted by commands at startup, so systems take it as `Option` for the first frame.
- Ctrl+click on the map (not a token) sends `DevTeleport` instead of `TravelTo` (dev tools: only the embedded server allows it; through walls in a site, but onto a walkable cell). Uses `connection::ToServer`.
