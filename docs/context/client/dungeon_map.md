# client: dungeon_map

The dungeon (and building) view: `shared::dungeon` cells drawn in 3D through `view3d`'s camera, with the same tokens as the world map. Shown while `GameView::Dungeon` is current (inside a site, `CurrentDungeon`). Draws what the player, or anything with `SharesSight`, can see (or glimpse), including the level's stairs as 3D steps and thin 3D walls, doors and windows, and what they remember (seen before, out of sight now) dim, grey and half see-through; hides tokens on cells nobody sees; lights it from `LightSource`s. Only cells the server has sent are drawn. Walls and floors use AngbandTk tile pictures chosen by the kind of place. Left-click a seen or remembered walkable cell to travel there.

## Files

- `crates/client/src/dungeon_map/mod.rs`: `DungeonMapPlugin`. `spawn_scene` (Startup: a `DungeonScene` root with one solid mesh per `Surface` (`SolidPart(surface)`), one remembered mesh per `Surface` (`MemoryPart(surface)`) and one see-through wall mesh, unlit, each with its own tile material (nearest sampling, `load_tile`), kept in `SurfaceMaterials`). `apply_theme` (when `CurrentDungeon` changes: wall and floor pictures from `Theme::of(LevelInfo::kind)`, queuing grey copies). `grey_pictures` (every frame while any are queued: once a picture has loaded, a grey copy (`greyed`, Rec. 601 weights) for the remembered material; copies are cached by picture). `pick_on_click` (in `PlayerSystems::Input` after `View3dSystems::Click`, unless a token was clicked: sends `TravelTo` for a remembered (including seen) walkable cell, using `CurrentDungeon::remembered` and the private `Shown` resource: last combined sight, solid wall sides, cells with stairs up). `show_scene` / `hide_scene` on entering/leaving the view (also sets `ViewGrid` to `CELL_SIZE` with the floor at 0, and the dim ambient light for tokens; leaving shows all tokens again). `update_meshes` (when a viewer or light moves, a `LightSource` or the level changes, or the camera's position changes (turning, zooming, following the player): one `Sight` per viewer plus the combined one, from `add_glimpses` (open cells partly in view count, so side openings off a corridor show), worked out on the known level (unknown cells are wall); seen known cells are added to `CurrentDungeon::remembered` (bypassing change detection); light, rebuild, store `Shown`; writes `ViewStats`, starting with `CurrentDungeon::describe()`; empty meshes are hidden, not replaced; sight is worked out again only when a viewer or the level changes, not when only the camera moves). `hide_unseen_tokens` (after `update_meshes`). `sync_lights` (one Bevy `PointLight` per `LightSource`, to light tokens). Constants `CELL_SIZE` (1.5 × token diameter = 24, about the token's picture), `WALL_HEIGHT`, `AMBIENT_LIGHT` (0.1), `SEE_THROUGH_ALPHA` (0.5), `MEMORY_ALPHA` (0.5), token light constants.
- `crates/client/src/dungeon_map/mesh.rs`: `Surface` (`Floor`, `Wall`, `Door` (frames of doors and windows in buildings), `Ground`, `Water`, `Lava`, `WallBlend` (the wall's picture fading across the floor, on a blended material): one mesh and picture each). `LightGrid` (light per cell: `add_light` uses `shared::dungeon::Sight` with the radius, so walls block it; strength `1 − (d / (radius + 1))²`; corner light = average over the open cells round a grid corner). `View` (a viewer's cell and its own `Sight`), `WallFace` (a piece of solid wall for picking, with its size). `build(level, views, sight, remembered, light, stairs, camera, shape)` (only remembered cells: flat open cells and stairs here, in `build_open_cell`; walls, doors and windows through `walls`; returns the solid faces for picking). `build_stairs` (given the cell's colour and the floor's; `STAIR_STEPS` 4, half a cell wide (`STAIRS_HALF_WIDTH`) down the middle of the cell, running `SeenStairs::descends`: stairs up are a block of steps in the wall's masonry on the whole floor, rising to `STAIRS_UP_HEIGHT` (0.75 of a wall); stairs down are a hole `STAIRS_DOWN_DEPTH` (half a cell) deep with floor either side, floor-picture steps and brick sides; shaded by hand since unlit: risers 0.6, sides 0.8, the hole from 0.6 at the rim to 0.05 at the bottom). `faces_camera(centre, normal, camera)` (the camera's position is in front of the side's plane). `MEMORY_SHADE` (0.3). `Builder` (collects quads; `free` takes any four corners). Unit tests.
- `crates/client/src/dungeon_map/walls.rs`: `build_wall` draws a wall cell in four quarters, each with its piece of floor (like the nearest open cell, stopping at the wall's foot: `Patch`, `wall_foot`; in `Natural` places `fade_wall` fades the wall's picture across it, `BLEND_WIDTH` 0.25 of a cell), up to two faces (`face_at`: where they stand, from `WallShape::reach`) and its top. A quarter shows if an open cell round that corner of the cell is seen or remembered, so the far side of a wall and the inside of solid rock stay dark; a face if a viewer also stands in front of it. `build_opening`: a door or window, floor plus a wall with a hole (`Opening`: its height, width, arch). Natural windows are `build_pillars` (5 columns, or stalagmites under stalactites, by a hash of the position). `half_thickness`: straight, or curved like a C (narrowest half way up at the shared thickness, `NATURAL_WALL_HALF` 0.2 of a cell at top and bottom); natural faces are cut up and their inner points wander (`ROUGHNESS`, also shading). `Layers`, `Layer` (`Solid`, `SeeThrough`, `Memory`), `Scene`.
- `crates/client/src/dungeon_map/picking.rs`: `pick(ray, level, sight, solid_faces, stairs_up)`: the ray to the floor (height 0), or first into the box of a seen staircase up, gives a cell, if it is walkable and seen and no solid wall piece is hit first (see-through ones don't block). A click on a wall cell's floor, beside the wall, means the walkable cell on that side. `floor_cell(ray)`: the cell under the ray, whatever it is (dev tools). Unit tests.
- `crates/client/src/dungeon_map/theme.rs`: `Theme { wall, floor, shape }` and `Theme::of(Option<FeatureKind>)`: stone (castles, forts, stone towers and cottages, warehouses, pyramids...; also the test room), wood (huts, tents, cottages, wooden towers), crypt (crypts, graves, mausoleums, ruins, cellars under barrels), cave (natural caves, boulders), ice (ice cave, ice shelter). `Shape`: `Built` (stone, wood: straight walls, square doors, framed windows), `Natural` (crypt, cave, ice: curved walls, arched doors, windows of columns). `picture(surface)`: doors (wood), outside ground (grass), water and lava are the same in every theme. Test: every named picture exists.

## Notes

- Grid cell `(x, y)` is centred on `(x × CELL_SIZE, 0, y × CELL_SIZE)`; walls are `WALL_HEIGHT` tall, thin, down the middle of their cells (their rules are in `shared::dungeon`), but only their seen sides are drawn, with their tops in the quarters shown.
- Both sides of a wall look the same (owner's): if any side someone sees is turned away from the camera, every seen side of that stretch of wall (each way it runs, at a corner) is see-through, and a viewer in line with a wall, side-on to it, sees both its sides. So side-on walls are see-through whichever way the camera is.
- Wall rule (owner's): only sides someone sees are drawn. A seen side facing the camera is solid; one facing away (the wall stands between the camera and what is seen) is drawn 50% see-through, from behind (the see-through material doesn't cull back faces). Facing is measured from the camera's position, not its direction, so as the camera turns a side flips only when the camera passes its plane; this is the same test as back-face culling, so solid sides are always front faces. Out of sight: remembered sides facing the camera are drawn grey (see-through ones are left out, so memory stays quiet); never-seen ones not at all. A thick wall (two rows) or solid rock shows black between its faces: those quarters can never be seen.
- A quarter can show because of an open cell round the wall's end or a column, with a face in it out of sight (the viewer is on the other side). If it faces the camera it is drawn as memory, even if never seen (the wall is known, and it gives nothing away; a hole looks wrong), and so are that quarter's floor and top if it has no face in sight.
- Nothing is drawn on the floor under a wall's foot, so see-through and remembered walls show a dark band there (wider on natural walls, whose foot is wider). Doors stop the floor at their sides; natural windows' floor runs on under the columns. The fade reaches at most `BLEND_WIDTH` so it is gone by the edge of a cell where a wall ends, and matches the next wall cell where it carries on; it stays inside wall, door and window cells, and doesn't cross into open cells.
- Light is worked out on the grid and baked into vertex colours (unlit materials): `picture × (ambient + (1 − ambient) × light)`, so full light shows the picture as drawn. Floors take corner-averaged light; walls blend between their cell's corners, except across the wall itself, so each side of it is lit by its own side. Light colours add up (clamped), so coloured lights work already; all are white for now. Stairs take their cell's light, times their shading (applied after the ambient, so the hole can be really dark).
- Each picture covers one cell (32 pixels per cell); parts smaller than a cell (steps) show part of it, so the texture keeps its scale.
- Remembered surfaces use grey copies of the pictures, made on the CPU once each picture loads (until then they show in colour), at an even `MEMORY_SHADE` rather than their light, half see-through (real blending, with floors' `depth_bias` keeping them behind walls; floors show through walls). `MEMORY_SHADE` is kept low so memory isn't brighter than cells in sight in the dull light.
- Pictures are clamped, not repeated: every piece keeps its picture coordinates within its cell, using its position there, so it lines up with the next cell.
- Natural shape is chosen by the theme for now; the generators may choose it per tile later, with more variations (door tops, window kinds). Material relief is only the wandering surface and shading: the materials are unlit, so no normal maps.
- Stairs are drawn from `CurrentDungeon::stairs`, not as tokens, running the way the server worked out (the same on both levels). The hole's near sides face away from the camera and are culled, so the steps show; at the starting tilt only the far part of the hole is visible.
- Tokens can't use baked light, so they get real lights: a `PointLight` (no shadows) `TOKEN_LIGHT_HEIGHT` above each light source, range (radius + 1) cells, plus a dim ambient. Tuned by eye. The player arriving by stairs stands on them (the token isn't raised or lowered).
- Sight and light use `MapPosition` (whole cells). Drawing (and hiding tokens, and click picking) uses glimpses, which are more generous than the game's symmetric sight; light keeps the symmetric rule.
- The see-through mesh is one mesh with `AlphaMode::Blend`, so its faces don't sort against each other; with one picture at 50% this hasn't shown.
- Clicks: travel uses the world map's straight-line rules (through walls) until pathfinding (planned as a separate task); `player` travels at 12 cells/s here.
- Rebuilding everything (with sight) is about 1 ms for a 50 × 66 castle floor; it happens every frame while the camera moves (dragging, travelling). Bigger levels may need to rebuild only what changed.
- Ctrl+click on the map (not a token) sends `DevTeleport` instead of `TravelTo` (dev tools: only the embedded server allows it; through walls in a site, but onto a walkable cell). Alt+click sends `DevSetCell` (floor → wall → door → window → floor), to try wall shapes. Uses `connection::ToServer`.
