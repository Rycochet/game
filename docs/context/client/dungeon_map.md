# client: dungeon_map

The dungeon (and building) view: `shared::dungeon` cells drawn in 3D through `view3d`'s camera, with the same tokens as the world map. Shown while `GameView::Dungeon` is current (inside a site, `CurrentDungeon`). Draws what the player, or anything with `SharesSight`, can see (or glimpse), including the level's stairs as 3D models and thin 3D walls, doors and windows, and what they remember (seen before, out of sight now) dim, grey and half see-through; hides tokens on cells nobody sees; lights it from `LightSource`s. Only cells the server has sent are drawn. Walls and floors use AngbandTk tile pictures chosen by the kind of place. Left-click a seen or remembered walkable cell to travel there.

## Notes

- Edge of sight: every surface is cut by the mask, so the edge falls part way across a cell or along a wall; what is lit shows inside it and what is remembered outside. Crops, herbs, see-through walls and stairs have no remembered copy, so they simply stop there. Upright surfaces read the mask a tenth of a cell in front of them (`IN_FRONT` in the shader), as a wall's side stands right on the edge. Tops raised above their own ground (of walls and roofs, `RAISED`, measured from the vertex's ground height so raised wilderness cells aren't tops) aren't cut, and go by the whole cell as they always did: sight stops at the wall below them, so they would always read as out of sight. The lines follow `SmoothPosition`, so shadows sweep while walking. Game rules, tokens, rain, crops and what the server sends stay on the grid: a cell whose centre the game sees is always inside the outline. Cells only the lines reach count as drawn and remembered, never as seen for tokens. Lit pieces are copied grey rather than built again as memory, so a side already remembered isn't drawn twice. Remembered liquid in those cells keeps its bed and sides, unlike other memory, only at the edge of sight. Memory is per cell, so the unseen part of a cell seen in part shows grey. Cells the server hasn't sent are wall here, so the lines can't reveal them.
- Towns (owner's rules): the outside light all over (`LightGrid::filled`, and brighter ambient on tokens) and the player's lantern, so dark at night until positional lights come with decorations; every cell is known, so out of sight is grey, not missing. Memory of `Surface::Town` is opaque rather than half see-through: a whole town would otherwise have to sort. Walls are `WALL_OUT` from cell centres, and the half cell beyond them is always the land's ground (even beside a road or garden), so it shows between buildings too. Roofs slope up across the outermost cells only, fading to black, flat black inside. Doors fill their wall cell with the kind's picture (a house's is plain); windows are black panes with nothing behind, until their lights come on. Clicking a door (`picking::door_clicked`, `DoorClicked`, for `shop`) travels to the cell it faces, and the HUD names the building from there. Hedges: 20% of a cell thick, tall and inset; they block nothing; arches are 1.5 cells tall. A stretch of 3 or more has an arch half the time (by a hash of its first cell, so all clients agree), never at its ends; a door facing a hedge always gets one and the stretch no other.
- `Cell::CoveredGround` uses `Surface::Ground`, so the land's colour, with no rain and only the light spilling under it, so the shade under stilt huts shows. In a town, the whole level has the outside light, so it would still be lit.
- Herb beds are drawn as plain outside ground, the land's own, under the plants. Nothing of the plants shows from memory. A herbalist's hut is lit from outside like a farm.
- Farms (owner's rules): outside cells have the outside light, with what spills into the house, and lanterns as usual; only what is in sight is drawn of crops, never memory, and ruined fields have none. Crops are upright pictures in a `#`, two rows each way per cell, 6, 12 or 18 pixels tall; a stage is looks only, chosen here, so the server never hears of it. Time of year is to decide it later. The debug menu's Fields State panel moves all fields a stage; `HOW_DEBUG_FIELDS` sets one.
- Outside (`Cell::Ground`, owner's rule): it is the world outside, so it shares the land and weather of the site's place on the world map (`WorldReturn`). The ground is one colour over the whole site, and precipitation falls only on outside cells in sight, at the world map's density and with its rules for rain, sleet, snow and hail. Clouds aren't shown here.
- Grid cell `(x, y)` is centred on `(x × CELL_SIZE, 0, y × CELL_SIZE)`; walls are `WALL_HEIGHT` tall, thin, down the middle of their cells (their rules are in `shared::dungeon`), but only their seen sides are drawn, with their tops in the quarters shown.
- Both sides of a wall look the same (owner's): if any side someone sees is turned away from the camera, every seen side of that stretch of wall (each way it runs, at a corner) is in the way, and a viewer in line with a wall, side-on to it, sees both its sides. So side-on walls are in the way whichever way the camera is.
- Wall rule (owner's): only sides someone sees are drawn. A seen side facing the camera is solid; one facing away (the wall stands between the camera and what is seen) is in the way: built into the see-through layer, then (`mesh/cutaway.rs`, owner 2026-11-09) moved into the solid walls turned to face the camera, and the whole cell cut down (`Builder::cut_down`, which leaves a flat top) to `STUB_HEIGHT` (a quarter of a wall) within `CUT_NEAR` (4 cells) of the player, rising, eased, to full height at `CUT_FAR` (6). Beyond that it stands whole, and what is behind shows only through the x-ray (`view3d`). Facing is measured from the camera's position, not its direction, so as the camera turns a side flips only when the camera passes its plane; this is the same test as back-face culling, so solid sides are always front faces. Out of sight: a remembered wall's sides facing the camera are drawn grey, even one never seen, where an open cell is known beyond it (owner's: otherwise a wall seen from one side vanishes with the camera behind it); see-through ones are left out, so memory stays quiet. The open cell beyond stays undrawn until seen. A thick wall (two rows) or solid rock shows black between its faces: those quarters can never be seen.
- A quarter can show because of an open cell round the wall's end or a column, with a face in it out of sight (the viewer is on the other side). If it faces the camera it is drawn as memory, even if never seen (the wall is known, and it gives nothing away; a hole looks wrong), and so is that quarter's top if it has no face in sight. Its floor is always lit: the mask cuts floors, so memory there would leave a black hole inside the edge of sight.
- Whatever the mask cuts must be lit inside the edge, as memory shows only outside it. So each viewer's `View::sight` includes the cells its own outline reaches (`SightLines::touched_by`), though the game's sight doesn't count them; otherwise a wall seen at a narrow angle would be memory and invisible. The edge can also run along the floor of a wall's cell without reaching the open cell beside it, so any quarter of a wall in sight with a known open cell round it counts as in sight (`quarter_shows`), without those open cells being seen or remembered.
- A `Cell::SecretDoor` only reaches a client that has found it (or has truesight): closed, it is drawn as wall, with only a black line (`CRACK_WIDTH` 1, wider than the mortar's) round where a door would be, on the faces towards the camera, and the same lines in the x-ray (owner, 2026-11-09). Open, it is an opening like a door's (`mesh/cells.rs` and `mesh/build.rs` go by `Level::is_wall` / `is_opening`), its leaf swung half as far.
- Doors (owner, 2026-11-09): every `Cell::Door` (and open secret door) has a leaf (1 thick, 0.3 clear of the hole) drawn as the door hangs (`Level::swing`, `walls/leaf_pose.rs` `LeafPose::of`), or part way there while it swings: shut in the hole, open 90° (`LEAF_OPEN`, a secret door 45°) flat to the wall on its `Side`, or broken: open and hanging askew from its top hinge (`BROKEN_TILT`). The hinge's side is from a hash of the cell (only looks); a door whose swing isn't known is open the hash's way. Black outline. Swinging (`door_swing.rs`: `DoorSwings`, `swing_doors` before `update_meshes`): when a door's state changes its leaf eases from where it was drawn to its new pose over `SWING_SECONDS` (0.35), turning through shut from one side to the other (`LeafPose::between`); the resource is marked changed only while one moves, so `update_meshes` rebuilds each frame then and not otherwise (`mesh::build_posed` takes the poses; `mesh::build`, without them, is for tests). A door first seen, a new level (or a moved wilderness window) and everything with Reduce Motion are drawn in place at once. Through what hides it, the leaf shows as a faint white fill and outline, and the hole as white lines round it on the wall's faces, so its shape says whether it is open. Town buildings' doors aren't real doors and get none.
- Nothing is drawn on the floor under a wall's foot, so see-through and remembered walls show a dark band there (wider on natural walls, whose foot is wider). Under a solid wall the footprint of the quarters in sight is filled (`fill_foot`), in the wall's picture at its top's shade: the curve lets the camera see in under the wall from the side, and it would otherwise show the background as a thin black slit. Doors stop the floor at their sides; natural windows' floor runs on under the columns. The fade reaches at most `BLEND_WIDTH` so it is gone by the edge of a cell where a wall ends, and matches the next wall cell where it carries on; it stays inside wall, door and window cells, and doesn't cross into open cells.
- Light from outside (owner's rules): every site's open cells take the flat-ground light of the suns and moons at `WorldReturn`, darkened by clouds as the terrain shader does, once a game hour; no mountains' shadows. They spill it (`Spill`): from open cells beside anything open under no sky (through doors, windows, under covered ground), half as bright at no distance, reaching `SPILL_REACH` cells, stopped by walls as sight is, with each cell taking the strongest rather than adding up. A low brightest light stretches it along its way (`STRETCH`), so dawn makes a long shaft and noon a short pool. Stairs whose top is out in the open (`SeenStairs::open_to_sky`) spill too, evenly. Holes don't: the client doesn't connect levels. Water and lava count as under the sky only on farms, herbalists' and stilt huts (`open_air`), not underground. Lanterns add on top, up to full. Clouds change it only hourly here.
- Town windows (owner's rules; only looks, on the client): when the suns and moons give flat ground under `LIGHTS_ON_BELOW`, ignoring clouds, a building's lights come on, each building up to `LIGHTS_SPREAD_MINUTES` later by a hash of the world, place and building, and go off the same way at dawn. All its windows change together: a yellow-white pane (`LIT_WINDOW_TILE`), unaffected by the light, and `WINDOW_LIGHT` reaching `WINDOW_REACH` cells from in front of it, strongest rather than added up between windows, on top of the outside light. It lights tiles, not tokens.
- A town's or farm's pieces (roads, buildings, fences, hedges, fields) take their light per vertex in `town::emit`, so they follow the outside light and a lantern's pool. Crops and herbs follow only the outside light, through their materials (`tint_outside`), so a lantern doesn't brighten them.
- Light is worked out on the grid and baked into vertex colours (unlit materials): `picture × (ambient + (1 − ambient) × light)`, so full light shows the picture as drawn. Floors take corner-averaged light; walls blend between their cell's corners, except across the wall itself, so each side of it is lit by its own side. Light colours add up (clamped), so coloured lights work already; all are white for now. Stairs take their cell's light, times their shading (applied after the ambient, so the hole can be really dark).
- Each picture covers one cell (32 pixels per cell); parts smaller than a cell (steps) show part of it, so the texture keeps its scale.
- Remembered surfaces use grey copies of the pictures, made on the CPU once each picture loads (until then they show in colour), at an even `MEMORY_SHADE` rather than their light, half see-through (real blending, with floors' `depth_bias` keeping them behind walls; floors show through walls). `MEMORY_SHADE` is kept low so memory isn't brighter than cells in sight in the dull light.
- Pictures are clamped, not repeated: every piece keeps its picture coordinates within its cell, using its position there, so it lines up with the next cell. A wall carried on under liquid and the sides of a channel start the picture again from its top at the floor, so they repeat rather than stretch.
- Liquid (owner's rules): narrow where it has floor on two opposite sides, and half a cell deep there; a narrow channel's bed stays flat from cell to cell, and reaches a full cell deep only where it meets a wider liquid cell or a wall. Doors and windows count as floor, so liquid stops short of them. Liquid cells never pass their surface to neighbouring floors (`floor_quarter` skips them). In a wall's cell the liquid and bed run on under the wall's foot, unclipped, so they show under a see-through wall; a channel stopping at the wall's centre line (the far side dry) is closed there in the wall's picture (`close_behind_wall`), since at a wall's end, where the wall is narrower than the channel, that shows through the curve. Remembered liquid is only its flat outline: blended meshes don't sort against themselves, so bed and sides would muddle, and memory stays quiet. No fade across liquid (owner: it doesn't need blending).
- Holes (owner's rules): geometry, not pictures. Off the level's edge counts as hole, so no floor or side is drawn along it, only emptiness. The client doesn't have the level below, so through a hole it is only dark. A wall beside a hole keeps its floor patch, as a ledge. Ground eaten from a ruined building is plain `Cell::Ground`: biome colour, weather and the same light as any other cell, checked in screenshots.
- Natural shape is chosen by the theme for now; the generators may choose it per tile later, with more variations (door tops, window kinds). Material relief is only the wandering surface and shading: the materials are unlit, so no normal maps.
- Stairs are drawn from `CurrentDungeon::stairs`, not as tokens, in the style and running the way the server worked out. Models stay within 0.4 of a cell of the centre, leaving a border of floor (rope pegs stand on it). Spirals and ladders are placed the same on both levels (top on the `-descends` side, where the upper level's floor is); turning stairs have both ends on one side, so they open towards `+descends` going up and `-descends` going down, each onto the floor of its own level. Near sides of a shaft are culled, so the model shows. Treads are the floor's picture brightened (`TREAD_TINT`), step sides the wall's, darker; wood is `Surface::Door`'s picture, running along one plank (`PLANK_U`, `PLANK_V`); rope is one spot of it, tinted paler (`ROPE_UV`, `ROPE_TINT`). Models going up are built taller than going down, so they climb at a believable rate, and cut off at `STAIRS_UP_TOP` (`Model::ceiling`), which is above the walls. Stairs going up have no handrails (owner's). Clicks only land on the floor of the stairs' cell, not the model standing on it, so what is behind can be clicked (owner's; may change back). Rungs and knots are `UP_SPACING` further apart going up. Stairs hiding a viewer (`hides_viewer`) draw what stands above the floor half see-through (`StairsThroughPart`, one mesh per surface, back faces culled, faces not sorted) ; the floor, hole and anything below stay solid. The owner's rule is the same as for walls in the way, but tested by line of sight to the viewer rather than by which way a side faces. Step fronts are a fixed amount darker than treads whichever way they face (`FRONT_SHADE`; `front_tint` undoes `facing_shade`). Rails have a top and a middle bar. Going down only (owner's): the steps have no rails; a spiral's top step is a landing of twice the angle, with a rail round the top of the hole beside the landing and across its far edge to the column; turning stairs have a rail round the hole's top leaving the way onto the top step open. Depth shades are linear, so small numbers (`MODEL_DEEPEST_SHADE` 0.03) are what show as about 80% darker on screen.
- Any entity with `MapPosition`, `SmoothPosition` and `LightSource` lights the grid and tokens, not only creatures: `hall_view` gives the wilderness's World Gates one (8 cells), so they glow at night as on the world map.
- Tokens can't use baked light, so they get real lights: a `PointLight` (no shadows) `TOKEN_LIGHT_HEIGHT` above each light source, range (radius + 1) cells, plus a dim ambient. Tuned by eye. The player arriving by stairs stands on them (the token isn't raised or lowered).
- Sight and light use `MapPosition` (whole cells). Drawing (and hiding tokens, and click picking) uses glimpses, which are more generous than the game's symmetric sight; light keeps the symmetric rule.
- The see-through mesh now holds only town buildings in the way (`town::in_the_way`): walls, doors and windows leave it by `cutaway::settle_in_the_way`. It is one mesh with `AlphaMode::Blend`, so its faces don't sort against each other; with one picture at 50% this hasn't shown.
- Clicks: `TravelTo`; `player` finds the route (`shared::pathfinding`) and travels at 4 cells/s here (`shared::energy::SITE_CELLS_PER_SECOND`).
- Rebuilding everything (with sight) is about 1 ms for a 50 × 66 castle floor; it happens every frame while the camera moves (dragging, travelling). Bigger levels may need to rebuild only what changed: the wilderness's 192 × 192 window, all remembered once walked round, measured 5 to 17 ms.
- In `common::ClickMode::Teleport`, a click on the map (not a token) sends `DevTeleport` instead of `TravelTo` (dev tools: only the embedded server allows it; through walls in a site, but onto a walkable cell). `ClickMode::ChangeCell` sends `DevSetCell` (floor → wall → door → window → floor), to try wall shapes. Uses `connection::ToServer`.
- With `common::DetectMonsters` on, mobs' tokens show on cells nobody sees.
- Shader uniforms must be a multiple of 16 bytes, or WebGL 2 (the web build) refuses the pipeline and the game quits on its first frame; native is fine either way, so nothing else catches it. Pad a short struct with unused `f32`s, the same in the Rust struct and the shader (see `project.md`); `MaskParams` is padded to 32 (one of its spare `f32`s is now `flow_time`). The sight mask is drawn to `Rgba8UnormSrgb`, as WebGL 2 can't take a plain `Rgba8Unorm` camera target (entering a site quit the web build). `cargo xtask lint` checks both.
- Lightning (`common::Lightning`, owner, 2026-11-08): only what is under the sky flashes. `light_from_outside` lights open ground towards `FLASH_LIGHT` (about three times full daylight, as colours are multiplied, not added) and what spills in from outside `FLASH_SPILL` (10) times as strong, never more than outside; the spill's shape isn't worked out again. `update_meshes` rebuilds when the flash changes (twice a flash, every frame of Reduce Motion's fade); remembered outside ground and plants (`tint_outside`), tokens' ambient out in the open and rain follow it too. Lanterns and the Hall don't. Lights never darken a cell past full light (`LightGrid`'s `brighter`), so a light source in a flash leaves no dark ring. Bolts (`bolts.rs`, `strike`): only in the wilderness, where the server's cells are the level's, on an outside cell in sight, `BOLT_HEIGHT` 10 cells tall, marked `DungeonScene`; other sites only flash.
- Sloping ground (owner, 2026-11-12, replacing raised cells): every level's ground is `common::DrawnGround` (`shared::relief`: corner heights, four triangles a cell meeting at its middle), flat in buildings and the Hall, the land in the wilderness and a site's first level set into it, a gentle swell in caves. Everything a cell builds is built flat and then draped (`Marks::drape`): each vertex raised by the ground under it, so floors slope, upright faces stay upright on it and tops keep their height above it (`RAISED`, measured from each vertex's ground). Liquid follows the land too; stairs cells and pools are level by the rule. Pois' covered ground is level (`FlatGround`), and the corners round it take its height, so streets slope up to it. Things drawn apart from the cell meshes stand on it: the border's stripes (each corner on the ground), rain (each cell's middle), bolts, token lights, crops and herbs (draped), bags and gates (`ground`, `hall_view`), tokens and the camera (`view3d`), and the mask's area is measured from the ground the camera looks at (`sight_lines::area`). Sight isn't blocked by height (later). Clicks find the surface (`ground_hit`); the camera keeps a clear line over cells' middles.
- Flowing water (owner, 2026-11-08): `flowing.rs` (`run_flow`, each frame) sets the lit water material's `flow_time`, seconds wrapping every 8 (`PERIOD`; the band speeds, 1/8, 1/4 and 1/2 a cell a second (`SLOWEST_FLOW` in the shader), each a whole number of pictures in it, so it never jumps); 0 off the wilderness and with Reduce Motion (owner: no animation), and memory's water is always still. The shader moves the picture against the flow and wraps it with `fract` (pictures are sampled clamped, without mipmaps). Water on level ground (the sea, lakes, flat stretches of river) is still: flow comes only from the heights (owner: flat river stretches later).
- Closed doors stop sight: `sight_lines/wall_map.rs` takes `Level::door_bar` as a wall's bars, and light and sight go by `Level`, which knows them.
- Field of view (PR 9): the player's sight (`update_meshes`, `add_glimpses_in` from their cell's middle) and outline (`update_outlines`, `outline_in`) follow `common::PlayerCone`, rebuilt when the heading or field changes; those with `SharesSight` see all round. What is outside the cone is drawn as memory and is not remembered anew; lights are unchanged.
