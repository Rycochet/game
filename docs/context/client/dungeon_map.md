# client: dungeon_map

The dungeon (and building) view: `shared::dungeon` cells drawn in 3D through `view3d`'s camera, with the same tokens as the world map. Shown while `GameView::Dungeon` is current (inside a site, `CurrentDungeon`). Draws what the player, or anything with `SharesSight`, can see (or glimpse), including the level's stairs as 3D steps, and what they remember (seen before, out of sight now) dim and grey; hides tokens on cells nobody sees; lights it from `LightSource`s. Only cells the server has sent are drawn. Walls and floors use AngbandTk tile pictures chosen by the kind of place. Left-click a seen or remembered walkable cell to travel there.

## Files

- `crates/client/src/dungeon_map/mod.rs`: `DungeonMapPlugin`. `spawn_scene` (Startup: a `DungeonScene` root with one solid mesh per `Surface` (`SolidPart(surface)`), one remembered mesh per `Surface` (`MemoryPart(surface)`) and one see-through wall mesh, unlit, each with its own tile material (nearest sampling, `load_tile`), kept in `SurfaceMaterials`). `apply_theme` (when `CurrentDungeon` changes: wall and floor pictures from `Theme::of(LevelInfo::kind)`, queuing grey copies). `grey_pictures` (every frame while any are queued: once a picture has loaded, a grey copy (`greyed`, Rec. 601 weights) for the remembered material; copies are cached by picture). `pick_on_click` (in `PlayerSystems::Input` after `View3dSystems::Click`, unless a token was clicked: sends `TravelTo` for a remembered (including seen) walkable cell, using `CurrentDungeon::remembered` and the private `Shown` resource: last combined sight, solid wall sides, cells with stairs up). `show_scene` / `hide_scene` on entering/leaving the view (also sets `ViewGrid` to `CELL_SIZE` with the floor at 0, and the dim ambient light for tokens; leaving shows all tokens again). `update_meshes` (when a viewer or light moves, a `LightSource` or the level changes, or the camera's position changes (turning, zooming, following the player): one `Sight` per viewer plus the combined one, from `add_glimpses` (open cells partly in view count, so side openings off a corridor show), worked out on the known level (unknown cells are wall); seen known cells are added to `CurrentDungeon::remembered` (bypassing change detection); light, rebuild, store `Shown`; writes `ViewStats`, starting with `CurrentDungeon::describe()`; empty meshes are hidden, not replaced). `hide_unseen_tokens` (after `update_meshes`). `sync_lights` (one Bevy `PointLight` per `LightSource`, to light tokens). Constants `CELL_SIZE` (1.5 × token diameter = 24, about the token's picture), `WALL_HEIGHT`, `AMBIENT_LIGHT` (0.2), `SEE_THROUGH_ALPHA` (0.5), token light constants.
- `crates/client/src/dungeon_map/mesh.rs`: `Surface` (`Floor`, `Wall`, `Door`, `Ground`, `Water`, `Lava`: one mesh and picture each). `LightGrid` (light per cell: `add_light` uses `shared::dungeon::Sight` with the radius, so walls block it; strength `1 − (d / (radius + 1))²`; corner light = average over the open cells round a grid corner). `View` (a viewer's cell and its own `Sight`), `WallFace`. `build(level, views, sight, remembered, light, stairs, camera)` (only remembered cells; those out of sight go to the memory meshes (`build_remembered`: floors and stairs evenly at `MEMORY_SHADE` 0.45, wall sides facing a remembered open cell and the camera, `grey_wall_side`; the outside of a wall seen from inside counts too); in sight: a flat quad per open cell by its kind (doors are open for now, so flat; secret doors look like walls); stairs instead of the quad on their cells; for each wall only its `visible_sides`, each solid or see-through by `faces_camera`; returns the solid sides for picking). `build_stairs` (given the cell's colour and the floor's; `STAIR_STEPS` 4, half a cell wide (`STAIRS_HALF_WIDTH`) down the middle of the cell, running `SeenStairs::descends`: stairs up are a block of steps in the wall's masonry on the whole floor, rising to `STAIRS_UP_HEIGHT` (0.75 of a wall); stairs down are a hole `STAIRS_DOWN_DEPTH` (half a cell) deep with floor either side, floor-picture steps and brick sides; shaded by hand since unlit: risers 0.6, sides 0.8, the hole from 0.6 at the rim to 0.05 at the bottom). `visible_sides` (a viewer sees a side when it sees the wall and the open cell in front of it, and stands on that side: at most two per viewer, never the top). `faces_camera(centre, normal, camera)` (the camera's position is in front of the side's plane). Unit tests.
- `crates/client/src/dungeon_map/picking.rs`: `pick(ray, level, sight, solid_faces, stairs_up)`: the ray to the floor (height 0), or first into the box of a seen staircase up, gives a cell, if it is walkable and seen and no solid wall side is hit first (see-through sides don't block). Unit tests.
- `crates/client/src/dungeon_map/theme.rs`: `Theme { wall, floor }` and `Theme::of(Option<FeatureKind>)`: stone (castles, forts, stone towers and cottages, warehouses, pyramids...; also the test room), wood (huts, tents, cottages, wooden towers), crypt (crypts, graves, mausoleums, ruins, cellars under barrels), cave (natural caves, boulders), ice (ice cave, ice shelter). `picture(surface)`: doors (wood), outside ground (grass), water and lava are the same in every theme. Test: every named picture exists.

## Notes

- Grid cell `(x, y)` is centred on `(x × CELL_SIZE, 0, y × CELL_SIZE)`; walls are `WALL_HEIGHT` tall, but only their seen sides are drawn.
- Wall rule (owner's): only sides someone sees are drawn (tops and bottoms never). A seen side facing the camera is solid; one facing away (the wall stands between the camera and what is seen) is drawn 50% see-through, from behind (the see-through material doesn't cull back faces). Facing is measured from the camera's position, not its direction, so as the camera turns a side flips only when the camera passes its plane; this is the same test as back-face culling, so solid sides are always front faces. Out of sight: remembered sides facing the camera are drawn grey (see-through ones are left out, so memory stays quiet); never-seen ones not at all.
- Light is worked out on the grid and baked into vertex colours (unlit materials): `picture × (ambient + (1 − ambient) × light)`, so full light shows the picture as drawn. Floors and wall sides take corner-averaged light (smooth across floors, no leaking out of walls). Light colours add up (clamped), so coloured lights work already; all are white for now. Stairs take their cell's light, times their shading (applied after the ambient, so the hole can be really dark).
- Each picture covers one cell (32 pixels per cell); parts smaller than a cell (steps) show part of it, so the texture keeps its scale.
- Remembered surfaces use grey copies of the pictures, made on the CPU once each picture loads (until then they show in colour), at an even `MEMORY_SHADE` rather than their light.
- Stairs are drawn from `CurrentDungeon::stairs`, not as tokens, running the way the server worked out (the same on both levels). The hole's near sides face away from the camera and are culled, so the steps show; at the starting tilt only the far part of the hole is visible.
- Tokens can't use baked light, so they get real lights: a `PointLight` (no shadows) `TOKEN_LIGHT_HEIGHT` above each light source, range (radius + 1) cells, plus a dim ambient. Tuned by eye. The player arriving by stairs stands on them (the token isn't raised or lowered).
- Sight and light use `MapPosition` (whole cells). Drawing (and hiding tokens, and click picking) uses glimpses, which are more generous than the game's symmetric sight; light keeps the symmetric rule.
- The see-through mesh is one mesh with `AlphaMode::Blend`, so its faces don't sort against each other; with one picture at 50% this hasn't shown.
- Clicks: travel uses the world map's straight-line rules (through walls) until pathfinding (planned as a separate task); `player` travels at 12 cells/s here.
- Rebuilding everything is ~0.1 ms for the test room and for a 50 × 66 castle floor; it happens every frame while the camera moves (dragging, travelling). Bigger levels may need to rebuild only what changed.
- Ctrl+click on the map (not a token) sends `DevTeleport` instead of `TravelTo` (dev tools: only the embedded server allows it; through walls in a site, but onto a walkable cell). Uses `connection::ToServer`.
