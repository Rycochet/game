# client: view3d

What every 3D view shares: the one camera (orbiting the player: turn, tilt, zoom) and the entity tokens. The views (`world_map`, `dungeon_map`) draw their own scenes and tell this module their grid layout through `ViewGrid`.

## Files

- `crates/client/src/view3d/mod.rs`: `View3dPlugin`. `spawn_camera` (Startup: `Camera3d` with `ViewCamera` and `OrbitCamera`, drawing out to `DRAW_DISTANCE` (3 × `CAMERA_DISTANCE`), tonemapping and debanding off, light slices from `light_clusters`; inserts `TokenAssets`). In `PlayerSystems::Input`: `camera::control_with_mouse`, `camera::control_with_touch`, `camera::control_with_keys`; in `View3dSystems::Click` (inside `Input`): `token::click_tokens`. After `PlayerSystems::Travel`, in `View3dSystems::Place`: `token::resize_tokens`, `grow_hulls`, `sync_tokens`, `sync_icons`, `sync_models`, `flash_tokens`, then `place_camera_and_tokens` (camera looks at the player's `SmoothPosition` × cell size at floor height, writes `common::ViewFacing` and `common::ViewTurn`; token views stand on the floor at their owners' `SmoothPosition`; icon anchors stand upright, turned to the camera; model anchors stand on the base's top, turned to the camera and then by the model's own `turn`, scaled by `MODEL_SCALE`). Adds the material plugins for `XrayMaterial`, `HullMaterial` and `common::ModelMaterial`. Constants `CAMERA_FOV_DEGREES` (20), `CAMERA_DISTANCE` (1100) and `CAMERA_PITCH_DEGREES` (34) for the starting view, `LOOK_AHEAD`.
- `crates/client/src/view3d/render_scale.rs`: `apply_render_scale` (Update): Render Scale and Max Resolution (`GraphicsPrefs::drawn_share` of the window's physical height). Below 100% the view's camera draws into a smaller picture (`ImageRenderTarget` whose scale factor keeps its logical size the window's), shown stretched by a second camera (`Camera2d`, order 1, layer `SCREEN_LAYER` 2) that then also draws the interface at full resolution. At 100% neither the picture nor the second camera exists. Unit test.
- Reduce Motion: `place_camera_and_tokens` puts the camera and tokens on their owners' `MapPosition` rather than their `SmoothPosition`, so they jump from cell to cell.
- `crates/client/src/view3d/light_clusters.rs`: `light_clusters(far)`: Bevy's default light clustering, but with the last depth slice fixed at the camera's drawing distance (`ClusterFarZMode::Constant`) instead of following the farthest light as the GPU measured it a few frames before.
- `crates/client/src/view3d/anti_aliasing.rs`: `apply_anti_aliasing`: the chosen `Msaa` on the view's camera (2× is 4× in a browser).
- `crates/client/src/view3d/clearance.rs`: `clearing_raise(orbit, target, cell, ground)`: how much higher than its setting the camera must be shown for a clear line from the top of the player's token to it, a quarter cell over every cell's ground it passes and the camera's own, in 1° steps up to the steepest tilt; `eased` (eases there and back; at once with Reduce Motion). `place_camera_and_tokens` uses it on raised ground (`ViewGrid::raised` with heights): the wilderness only (owner: a clear line, never the back of the land's meshes; the world map's edge faces never let it under). Unit tests.
- `crates/client/src/view3d/camera.rs`: `OrbitCamera` (component: pub `yaw`, `pitch` (the player's tilt setting), `distance`; `new(pitch, distance, look_ahead)`, `reset()`, `transform(target)` (at `shown_pitch()`: the setting plus a `raise` the view keeps, over raised ground; tilting by mouse or touch starts from what is shown, which becomes the setting), `ground_axes()` (right and towards-camera along the ground), `quarter_turns()`, `zoom()` (distance / starting distance), `set_reach(r)` / `reach()` (how far out it may go, times the starting distance: 1 usually, 1.25 in the Hall of Worlds, `hall_view`; `reset` goes that far), `set_lowest_pitch(degrees)` (`None` the usual 10°; 18° in the Hall)), `control_with_mouse` (right-drag turns and tilts, `RADIANS_PER_PIXEL` 0.005; pitch 10°–85°; wheel zooms ×0.9 per notch, between the starting distance (the furthest out) and `MIN_ZOOM` 0.6 of it, both the owner's choice; Home resets all three), `control_with_touch` (from `common::TouchGesture`: a pinch zooms, a twist turns with the fingers, both fingers up or down tilt as right-dragging does, within the same limits). In `camera/keys.rs`: `control_with_keys` (Turn Camera Left / Right and Zoom In / Out: a press steps `TURN_STEP_DEGREES` 5° or one wheel notch at once, holding goes on at 60°/s or 3 notches/s by real time, judged afresh each frame so it stops when the key comes up). Unit tests.
- `crates/client/src/view3d/bands.rs`: `drift_bands`: turning, tilting, zooming and walking slide `common::BandShift` the way they change, eased and capped (owner's design, numbers Claude's), on top of the slow drift `widgets` gives it. Unit tests.
- `crates/client/src/view3d/token.rs`: how `Token`s are drawn. `TokenAssets` (resource: one body and one outline mesh per shape, the icon quad, one lit body material per attitude and one outline material, each in a normal and a completed version; `icon_offset(scale)`, `icon_rotation(camera_rotation)`), `prism` (by size: `base_radius`, Medium's `DIAMETER` 16; thickness 15% of Medium's), `TokenView` (separate root entity per token: body, outline, their x-ray copies and `IconAnchor` children), `sync_tokens`, `sync_icons` (unlit, blended icon quads (they must write no depth, see X-ray), each with its x-ray silhouette beside it, nearest sampling, one material per icon file; none where the owner has a `common::TokenModel`), `resize_tokens` (outline meshes rebuilt in place when the window height or zoom changes), `HoveredToken`, `ClickedToken` and `find_token_under_cursor` (every frame, the nearest visible `Clickable` token whose base circle, icon picture or model's upright box (`ModelMeshes::height`, `reach`) is under the cursor, measured on screen; `ClickedToken` if the left button was just pressed). Outline: inverted hull (`cull_mode: Front`) grown by `OUTLINE_PIXELS` (2) in screen pixels. In `token/`: `parts.rs`, `assets.rs`, `sync.rs`, `pick.rs` (`ClickedToken`, `HoveredToken`), `flash.rs` (`TokenFlash`, `flash_tokens` after `sync_icons` and `sync_models`: the body takes `TokenAssets::flash_body`, unlit red, a model's every piece `HullMaterials::flash`, and the picture a silhouette, black lit by nothing and glowing red through the same alpha cut-out, one per icon made when first needed; the outline stays black), `xray.rs` (`XrayMaterial`, `XRAY_WHITE`, `XRAY_FIRST`; shader `assets/shaders/xray.wesl`), `model.rs` (`MODEL_SCALE`, 0.0124 world units a millimetre: a person about as tall as the pictures were; `ModelAnchor` (on each `TokenView`: the model it shows, in which attitude's colour); `ModelPart` (a piece's usual material, for the flash); `sync_models`: an owner's `TokenModel` as children of its anchor, each piece in its `ModelMaterial`, the black hull, and the skin's x-ray fill and outline; it hides the base's own x-ray while a model stands on it, as the model would count as hiding it), `hull.rs` (`HullMaterial` by `HullKind`: `Black`, `XrayFill`, `XrayOutline`; `HullMaterials`: the black one, a fill and an outline per attitude, the flash's red; `grow_hulls` keeps their growth `OUTLINE_PIXELS` on screen as the window or zoom changes; shader `assets/shaders/hull.wesl`).

## Public API

- `View3dPlugin`, `View3dSystems::Click` (systems handling clicks run after it) and `::Place` (views that adjust token heights run after it).
- `ClickedToken(Option<Entity>)` (resource): the `common::Clickable` entity whose token the left button was just pressed on, this frame only. Views skip their ground click when it is set; `site` uses it.
- `HoveredToken(Option<Entity>)` (resource): the entity the mouse is over whose token is `common::Clickable` or has a `common::HoverName`, every frame; `world_map` outlines it and `hover` names it. `ClickedToken` is only ever a clickable one, the nearest under the pointer even when a named one stands in front.
- `ViewCamera` (marker on the camera), `OrbitCamera` (read or set `yaw` (0 looks north, positive moves the camera east), `pitch`, `distance`; used by `dev_tools` `HOW_DEBUG_VIEW`, `world_map`, `dungeon_map` and `hall_view`).
- `ViewGrid` (resource, set by the current view on entering): `cell_size` (3D units per grid step), `floor` (height the camera looks at and tokens stand on) and `raised` (the dungeon view's: the level's ground has a shape, `common::DrawnGround`, and `floor_at(ground, x, y)` adds the ground's height there, `drawn_between`, so the camera keeps the player where they are on screen as the land moves under them, and tokens stand on the slope).
- `TokenView` (`owner`), `TOKEN_DIAMETER`, `TOKEN_HALF_THICKNESS`: for views that place tokens themselves (the world map rests them on the terrain) or size things to them.
- `XrayMaterial` (`outline(colour)`, `fill(colour)`, `picture(colour, image)`; its plugin is added here) and `XRAY_WHITE`: for any model that should show through what hides it (`ground`'s bags, `dungeon_map`'s stairs and doors).
- `TokenFlash` (component, `Default`): put on a token's owner to draw its body, model and picture solid red for `FLASH_SECONDS` (0.1 s, by the clock, seen for at least a frame), then it is taken off; adding it again starts again. `entities` puts it on anyone harmed.

## Depends on

- `common` (`ViewFacing`, `ViewTurn`, `Token`, `Icon`, `TokenModel`, `ModelMaterial`, `SmoothPosition`, `Clickable`), `player` (`Player`, `PlayerSystems`).

## Used by

## Notes

- Render Scale: because the picture's logical size is the window's, `viewport_to_world` and `world_to_viewport` with logical pointer positions work unchanged at any scale. The interface follows Bevy's default UI camera, which is the highest-order camera drawing into the window: the view's camera at 100%, the second camera below it.
- Light slices end at a fixed depth (`light_clusters.rs`): with Bevy's default the end trails the camera by a few frames (the GPU reads it back late, and only the farthest light, or the world's origin, sets it), so zooming out fast left a light wholly past it and unlit: the glow on the ground round a World Gate on the world map vanished while the wheel turned, wherever the origin was behind the camera. Don't go back to `MaxClusterableObjectRange`.
- Anti-aliasing off: faded tokens' `AlphaToCoverage` needs MSAA; without it Bevy cuts them out instead (rougher, as the tooltip says).

- Camera: narrow field of view (20°) from far away (a "long lens"), so perspective is flat. It looks at the floor (sea level on the world map, so it doesn't bob over hills), ahead of the player towards the camera by `LOOK_AHEAD` in the starting view (shrinking as it zooms in or looks down). If the FOV changes, scale `CAMERA_DISTANCE` by `tan(old/2) / tan(new/2)` to keep the framing; `far` is twice the starting distance.
- `ViewFacing` (common) is the camera's yaw rounded to a quarter turn; `player` uses it so arrow keys step towards the top of the screen on the world map. `ViewTurn` is the yaw itself (negated: clockwise from north), for walking at any angle on levels. Both written with `set_if_neq`.
- Token sizes are in 3D units, the same in every view: on the world map a token covers 16 grid points; in a dungeon cell (24 units) it fits inside.
- Models (owner, 2026-11-12, free positions PR 8): creatures are 3D models (`common::TokenModel`, from `item_art::CreatureModels`) standing on their bases, in place of the flat pictures. They turn the way their owners face (`common::Facing`, PR 9): `token/turn.rs`'s `turn_models` (after `place_camera_and_tokens`) turns each `ModelAnchor` towards its heading at up to two turns a second (`ModelAnchor::angle` keeps what is shown), at once with Reduce Motion; front +z along the heading. One whose owner has no `Facing` faces the camera, as icons do. Glow (`common::TokenGlow`, a quest boss's tier): the same hull as `HullKind::Glow` in the colour, grown `GLOW_SHARE` (3) times as far and pushed back as far again, so a band of colour shows beyond the black line; one material per colour, made as wanted (`HullMaterials::glow`). Outline: the model's welded hull with smooth normals, grown in the vertex shader by `OUTLINE_PIXELS` in world units (`grow_hulls`: no mesh is rebuilt on zoom) and pushed back from the camera by 1.5 times that, seen from inside, so lines show round the silhouette and where a part stands well clear of what is behind it, never round every rivet. X-ray: the skin alone (not what is worn, nor sheets of hair, whose inner faces would count as hidden), pulled 40 mm towards the camera so the base never hides the soles.
- Drawing order of models (Bevy sorts see-through things by the middle of each mesh's bounds): pieces are drawn with see-through things (`ModelMaterial`: blended, yet solid and writing depth), and every mesh of a model has the same bounds, reaching far below the ground (`item_art`), so they sort together and before any see-through wall, memory or water, which then blend over them where they are in front. Every x-ray (`XRAY_FIRST`: tokens', bags', stairs', doors' and models') is drawn before that, so a model covers the x-ray of whatever stands behind it, and its own x-ray is only tested against what is solid, never against itself. An x-ray's fill writes depth, so rarely a model further back may draw over a wall where something x-rayed stands behind it.
- Token views never rotate (shape turns are baked into the meshes). An `IconAnchor` stands upright on the map, turned about the vertical towards the camera (`TokenAssets::icon_rotation`), so it faces the screen but never leans back as the camera tilts (owner, 2026-11-09; models turn the way their owners face instead); looking steeply down it is seen shortened. Icons are sized relative to the token, not the screen (owner's choice, for a blockier retro look): the central 32×32 picture is `ICON_ART_SIZE` (1.5 × the diameter) wide at `TokenSize::Default`, scaled by the owner's `TokenSize` (the anchor's transform scale, kept in `IconAnchor::scale`, set by `sync_tokens`; `icon_offset` takes it too). Token bases are drawn to the creature's size (`Token::base`, by `Size::radius`: Medium's is `DIAMETER`, Tiny a quarter of it, Giant three times; one mesh per size and shape, outlines kept the same width), whatever the icon size; clicks on a base go by its radius (`TokenView`'s). The feet (bottom of the central picture less `ICON_SHADOW_PIXELS`: 3 art pixels) stand `ICON_GAP_PIXELS` (1 art pixel) above the top's centre; what hangs below them is inside the token and hidden. The art (Gervais tiles) is planned to be replaced: retune `ICON_FILE_PIXELS`, `ICON_ART_PIXELS`, `ICON_ART_SIZE`, `ICON_GAP_PIXELS` and `ICON_SHADOW_PIXELS` to match it. Polygons have a corner pointing north, then turn: the square to line up with the grid, the hexagon (30°) and octagon (22.5°) to put a flat side towards the camera in the starting view.
- Completed tokens (`fade`): sRGB colour × `COMPLETED_SHADE` (0.7), alpha `COMPLETED_OPACITY` (0.9, owner's choice) with `AlphaMode::AlphaToCoverage` (needs MSAA): ordinary blending showed the outline's inner faces through the body. Coverage comes in coarse steps, so small opacity changes may do nothing.
- Tokens are lit by real Bevy lights: the world map's sun, the dungeon's point lights. Every token is drawn in every view; views hide the ones that don't belong (the world map hides tokens outside its window via the `TokenView`'s `Visibility`, and despawns its pois when left). The dungeon view hides tokens on cells nobody sees.
- Bevy UI draws on this camera (the only one).
- The camera draws to three times `CAMERA_DISTANCE` (`far`), so the Hall of Worlds' floor reaches the top of the screen at its lowest tilt.
- X-ray (owner, 2026-11-09): tokens and things the player should know about show through whatever hides them (walls, hills, other tokens), in the token's attitude colour, or white for anything else: a coloured outline round a faint fill, and a faint silhouette of the picture. Each is an `XrayMaterial` copy of the model's meshes whose pipeline only draws what is further than what is already there (depth test `Less`, depth reversed), in the transparent pass after everything solid, ordered within a model by `Material::depth_bias`: picture, then the body's front faces (writing depth, so the outline isn't drawn over them), then the outline's front faces. Parts in plain sight sit exactly at the drawn depth and fail, with a little pipeline depth bias to be sure. The x-ray outline is `raised_prism` (not below the ground, or every token would get a line under it), and the picture's silhouette is raised by `ICON_SHADOW_PIXELS - ICON_GAP_PIXELS` so its feet aren't hidden by its own token. Icon pictures are blended (`AlphaMode::Blend`) rather than cut out: written into the depth, a picture would hide its own token's back half and the x-ray would show through it. See-through walls (blended) write no depth, so they hide nothing from it. Clicks already reach hidden tokens (`find_token_under_cursor` checks only on screen).
- The wheel zooms only while the pointer is on the view: with `dom::Hit` on anything of the interface (a panel, a button, markup), `control_with_mouse` leaves it alone (owner, 2026-11-10).
