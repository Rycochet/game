# client: inventory

The Character panel, in tabs: Character (what the character is like, `character`'s words), Inventory (what they wear and carry, and the ground), Abilities (`abilities` fills it) and Skills (`skills_page` fills it, `common::SkillsPage`); a moveable panel for each open bag; and picking items up, holding them and putting them down. Native and web.

## Files

- `crates/client/src/inventory/mod.rs`: `InventoryPlugin`, `Carried`, `InventoryDebug` (native), the module list. Beside it: `holding.rs` (`Holding`, `HeldBy`, `Hand`, `cancel_held`), `server.rs` (`read_server`, `Stacks`: the server's `StackLimits` from `Welcome`), `finger.rs` (`pick_up_by_finger`, `PreUpdate`, before widgets), `early_pictures.rs` (`ask_early`: pictures of everything carried and worn asked for as soon as `Carried` changes, so they are drawn before a panel shows them), `debug.rs` (`run_debug`, native), `sort.rs` (`SortButton`, `spawn_sort`, `press_sort`, the sort icon drawn in code), `ui_ids.rs` (`grid_id`), `wear_note.rs` (why something didn't go on, and `try_wearing`), `split.rs` (`Split`, the split box: `show_split`, `move_split`, `close_split`; `split/picker.rs`: `PickRow`, a mixed stack's rows).
- `crates/client/src/inventory/hold_items.rs`: `hold_items` (`PreUpdate`, after `WidgetSystems::Modal`), `is_click`, `worth_wearing`, and its `SystemParam`s `Under` (what the pointer may be over) and `Outbox` (what letting go writes). It runs three steps in `hold_items/`, in order: `press.rs` (presses: a double click opens a bag, a press picks up), `aim.rs` (turning, following the pointer or finger, and the `Target`), `let_go.rs` (taking, wearing, dropping, giving, binning or moving).
- `crates/client/src/inventory/hold.rs`: without Bevy: `Held`, `Target` (with `Stack` and a bag's stack), `target`, `drop_move`, `stack_onto`. Unit tests.
- `crates/client/src/inventory/body.rs`: `spawn_rows`, `BodyNode`, `show_clothes` (the table, then the Held column), `show_body_aim`, `worn_notes`. `hands.rs`: `held_now`, `in_hands`, `spawn_held_column` (a slot for each hand).
- `crates/client/src/inventory/give.rs`: `NearbyPanel`, `GiveRow`, `show_nearby`, `show_give_aim`.
- `crates/client/src/inventory/ground.rs`: `Ground`, `spawn_section`, `read_server`, `show_section`, `show_aim`.
- `crates/client/src/inventory/filter.rs`: `GridFilter`, `FilterIcons`, `make_icons` (`Startup`), `spawn_filter`, `chosen`, `dims`, `keep_filters` (icons on new filters, greying empty categories, the ground grid's filter). `filter/icons.rs`: the pictures for All and each category, drawn in code.
- `crates/client/src/inventory/panels.rs`: `OpenGrids` and the panels' components, the module list. In `panels/`: `fixed.rs` (`spawn_fixed_parts`), `icons.rs` (the person, bin and ground icons, drawn in code), `grids.rs` (`OpenGrids::open`, each grid with its sort and filter buttons above it), `pages.rs` (`Page`, `Pages`, `spawn_pages`: the tab row and a page per tab, only the chosen one laid out; `switch_pages`), `own.rs` (`toggle_inventory`: the button and the tabs' keys, `forget_closed`: a grid whose drawn size changes is drawn again in place), `shown.rs` (`shown_size`, `grows`: a growing bag's grid drawn to the rows in use plus room for the largest thing it takes, never past its most rows), `bins.rs` (`show_bins`, `show_drop`), `show.rs` (`show_grids`, `show_weights`), `aim.rs` (`show_aim`), `held.rs` (`show_held`, `show_tooltip`, `show_button`).

## Public API

- `InventoryPlugin`.
- `OpenCharacter` (message): opens the Character panel on its Inventory tab, or brings it to the front (`ground`).
- `Carried(Inventory)` (resource): what is carried, as the server last said plus any move since.
- `Stacks(StackLimits)` (resource): how big the server lets stacks get, from `Welcome` (`debug_menu`'s Give Item).
- `InventoryDebug` (message, native): `OpenInventory`, `OpenBags`, `PickUp(index)`, `DropSome`. Used by `dev_tools`.

## Depends on

- `shared::item::ItemCategory`, `common` (`GameView`, `PointerPosition`, `PointerSystems`, `TouchGesture`, `GROUND`, `SpentEnergy`), `connection` (`ToServer`, `FromServer`), `player` (`Player`), `widgets`, `shared::item`, `shared::id`, `shared::protocol`.

## Used by

- `dev_tools`, `debug_menu` (`Carried`, `Stacks`).

## Notes

- Ids (`widgets::UiId`, from `ui_ids::grid_id`): a grid's panel, `sort` and `filter` are in `inventory`, `bag#<id>` or `ground`; the corner button is `hud/character`, the split box's slider `split/count`, the Nearby panel `nearby/panel`.

- Kinds with models show their pictures (`item_art`) in grids, in the hand, on the clothes panel and in the bin, with the same edges. Other kinds keep their colour and label.
- An item's quality colours its edge (2 px, wider than a plain item's) in grids, worn and held, and its name, on a dark tooltip so every colour can be read (`widgets::spawn_item_cells`, `ItemTooltip`, which draw items anywhere).
- Owner's design: press on an item to pick it up. It follows the pointer at its true size, held by the cell it was pressed on, and letting go drops it; a quick click (`is_click`) instead keeps it held until the next click (`Hand::Clicked`), which goes to nothing else under it. Anywhere it can't go, it snaps back, and Esc always puts it back (`cancel_held`, before the menus see Esc). A held item isn't drawn where it was but nothing has changed until the drop, which is one `MoveItem`, so a lost connection can't lose it.
- The cells it would take show green, or red if refused. Dropped on a closed bag, the bag's own cells show this, and it goes in the first place it fits (`Inventory::first_place_for`), turning if it must.
- Right-click or R turns what is held; by touch, a tap with a second finger (owner agreed to try this, to adjust after testing). While a finger holds something, its two-finger gestures don't reach the camera, and the right button doesn't turn it.
- The bin at the bottom right of the inventory is a slot (owner's design): what is dropped on it stays there (`Inventory::bin`, whatever its shape, drawn as one cell), weighs nothing and can be picked up again, until something else is dropped there, which destroys it and all in it. Green if empty, red if it would destroy, with the name saying so. It only counts if nothing is in front of it at the pointer. A bag in it can't be opened. Panels of bags destroyed close themselves.
- What is secret isn't known here (`shared::item`): dropping something refused only for size or fingers on the clothes still asks the server (green), and it snaps back if refused. Something worn with a known curse can't be picked up, unless in `common::GodMode`; one not yet known can, and the answer puts it back, now glowing.
- The three parts share a panel, in the owner's order, with `character` filling in `common::SheetText`. Clothes (owner's design: dragged on and off): a row per place on the body and a column per layer (Claude's layout). Weapons are held in the Held column, between the table and the inventory (owner, 2026-11-07): a slot for each hand, one above the other (a two-handed weapon shows in both), dropped there as anything worn is. Fingers show only their Skin slot (owner): only rings are on fingers alone, and gloves covering them show on Hands. Dropping anything on it wears it (`WearItem`), with the rows green or red first, by the server's rule (`wear_note::try_wearing`, `Inventory::wear_swapping` on a copy): what on its layer is in the way comes off into the inventory (owner, 2026-11-07). A red line under the clothes (`wear_note.rs`: `WearNote`, `show_wear_note`, `explain`) says why it can't go on while held there, and for 4 s after a refused drop or the server's `NotWorn` (what is in the way won't come off, or has no room). Dragging off into a grid takes it off (`MoveItem`); dropped elsewhere, it snaps back. Something covered is paler and its name says what it is under, or that it is loose; it can still be picked up, dropped, given or binned, only changing it takes longer (owner, 2026-11-09). Rings on several fingers share a slot, with a count. A worn bag opens with a double press as anywhere.
- Giving: a Nearby panel opens with the Character panel, to become a trading panel (owner), listing who is within `mob::GIVE_REACH`, by `common::Other`. Dropping a held item on a row sends `GiveItem`; the server also checks they are in sight, and `Inventory` puts it right if not. Clicking people on the map to give is for later, as dropping on tokens needed more than this.
- Let go on a text box that takes items (`common::TakesItemText`, in front at the pointer), anything held, carried or someone else's, is written into it (`DroppedOnText`) and goes back where it was, aiming at nothing meanwhile (`HeldOverText`).
- Anything with `common::TakesGifts` counts as a row: `inventory` says what is held over it and let go on it (`HeldOverGifts`, `GaveItem`), for `trade` to show.
- Something picked up from a trading panel is held in `Holding::stock`, still theirs until dropped (`hold::target_for_new`, `TakeItem`). It can't go on the bin, the clothes or someone's row from there.
- Double press on a bag opens it: a second press within `DOUBLE_PRESS_SECONDS`, which by mouse puts back what the first picked up. A press on anything else always picks up.
- By touch (owner's design): a finger staying still on an item picks it up (`TouchGesture::still_at`), and it follows `first_finger`; a finger sliding at once scrolls instead. A tap (`TouchGesture::tap`) never picks up: the pointer stays on it, so its name shows, and two on a bag open it. Only what is in front at the point counts, the same as for presses.
- Moves are shown at once and `Inventory` follows every one, so a refused move goes back. Client-only choices: a panel per grid, a bag's panel closing when it is no longer carried.
- Items are plain coloured cells with the first letters of their names, until there are pictures.
- The ground (owner's design; sites only, never the world map): what is dropped on the Drop icon, left of the bin, goes on the cell under the player (`DropItem`), red on the world map, for what is covered and for anything held from the ground or a shop. While the player stands on something, a section to the right of the inventory shows it in a scrolling grid 8 wide (`widgets::StockGrid`, with `common::GROUND` standing for who it is with, so `hold_items` treats it as stock and gifts). What is dropped on the grid goes on the ground. From the ground, things can go to grids, the clothes (`WearFromGround`) or the bin (`BinFromGround`), but not the Drop icon, someone's row or a shop. Worn things can go straight to the ground if nothing covers them. A bag on the ground can't be opened until picked up.
- Each grid is in a `widgets::spawn_container` frame (`panels/grids.rs` `spawn_grid`): sort and filter in its controls, its weight under it, and for the inventory the Drop and bin in its slots. A growing bag's (`ContainerDef::grows`) grows too, scrolling past `MOST_HEIGHT` of the window, with its weight, keeping its place by the bag's id (`widgets::ScrollKey`). The ground section's grid is one too, growing, titled "On the ground", with only the filter.
- Under each grid, its weight (`widgets::weight_words`): the inventory's against `common::CarryLimit` (`12.34kg / 45.00kg`), a bag's against its `max_total`, or alone if it has none. `show_weights` writes them when what is carried or the limit changes.
- Moving things between containers takes time, by weight (`Inventory::move_cost`, `put_down_cost`, `energy::handling_cost`); sorting one grid doesn't. Putting on and taking off take `Swapped::cost` or `Inventory::taking_off_cost`, longer under other things (binning something worn too). `SpentEnergy` tells `player`, so it doesn't run ahead of the server; the dressing part also goes out as `common::Dressing`, for `entities`' placeholder bar.
- Stacks (`shared::item`): shown by their top, with a count, and named by it. Held over something that looks the same, its cells show green if some can go on (`StackItem`, or `StackFromGround` from the ground; never from a shop), red if none can; dropped on a closed bag, a stack inside it that takes all of it comes before a free place. Only a stack's top is worn, from a grid or the ground, the rest staying where it was.
- Splitting (owner's design): shift+click, or a tap, on a stack opens a dark box by it (`split`) with its name, a slider from one to all (opening at half) and the stack at that count. Pressing that stack picks up that many from its top (`Inventory::part_of`; the part is lifted out in `Carried` while held, again if the server's answer comes first) and closes the box; Esc, a press elsewhere or the stack going closes it. Letting go sends the usual message with the part's id. Once nothing is held, any part left lying on its stack goes back (`settle_parts`), so snapping back needs nothing more. The item's own name isn't shown while the box is.
- Mixed stacks (owner's design): a stack with something known to differ has a red "!" in its top right corner (`widgets`). Any press on it, a tap or a finger held on it opens the split box with rows instead of a slider: All, then each kind in it, top's first, each with a small picture keeping its glow, its name and how many. Pressing a row holds those items (`Holding::group`, lifted out in `Carried` by `lift_group`), and every message letting go sends is wrapped in `FromStack`. Snapping back puts them on top of the stack; once nothing differs the mark goes. Mixed stacks on the ground or in a shop are picked up whole for now.
- Filters (owner's design): a small button at the top right above each carried grid, and beside "On the ground", opens a list of All then each `ItemCategory`, with pictures; categories with nothing in that grid are greyed. The button shows the chosen one's picture. What isn't in it is faded (`ItemLook::dimmed`) but can still be picked up and named; a stack goes by its top. Each grid has its own, kept on its button, so closing a panel resets it to All.
- Sorting (owner's design): the Sort button, left of a carried grid's filter, lays it out again in one go (`Inventory::sort_grid`): what looks the same stacked, then largest first from the top left. The client sorts first and sends `SortGrid` only if that worked and changed something, so a sort that can't fit everything does nothing; the server does the same and answers with `Inventory`. Nothing happens while something is held. Not on the ground, which the server lays out.
- Tabs (owner): C opens the Character panel on Character, I on Inventory, B on Abilities, K on Skills (owner, 2026-11-07: Skills was a page of its own) (`common::Action`); the key of the tab on show closes it, another key switches tab. The person button opens or closes it on the tab last shown; `OpenCharacter` always shows Inventory and never closes it. Hidden pages stay spawned (`Display::None`), so grids and the ground section keep working; a hidden grid has no size, so nothing is dropped on it. Ids: `inventory/tabs`.
