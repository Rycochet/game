# client: minimap

The maps: the round minimap always in the window's top-right corner, and the full parchment map that M or a click on the minimap opens. Both show only what is known (lighting doesn't matter), painted on the CPU into a picture shown by Bevy UI, redrawn only when something it shows changes. On the world map they show the land's plain colours from above and pois' pictures; in sites, remembered tiles as a grid, coloured by their ground.

## Files

- `crates/client/src/minimap/mod.rs`: `MinimapPlugin`, `FullMap`. `spawn_maps` (Startup). `take_input` (`PreUpdate`, after Bevy's input systems: M, Esc, clicks on the minimap, close button and outside the parchment, dragging, wheel; takes what it uses out of the input resources, so the game's systems never see it). `draw_maps` (`Update`, after `View3dSystems::Place`: sizes the UI, decides whether to redraw, paints). `name_what_is_hovered` and `hovered_name` (the shop door or poi under the mouse, in the raised box). `location_title`, `words`. Sizes are constants at the top, in logical pixels (multiplied by the window's scale factor when painting), since the owner expects to tweak them: `MINIMAP_OF_HEIGHT`, `BORDER`, `NOTCH`, `CELL_PIXELS`, `SIGN_PIXELS`, `ICON_PIXELS`, `STAIRS_PIXELS`, `COMPLETED_OPACITY`, `DOT_PIXELS`, `DOT_OUTLINE`, `FULL_MAP_OF_WINDOW`, `FULL_MAP_MARGIN`, `TITLE_HEIGHT`, `CLOSE_PIXELS`, `HOVER_LIFT`, `OPENING_ZOOM`, `MIN_ZOOM`, `MAX_ZOOM`, `ZOOM_PER_NOTCH`, `COMPASS_LINE`, `COMPASS_OF_HEIGHT`, `COMPASS_MIN_LENGTH`, `COMPASS_INSET`, `KEEP_KNOWN_INSIDE`. Unit tests drive `take_input`.
- `crates/client/src/minimap/canvas.rs`: `Canvas` (an image's bytes: `fill`, `set`, `blend`, `shape` by a distance function, `disc`, `sprite`), `Sprite`, `polygon_distance`.
- `crates/client/src/minimap/projection.rs`: `Projection` (`to_grid`, `to_picture`, `grid_box`: turned and scaled), `slide` (how far a drag can go while staying allowed).
- `crates/client/src/minimap/floors.rs`: the full map's floor buttons: `spawn_column`, `buttons` (which, top first), `column` and `under` (where they are), `show_buttons` (rebuilds them when what they say changes), `back_to_own_floor`. Sizes: `BUTTON`, `BUTTON_WIDTH`, `BUTTON_GAP`.
- `crates/client/src/minimap/grid.rs`: site levels. `Looks::of` works out each cell in view as a `CellLook` (`Empty`, `Floor` with its colour from `ground_colour`, `Wall` with the `Sides` it is hatched along, `Door`, `HouseDoor`, `Sign`); `colour_at` is the drawing of a cell (grid line, hatching, door); `draw` samples it per pixel (four samples when turned).
- `crates/client/src/minimap/world.rs`: world map. `WorldTiles` keeps a chunk's colours (one per point, averaged by `draw` where a pixel covers more, from `SurfaceStyle::ground_colour`), worked out for chunks in view, nearest first, within a time budget per redraw; `shows_known`.
- `crates/client/src/minimap/frame.rs`: `RoundFrame` (cuts the picture round, ridged border and notch), `compass` (the same ridge, through `ridged`), `stairs_icon`, `player_dot`, `arrow_place`, `arrow`, `floor_hint` (the up or down arrow beside the player on another floor).
- `crates/client/src/minimap/sprites.rs`: `Sprites`: small copies of pictures, averaged down once per size, read from loaded `Image`s (`prepare`) or drawn by the caller (`make`: a poi's model, from `world_map::poi_picture`, which is why there are no icon files for pois, with a solid black border by `with_border`).

## Public API

- `MinimapPlugin`.
- `FullMap` (resource): `open`, `centre` (`None` follows the player), `zoom`. Used by `dev_tools`.

## Depends on

- `common` (`OtherLevels`, `FirstFloor`, `CurrentWorld`, `KnownPois::is_known`, `CurrentDungeon`, `GameClock`, `GameView`, `Portal`, `SmoothPosition`, `Token`, `Weather`), `surface_style` (`SurfaceStyle`), `view3d` (`OrbitCamera`, `View3dSystems`), `player` (`Player`, `PlayerSystems`), `world_map` (`HALF_SIZE`, `poi_icon`), `dungeon_map` (`TOWN_ATLAS`, `door_picture`), `ui` (`raised_box`), `shared::dungeon`, `shared::poi`, `shared::site` (`BuildingKind`).

## Notes

- Minimap (owner's design): about a fifth of the window's height across, top right, over everything; contents and notch turn with the camera so the notch points north. World map scale: its diameter shows the same distance as the 3D window's width, so zooming changes it. Sites: fixed `CELL_PIXELS`.
- Full map (owner's design, plus Claude's choices marked): a square of 90% of the window's shorter side, a 20 px bare margin, a centred title (location, and the floor or level shown), an X button, and a compass. Closes by M, Esc, the button or a click outside the parchment. It opens at twice the minimap's scale; the wheel zooms it from 1x to 4x towards the mouse, with pictures, the player's dot and the arrow to them scaling too (the grid lines stay their size). Dragging moves it; it can't leave what is known (at least `KEEP_KNOWN_INSIDE` px inside its edges must show something known). An arrow on its edge points to the player when off the map. North is always at the top, like a sheet of paper (Claude's choice). The minimap is hidden while it is open (Claude's choice). The game keeps running and the keyboard still walks; the mouse belongs to the map, so right-drag and the wheel do nothing to the camera (Claude's choice). Esc closes it rather than going back to the start. Hovering over a shop door or poi names it in a box near the bottom of the parchment.
- Floors (owner's design, full map only, never on the world map): with more than one floor known, a column of numbered buttons on the left of the full map, vertically centred; all the same width; the floor shown is darker with a flat border, the player's floor also has a flat border when not shown, the others are raised. Clicking one shows that floor, centred on the player's place on it (`OtherLevels::spot`: the same cell in a building, the same share of the level in a cave, whose levels differ in size), keeping any offset the map was dragged by, so floors can be compared. There the player's dot, or the arrow when off the map, is 20% smaller, with a little white up or down arrow (head and shaft, edged in black like the dot) almost touching it for which way their floor is. The arrow is as wide as the dot, so what is beside it doesn't move. The title names the floor being looked at ("Castle, floor 1", no "of"), numbered as its button is; the HUD keeps the player's floor. Claude's choices: a building's highest floor is on top, a cave's deepest at the bottom; closing the map or changing level goes back to the player's floor; dragging is free on a floor with nothing known round the player's place, until it finds something (`FullMap::kept_on` also stops the map sliding back from a kept offset).
- Clicks on the minimap's circle open the full map, and don't reach the land under it.
- Known: on the world map, the chunks the server has sent (`KnownPois`), saved with the character; in sites, `CurrentDungeon::remembered`. A wall is hatched towards a cell only if that is remembered too.
- Pictures stay upright when the map turns (the main view's icons face the camera too), so they are stamped on after the contents are turned: this is why the whole map is repainted rather than a north-up picture being turned by a shader.
- Grid squares are `CELL_PIXELS` (11) apart: 10 px of white and the 1 px line shared with the next.
- Tile colours (owner's): white floors, pale colours by road type, pale green outside and in gardens (Claude's choice), blue water and red lava; holes black (`HOLE`, owner's), with walls hatched towards them. A ruined building's title and hover name start "Ruined" (`LevelInfo::ruined`, `PoiLook::is_ruined`). The world map doesn't show rivers.
- Stairs (owner's): a flat icon of four steps on whatever tile they are on, highest on the left going down and on the right going up, so it stays upright when the map turns. Smaller than the player's dot, leaving more of a gap; remembered stairs only.
- Hatching (owner's): inside remembered wall cells, reaching a quarter of a cell in from each side that meets something open, including a dungeon door (so its sides don't look open), or a wall of a different building (so touching buildings are hatched on both sides). Town doors, house or shop, have none, and nothing hatches towards them.
- Touching corners (owner's): where two walls meet only at a corner with open cells either side, you can walk between them, so each loses a triangle at that corner (`Sides::cut`, `CORNER_CUT`) in the floor's colour.
- A shop's sign is its door picture from the town atlas (`door_picture`); a house has its own look, a red diamond on the line of its wall (the diamond is Claude's choice), so it differs from a dungeon's door.
- Hovering a poi on the world map itself names it in a box near the bottom of the window (`name_hovered_token`, from `view3d::HoveredToken`), like the full map's box.
- Pois' completed look reads `Token::completed` on their entities, which exist only near the player, and nothing marks them completed yet. It should move to the server's per-player poi state once something does.
- `dungeon_map` adds to `remembered` without marking it changed, so maps redraw at least every `REDRAW_SECONDS`.
