# client: planet_view

View Planet: the whole world as a turning globe, a day a minute, with drifting clouds and its moons going round it, over everything until a click, tap or Esc. A bar along the top chooses the surface generator, the seed (any planet's, typed or random) and the layer shown, so generators can be compared without changing the game. A debug view for now (the debug menu's View Planet), planned as the loading screen for travelling between planets. Only for looks: nothing here is a rule, and the server knows nothing of it.

## Files

- `crates/client/src/planet_view/mod.rs`: `PlanetViewPlugin`, `ViewPlanet`, `PLANET_LAYER` (8), `HOURS_PER_SECOND` (a day a minute).
- `open.rs`: `open` (on `ViewPlanet`, if there is a `CurrentWorld`): the black UI layer (`widgets::layer_z(50)`, above the menus; `leaves_presses`) with the picture as large as fits, the bar and a note, and the globe for the current world.
- `showing.rs`: `PlanetView` (resource: `showing`), `Showing` (layer, picture, bar, `Choice`, `Globe`, the snow line, the hour and real time on opening, `typing`), `Choice` (seed, generator, layer; `of(world)`: the world as it is), `Globe` (what it was `built` for and its `layer`, the `Planet`, scene, clouds, sky, texture, `relief` and the `heights` it comes from, size, `Fill`), `SceneAssets`, `build_globe`.
- `planet.rs`: `Planet` (params, surface by the chosen generator, climate, vegetation, its `WorldGates`, and its own suns and moons unless it is the current world), `celestial`, `climate_at` (`sample_metres` for generators in metres), `shown` (oceans deeper than `Classic`'s squeezed into the world map's water shading, or they'd be black), `has_sea_ice` (for surfaces in metres, sea whose climate is below `climate::SEA_ICE`; drawn pale).
- `bar.rs`: `Bar`, `spawn_bar` (a panel: Generator dropdown, Seed box (digits only), new seed button (`widgets::spawn_new_seed_button`), copy button (`widgets::spawn_copy_button`), Reset, Layer dropdown, the line on the World Gates; `stops_presses`). `read_bar.rs`: `read_bar` (another seed or generator builds the globe again; another layer refills the picture; the new seed button picks any `u64` from the time; the copy button (native only, as the clipboard is) puts the seed on the clipboard; Reset goes back to `Choice::of`, keeping the layer).
- `relief.rs`: `new_relief_texture` (linear `Rgba8Unorm`, level), `relief_row` (slopes from the heights to a normal map, `RELIEF` 20 times steeper than real, east-west spacing narrowing towards the poles; alpha the height, full at 9 km).
- `gates.rs`: `stamp_gates` (a ring round each World Gate, drawn into the picture once the colours are done), `gate_words` (the bar's line: "16 World Gates, round 2, 300 tries"), `show_gate_words`.
- `layer.rs`: `Layer` (`Surface`, `Elevation`, `Plates`, `Temperature`, `Moisture`), `temperature_tint` (blue cold to red hot, pale near freezing), `moisture_tint` (brown dry to blue wet), `elevation_tint` (map tints by metres), `plate_tint` (a colour a plate, brighter if continental; edges red closing, blue parting, white sliding; grey without plates). `colour.rs`: `Colouring`, `colour_at` (the colour and the height in metres, the sea flat).
- `debug_api.rs` (feature only): `client.planet {generator?, seed?, layer?, path?}` (generators and layers by name, as the bar has them): changes the choice (the bar's widgets don't follow) and saves the picture as a PNG; answers `filled`, `seed`, `world_gates` (`x`, `y`, `round`) and `gate_tries`.
- `globe.rs`: `Scene`, `spawn_scene`: the globe (Bevy's UV sphere, finer and with tangents, tipped so north is +Y; the picture and the relief as its normal map), a `DirectionalLight` per sun (colour, `SUN_LUX` × strength), the moons (size × `MOON_RADIUS`, textured, tinted by colour), and a camera drawing into the picture (order -50, black, `Tonemapping::None`, a little ambient), far enough back for the furthest moon.
- `sky.rs`: the maths, tested alone: `equator`, `from_ground` (`[east, up, south]` at a point of the equator to a direction round the globe), `Sky` (`ground_angle`, `globe_turn`, `sun_direction`, `moon_place`), `moon_distance`.
- `texture.rs`: `Fill`, `texture_size`, `ground_at`, `new_texture`, `pass_columns`, `fill_texture` (coarse first: passes of 8, 4, 2 and 1 pixel blocks, each working out only what the last didn't, keeping each pixel's height; then the relief a row at a time; all within `FRAME_BUDGET`, 6 ms a frame).
- `spin.rs`: `spin`: the view's own clock, then the globe, lights and moons.
- `moon_look.rs`: `MoonLook` (`of(terrain seed, moon number)`: style, own axis and turn, `turn`, `image`: 128×64 greys); in `moon_look/`: `pattern.rs` (cratered, banded, cracked ice, patchy, from value noise on the sphere).
- `clouds.rs`: `PlanetCloudMaterial` (`CloudParams`: the seed's start in the noise and the hours, towards the main sun; the relief, for its heights), `spawn_clouds` (a shell at `CLOUD_HEIGHT` 1.006, a child of the globe, tipped as it is), `move_clouds` (each frame, after `spin`). Its shader: `crates/client/assets/shaders/planet_clouds.wesl`.
- `input.rs`: `take_input` (`PreUpdate`, after `PointerSystems`, before `WidgetSystems::Presses`); `close.rs`: `close`, `close_without_world`.

## Notes

- Ids (`widgets::UiId`): the bar's widgets are `planet/generator`, `seed`, `new_seed`, `copy`, `reset`, `layer`; `client.ui.set` on them works as the bar does.

- The picture is equirectangular: columns are `x` round the world, rows `y` from `-width / 4` to `width / 4`, the climate's poles (the world goes on past them; the globe can't show it). At most 1024 × 512; a smaller world gets a pixel a grid point. Surface: each pixel is `SurfaceStyle::ground_colour` at mid-tide (`sea_level`) and the snow line on opening, darker under trees. Elevation: tints by `Surface::metres`. From `BROAD_FROM` (4) grid points a pixel only `Surface::broad_height` is used, as finer layers only speckled it. The whole globe shows coarsely at once and sharpens as it turns (a few seconds to the last pass on a debug build, longer for `Continents`). No threads (owner, 2026-11-04: not unless they clearly help).
- What it shows of the generator (owner's reason for it): the noise is sampled on a cylinder, so near the poles features squeeze into a pinch; and its largest layer is about 256 grid units, small next to an Earth-sized world, so there is nothing continent-sized. `Continents` is the answer to that (see `shared/surface.md`).
- The sky: the main sun stays put about 60° right of the camera, and the globe turns under it, towards the east if suns rise in the east, with `x = 0` facing it at noon (no time zones, so that is where the clock is true). Other suns are where they are seen from `x = 0`. Moons' orbits ignore the planet's turning, so they stay put in space (`from_ground` at the noon angle); distances follow Kepler (period to the 2/3) squeezed into 1.7 to 4 radii. Moon phases come from the lights, not `BodyNow::lit`.
- While open it takes all input (`take_input`, after `WidgetSystems::Presses`, before `Front`), so Esc never reaches the system menu and clicks never reach the map; the bar's widgets get theirs first, and an open dropdown or the seed box its keys. Presses in the first 0.25 s are ignored, so the debug menu's own press can't close it; the click or Esc that takes the keyboard from the seed box only does that. It closes by itself if the world goes (the connection lost).
- Clouds are for looks only, not `shared::weather` (its detail would blur): soft blobs from 3D value noise on the sphere, worked out in the shader from the shell's own longitude and latitude (its UVs), so nothing is drawn on the CPU. They change two ways: the noise is moved through with the hours (`CHANGE`), and bands drift east or west by latitude (`-cos(4 × latitude)`, trade winds, westerlies, polar easterlies) at `DRIFT`, shearing them into streaks. The drift runs in cycles of `CYCLE_HOURS` (24, a minute in the view; the owner saw the twist build up without end): two copies half a cycle apart, each starting again from fresh noise while unseen, fade into each other (spread again so the blend isn't thinner), so the twist stays within `DRIFT × CYCLE_HOURS`. Highlands (from about 2 km, full by about 6 km) gather more cloud, and land just downwind of them (about 5° upwind is looked at, by the bands' wind) has less: a rain shadow. Only highlands, or `Classic`, whose land is high everywhere at 100 m a unit, would be all cloud.
- Relief: once the colours are done, slopes from the heights become a normal map, so the suns pick out ranges and the day-night line shows them; the globe stays round. The sea is flat, and before the relief pass the globe is level. More cloud at the equator and middle latitudes, less about 30° out and at the poles (`cos(6 × latitude)`). Lit by the main sun only, dimmer and thinner on the night side; no shadows on the ground. The noise's hash is integer maths: a `sin` hash isn't exact on graphics cards and broke the clouds along lines of the noise's grid.
