# client: player

The player's position on the current view's grid (world map or dungeon): keyboard steps and travelling to a clicked point.

## Files

- `crates/client/src/player/mod.rs`: `PlayerPlugin`; spawns the player (with a default `Token`: white circle, `Icon` `monsters/humanarcher.png`, and a white `LightSource` of `LIGHT_RADIUS` 6 cells), `move_player` (WASD / number pad, or the movement pad's arrows held (`common::PadSteps`): a `TakeStep` while held, only when none is waiting, so steps never pile up and walking stops when the key comes up (the first step of a hold is always taken; once it has been, letting go drops the one waiting, so a click or tap lasting a few frames is one step), as energy sets the pace; directions are screen directions, turned to grid steps with `common::ViewFacing`), `take_steps` (in `Travel`, before `travel`: keeps up to `MOST_STEPS_WAITING` `TakeStep`s and takes them in order as the player's energy allows (`PlayerEnergy`, by `common::PlayerSpeed`), in the dungeon view only onto walkable cells or over liquid to one (`shared::movement::step_on_level`), on the world map not into water (`step_on_world_map`), costing `world_step_time` by `common::PlayerGait` (steps by `step_on_level_by` / `step_on_world_map_by` with `PlayerGait`, held to a sneaking pace on levels while `common::Sneaking` (`energy::sneak_step_energy`, travel too), so a good swimmer swims deep water and the coast; on a level `energy::level_step_energy`, by `common::WildCellHeights` in the wilderness and wading onto `Shallows`; on the world map wading where `common::NearRivers` has a river), announcing each as `Stepped`), `level_costs.rs` (`LevelCosts`: a level step's `cost` for routes and `time` in energy, slopes, wading, terrain (`footing_on` with `WildCellHeights::terrain`), jumps and a sneaking pace, for both `take_steps` and `travel`), `keep_river_points` (`rivers.rs`, first in `Travel`: on the world map, `NearRivers` traced round the player, started again for a new world), `keep_wild_heights` (`wild_heights.rs`, beside it: `WildCellHeights`: the land made again whenever the wilderness's window moves, with its `reference`; its covered cells levelled again (`WildHeights::relevel`) when `CurrentDungeon::flats` change or, with flats about, when its cells do, marked changed only if a height did), `travel` (travel along a found route (`find_world_path` by `PlayerGait`; on a level `find_path_by` and `straighten_by` with the wilderness's slopes), at constant speed except that slopes (world map and wilderness) are slower, roads and Running faster, by what they take (`world_step_time`, `CurrentWorld::roads` steps); announcing each grid point it moves to as `Stepped` (several if a frame covers more), sending `Arrived` at the end, and showing jumps, `TRAVEL_SPEED` 60 units/s on the world map, `DUNGEON_TRAVEL_SPEED` 4 cells/s in the dungeon (`shared::energy::SITE_CELLS_PER_SECOND`), both scaled by `common::PlayerSpeed`, spending energy for each step). Beside it: `messages.rs` (`Player`, `PlayerSystems`, `TravelTo`, `Arrived`, `TakeStep`, `Stepped`), `moving.rs` (`PlayerMoving`, `note_moving`), `state.rs` (`Travel::door_asked`: a door asked open and not yet answered), `keys.rs`, `steps.rs` (a step into a closed door asks it open instead), `travel.rs` (waits at a closed door on the route), `route_help.rs` (`dungeon_route`: on a level, through closed doors as if open, a step dearer each; `towards`, `enemy_cells`, `nearest_cell`), `doors.rs` (`AskDoor`, sent as `ClientMessage::Door` by `send_door_asks`, which spends `door::door_cost` as `SpentEnergy`; `wait_at_door` (asked once; a journey to the door itself ends there); `door_answers` (`DoorRefused` ends a journey waiting there, and one found locked is marked on `CurrentDungeon::level`, which `dungeon_route` goes through only if there is no other way, `LOCKED_COST`); `close_doors`: `Action::CloseDoors`, L).

## Public API

- `Player` (component): marks the single player entity.
- `TravelTo { x, y }` (message): travel to a grid point by the quickest route, or to the nearest point it can reach: in the dungeon through cells the player has seen, never through a World Gate's cell unless it is the goal, on the world map round water and along roads where they are quicker. A new one replaces the current journey from wherever the player is.
- `Arrived { x, y }` (message): a journey (`TravelTo`) has ended there, where it was asked to go. Not sent for keyboard steps or other direct moves, nor when it stopped short (so clicking a wall beside stairs doesn't take them). Used by `site` to enter pois, take stairs and leave by outside ground.
- `TakeStep(Step)` (message): ask for a step (keyboard, auto walk, dev tools).
- `Stepped { step, moved }` (message): the player took a step (by `TakeStep` or travelling), which moved them by `moved` (two cells for a jump); `connection` sends it to the server. Not sent when the position is set from elsewhere (the server's word: arriving on a level, corrections).
- `PlayerMoving` (resource): `is_moving()` while a journey has way to go (or is stopped on the way), a jump is landing, steps wait for energy, or a movement key is held; set by `move_player` and `moving::note_moving` (last in `Travel`). `abilities` holds automatic abilities back meanwhile.
- `PlayerSystems` (system set): `Input` then `Travel`, chained. Systems that send `TakeStep` or `TravelTo` go in `Input`; systems drawing the position or reading `Stepped` run `.after(PlayerSystems::Travel)`.

## Depends on

- `shared::movement`, `shared::pathfinding`; `common` (`Token`, `Icon`, `LightSource`, `ViewFacing`, `PadSteps`, `GameView`, `CurrentDungeon`, `CurrentWorld`, `MapPosition`, `SmoothPosition`: the player's position components, in `common` because other entities (world map pois) have them too).

## Used by

- `abilities` (`PlayerMoving`, `TakeStep`, `TravelTo`), `connection` (`Stepped`; sets the position on joining and on corrections), `site` (`Arrived`; sets the position on entering and changing levels), `view3d` (the camera follows the `Player`), `world_map` (centre of view, clicks), `dungeon_map` (sight and light), `ui` (position readout), `debug_menu` (auto walk: `TakeStep`), `dev_tools` (`HOW_DEBUG_MOVE`: `TakeStep`; `HOW_DEBUG_TRAVEL`), all with `common::MapPosition` / `SmoothPosition` on the `Player`.

## Notes

- Takes no steps while `common::MakingCharacter` is on or `common::CharacterSelect` is open (those screens are up): the server would ignore them.

- Any other change to `MapPosition` (a step, the server putting the player somewhere) cancels travel and jumps there: `travel` notices because `MapPosition` differs from the value it last set (private `Travel` component).
- `move_player` and `travel` don't run while `GameView::Connecting` (there is no world yet), nor while `common::ServerStatus` isn't `Connected`.
- Keys count if "pressed" **or** "just pressed": a tap released within one frame is otherwise lost.
- Movement is predicted: steps are shown at once and the server checks them again (`connection` moves the player back if it refused one). Unknown cells count as wall here, so the client is stricter than the server. World map routes use the same rules as the server (`CurrentWorld::ground`, public terrain, the water at `GameClock`'s hour), so they aren't limited to what has been seen. If the tide cuts a route, a new one is found from there to the same goal (private `Travel::goal`). In the dungeon a route uses only `CurrentDungeon::remembered` cells, so it gives nothing away, and each step is checked again when taken: if the cells have changed, the journey ends there. The route isn't looked at again as more of the level is seen.
- Jumps over liquid: `MapPosition` goes straight to the cell landed on and `SmoothPosition` glides there at travel speed. Until the jump is over, steps are ignored and a new `TravelTo` waits. A journey jumps on leaving the cell before the liquid and carries on from the edge of the landing cell; a keyboard jump ends on the landing cell's centre.
- Systems use `Single<..., With<Player>>`, assuming one player entity. Multiplayer will need local vs remote players.
- A new native window takes keyboard focus, so typing elsewhere at that moment can move the player.
- Enemies (`common::Other` that is `Unfriendly`) can't be stepped onto and routes go round them, finding a new way if one comes between. Anyone else swaps places, which the server tells of as their move. With `common::GodMode` on, enemies swap too and routes go straight through them.
- `SpentEnergy` (common) spends energy on what isn't a step, and stops a journey for as long as that takes (`PlayerEnergy::stopped_for`).
- `CancelledEnergy` (common) gives back the energy owed beyond its penalty, and ends a journey's pause: an ability called off (`abilities`).
