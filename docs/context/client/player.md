# client: player

The player's position: on the world map a grid point, stepped and travelled cell to cell; on levels and in the Hall an exact `Spot` in units (`common::SpotPosition`), walked freely at any angle and travelled along routes at any angle (free positions PR 4).

## Files

- `crates/client/src/player/mod.rs`: `PlayerPlugin`; spawns the player (with a default `Token`: white circle, its base sized by `common::PlayerSize` (`state::size_token`), `Icon` `monsters/humanarcher.png`, and a white `LightSource` of `LIGHT_RADIUS` 6 cells), `move_player` (`keys.rs`: WASD / number pad, or the movement pad's arrows held (`common::PadSteps`): on a level or in the Hall a `Walking` direction every frame held, the screen direction turned by `common::ViewTurn` (any angle); on the world map a `TakeStep` while held, only when none is waiting, so steps never pile up and walking stops when the key comes up (the first step of a hold is always taken; once it has been, letting go drops the one waiting, so a click or tap lasting a few frames is one step), as energy sets the pace, turned to grid steps with `common::ViewFacing`), `take_steps` (`steps.rs`, in `Travel`, before `travel`: keeps up to `MOST_STEPS_WAITING` `TakeStep`s and takes them in order as the player's energy allows (`PlayerEnergy`, by `common::PlayerSpeed`), on a level as one `Moved` to the next cell's middle (`level_step.rs`: from a middle by the cell rule, `step_on_level_by`, so over liquid and swimming as before; from elsewhere a straight walk there by `check_segment`), shown gliding there (`Travel::landing`), on the world map not into water (`step_on_world_map`), costing `world_step_time` by `common::PlayerGait` (steps by `step_on_level_by` / `step_on_world_map_by` with `PlayerGait`, held to a sneaking pace on levels while `common::Sneaking` (`energy::sneak_step_energy`, travel too), so a good swimmer swims deep water and the coast; on a level `energy::level_step_energy`, by `common::WildCellHeights` in the wilderness and wading onto `Shallows`; on the world map wading where `common::NearRivers` has a river), announcing each as `Stepped`), `level_costs.rs` (`LevelCosts`: a level step's `cost` and `time` in energy, slopes, wading, terrain (`footing_on` with `WildCellHeights::terrain`), jumps and a sneaking pace; `move_time(from, to)` for a move in units as the server prices it (within a cell by length, into another as that cell step in proportion), `slowness` for pacing), `keep_river_points` (`rivers.rs`, first in `Travel`: on the world map, `NearRivers` traced round the player, started again for a new world), `keep_wild_heights` (`wild_heights.rs`, beside it: `WildCellHeights`: the land made again whenever the wilderness's window moves, with its `reference`; and `keep_drawn_ground` (`drawn_ground.rs`, after it: `DrawnGround` from the level's `LevelInfo::ground`, the wilderness's from those heights, a site's first level's land sampled once and levelled again as cells come, a cave's swell; marked changed only when it changed); its covered cells levelled again (`WildHeights::relevel`) when `CurrentDungeon::flats` change or, with flats about, when its cells do, marked changed only if a height did), `travel` (`travel.rs`, world map only: travel along a found route (`find_world_path` by `PlayerGait`), at constant speed except that slopes are slower, roads and Running faster, by what they take (`world_step_time`, `CurrentWorld::roads` steps); announcing each grid point it moves to as `Stepped` (several if a frame covers more), sending `Arrived` at the end, `TRAVEL_SPEED` 60 units/s scaled by `common::PlayerSpeed`, spending energy for each step). `travel_on_level` (`level_travel.rs`, after it, levels and the Hall only): any other change to `SpotPosition` resets it; a move being shown (`landing`: a jump or a step asked for) glides first; `Walking` walks the stride (`stride.rs`) that way, a jump if stuck at liquid or a hole, a door asked open if stuck at one (`level_moves.rs`: `door_ahead`, `jump_ahead`); a `TravelTo` finds a route (`level_route.rs`: `find_sized_path` for the player's size (`common::PlayerSize`) on the level with doors open, through remembered cells, round enemies, never through a World Gate but the goal, locked doors only if there is no other way) followed by `follow_route` (jumps whole, waits at closed doors, finds a new way once when blocked, then gives up), `Arrived` at the end; every finished stretch is a `Moved`, its energy spent (`move_time`, slower for squeezing through a gap or past someone, as the server charges it); paced at `DUNGEON_TRAVEL_SPEED` (4 cells/s, `SITE_CELLS_PER_SECOND`) scaled by speed and `slowness`, held back while the energy is `AHEAD` (0.25 s) behind. `stride.rs`: `Stride` (the straight stretch walked since the last move sent: `walk` in pieces of 40 units, ending a move on turning, entering another cell, a cell's length or stopping, sliding by `clearance::slide`, else closer by halves or along an axis; `exactly` stops on a route's point without sliding), `Walker` (level, size, who else is about (`route_help::crowd`: `clearance::Other`s from the others' `SpotPosition`s; enemies block, other players are walked through, the rest squeezed past; Beside it: `messages.rs` (`Player`, `PlayerSystems`, `TravelTo`, `Arrived`, `TakeStep`, `Stepped`, `Walking`, `Moved`), `moving.rs` (`PlayerMoving`, `note_moving`), `state.rs` (`Travel`: the world map's route and jump; on levels `spots`, `last_spot`, `landing`, `stride`, `asked`, `rerouted`; `door_asked`: a door asked open and not yet answered), `keys.rs`, `steps.rs` (a step into a closed door asks it open instead), `travel.rs` (waits at a closed door on the route), `route_help.rs` (`towards`, `enemy_cells`, `nearest_cell`), `doors.rs` (`AskDoor`, sent as `ClientMessage::Door` by `send_door_asks`, which spends `door::door_cost` as `SpentEnergy`; `wait_at_door` (asked once; a journey to the door itself ends there); `door_answers` (`DoorRefused` ends a journey waiting there, and one found locked is marked on `CurrentDungeon::level`, which `dungeon_route` goes through only if there is no other way, `LOCKED_COST`); `close_doors`: `Action::CloseDoors`, L).

## Public API

- `Player` (component): marks the single player entity.
- `TravelTo { x, y }` (message): travel to a grid point by the quickest route, or to the nearest point it can reach: in the dungeon through cells the player has seen, never through a World Gate's cell unless it is the goal, on the world map round water and along roads where they are quicker. A new one replaces the current journey from wherever the player is.
- `Arrived { x, y }` (message): a journey (`TravelTo`) has ended there, where it was asked to go. Not sent for keyboard steps or other direct moves, nor when it stopped short (so clicking a wall beside stairs doesn't take them). Used by `site` to enter pois, take stairs and leave by outside ground.
- `TakeStep(Step)` (message): ask for a step (world map keys, auto walk, dev tools; on a level one cell's move).
- `Walking { x, y }` (message): the player walks freely this way this frame on a level or in the Hall (keys or pad held; a unit grid direction). `abilities`, `ground` and `trade` take it, like `TakeStep`, as the player taking over.
- `Stepped { step, moved, to }` (message): a world map step (by `TakeStep` or travelling); `connection` sends it to the server. Not sent when the position is set from elsewhere.
- `Moved { from, to }` (message): a straight move on a level or in the Hall, in units; `connection` sends it as `ClientMessage::Move`. Not sent when the position is set from elsewhere (the server's word: arriving on a level, corrections).
- `PlayerMoving` (resource): `is_moving()` while a journey has way to go (or is stopped on the way), a jump is landing, steps wait for energy, or a movement key is held; set by `move_player` and `moving::note_moving` (last in `Travel`). `abilities` holds automatic abilities back meanwhile.
- `PlayerSystems` (system set): `Input` then `Travel`, chained. Systems that send `TakeStep` or `TravelTo` go in `Input`; systems drawing the position or reading `Stepped` run `.after(PlayerSystems::Travel)`.

## Depends on

- `shared::movement`, `shared::pathfinding` (`find_world_path`, `find_sized_path`), `shared::clearance` (`check_segment`, `check_segment_by`, `slide_by`, `RoomMap`), `shared::spot`; `common` (`SpotPosition`, `ViewTurn`, `Token`, `Icon`, `LightSource`, `ViewFacing`, `PadSteps`, `GameView`, `CurrentDungeon`, `CurrentWorld`, `MapPosition`, `SmoothPosition`: the player's position components, in `common` because other entities (world map pois) have them too).

## Used by

- `abilities` (`PlayerMoving`, `TakeStep`, `Walking`, `TravelTo`), `ground` and `trade` (`TakeStep`, `Walking`, `TravelTo`, `Arrived`), `connection` (`Stepped`, `Moved`; sets the position on joining and on corrections), `site` (`Arrived`; sets the position on entering and changing levels), `view3d` (the camera follows the `Player`), `world_map` (centre of view, clicks), `dungeon_map` (sight and light), `ui` (position readout), `debug_menu` (auto walk: `TakeStep`), `dev_tools` (`HOW_DEBUG_MOVE`: `TakeStep`; `HOW_DEBUG_TRAVEL`), all with `common::MapPosition` / `SmoothPosition` on the `Player`.

## Notes

- Takes no steps while `common::MakingCharacter` is on or `common::CharacterSelect` is open (those screens are up): the server would ignore them.

- Any other change to `MapPosition` (a step, the server putting the player somewhere) cancels travel and jumps there: `travel` notices because `MapPosition` differs from the value it last set (private `Travel` component); on levels `travel_on_level` does the same with `SpotPosition`, which whoever places the player sets with `MapPosition` and `SmoothPosition` (`SpotPosition::place`).
- On levels the player may be shown ahead of what was sent: the stretch walked since the last `Moved` is sent when it turns, crosses into another cell, reaches a cell's length or stops (`connection` corrects against the last move sent, not the place shown).
- `move_player` and `travel` don't run while `GameView::Connecting` (there is no world yet), nor while `common::ServerStatus` isn't `Connected`.
- Keys count if "pressed" **or** "just pressed": a tap released within one frame is otherwise lost.
- Movement is predicted: moves are shown at once and the server checks them again by the same rule (`connection` moves the player back if it refused one). Unknown cells count as wall here, so the client is stricter than the server. World map routes use the same rules as the server (`CurrentWorld::ground`, public terrain, the water at `GameClock`'s hour), so they aren't limited to what has been seen. If the tide cuts a route, a new one is found from there to the same goal (private `Travel::goal`). On a level a route uses only `CurrentDungeon::remembered` cells, so it gives nothing away, and each piece is checked again when walked. The route isn't looked at again as more of the level is seen. Level routes go by distance (and squeezes) only: the wilderness's slopes don't steer them any more, though they still slow the walk and cost energy by cells' heights.
- Jumps: `SpotPosition` goes straight to the spot landed on and `SmoothPosition` glides there at travel speed (`Travel::landing`; the world map's `Travel::jump` is the same for points). Until the jump is over, steps and walking are ignored and a new `TravelTo` waits.
- Good swimmers swim deep water only by steps between cells' middles (the cell rule): free walking and level routes don't swim yet.
- Systems use `Single<..., With<Player>>`, assuming one player entity. Multiplayer will need local vs remote players.
- A new native window takes keyboard focus, so typing elsewhere at that moment can move the player.
- Enemies (`common::Other` that is `Unfriendly`) can't be walked into (their cell) and routes go round them, finding a new way if one comes between. Anyone else swaps places, which the server tells of as their move. With `common::GodMode` on, enemies swap too and routes go straight through them.
- `SpentEnergy` (common) spends energy on what isn't a step, and stops a journey for as long as that takes (`PlayerEnergy::stopped_for`).
- `CancelledEnergy` (common) gives back the energy owed beyond its penalty, and ends a journey's pause: an ability called off (`abilities`).
