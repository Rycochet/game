# shared: mob

Who is on a site level to begin with, from the site's secret seed, and what a player is told of anything on the map. Mobs themselves are kept by the server (`server::entities`).

## Files

- `crates/shared/src/mob.rs`: `Spawn`, `spawns`, `EntityLook`. Tests. In `mob/`: `memory.rs` (`Memory`), `scent.rs` (how strong a trail is, how long a nose follows it), `wild.rs` (the wilderness's mobs; tests in `wild_tests.rs`), `trail.rs` (where they have wandered lately; tests in `trail_tests.rs`).

## Public API

- `spawns(seed, index, site_kind, level, stairs) -> Vec<Spawn>`: the level's mobs, each with its `number`, kind, position, appearance (fitted to its gender: no beard on a female), `gender` (`Gender::rolled` from its own hash, `GENDER_ROLL`: 45% Male, 45% Female, 10% Neutral; `own(61)` in the wilderness), `alignment` (`Alignment::rolled` from its own hash, `ALIGNMENT_ROLL`, `own(62)` in the wilderness: about its kind's leaning, `None` for animals; secret, never in `EntityLook`), `attributes` (a build rolled near the centre, `sheet::Build::rolled`, plus its kind's and gender's bonuses) `gear` (kinds and qualities, rolled by `item::roll_drop`) and `coins` (its money in copper, one roll of its own in `mob/purse.rs`: a chance and a range per kind, first guesses; the server gives it as the fewest coins).
- `who_lives_in(site_kind) -> &[(CreatureKind, weight)]`: who can be found in a kind of place, how many of each in 100 (what `spawns` rolls from; `shared::quest` picks the kinds quests ask to be slain from it).
- `chosen_spawns(seed, kind, count, level, clear_of) -> Vec<Spawn>` (`mob/chosen.rs`): developer tools' mobs of one kind (a Dev Area), on any walkable cell at least `FROM_STAIRS` from each of `clear_of`, one to a cell, rolled as a level's own; fewer if no room is found in 64 tries each.
- `wild_spawns(seed, block, level, biome) -> Vec<Spawn>`: a wilderness block's creatures from the secret seed and its position alone: nothing in 45% of blocks (and always in desert), else a pack or herd of 1 to 3 of one kind round a den on open ground (`Ground` or `Grass`): wolves in forests, tundra and taiga; horses and cattle (with a few wolves) in grassland and savanna; a mix in woodland. Each number is a hash, its own across the wilderness. `wild_mob_id(seed, number)`: their ids (UUID v5 of a hash), always the same, so nothing needs saving for one nobody has touched.
- `wild_trail(seed, number, home, level) -> Vec<cell>`: where a wild mob has wandered lately, `home` first: `TRAIL_SHORTEST` (24) to `TRAIL_LONGEST` (96) legal steps out (`movement::mob_step_on_level`, never onto a World Gate), keeping a heading that turns an eighth one step in four, going round what is in the way, stopping at the level's edge or where it can't go on.
- `Memory` (`set_standing` (dev tools: Create Mob's attitude), `remember_gift`, `remember_harm(who, harm, start)`, `remember_attacking(who)`, `remember_trespass`, `forget_old`, `standing(who, start)`, `opinion(who)`, `provoked(who)`), `Event` (`Gave` from older saves only, `Trespassed(tick)`, `Regard`), `Regard { standing, opinion, harmed, provoked }` (`provoked`: it attacked them, or was unfriendly to them (a trespass too), before their first blow, so killing it is self-defence and costs no alignment; false in older saves), `GIVE_REACH`, `GIFT_OPINION`, `FIRST_HARM_OPINION`, `TRESPASS_FORGOTTEN_AFTER` (3 minutes of action ticks): what a mob remembers about characters (owner, 2026-11-07). Two things per character, kept apart: its **standing** (`Feeling`: what it does and the token's colour; its kind's `feeling_about` until something changes it) and its **opinion** (a number in thousandths of a Health point, starting at 0, which never sets the standing by itself). A first blow lowers the standing a step (friendly to neutral with the opinion lowered by `max(harm, 10 points)`; neutral to unfriendly with the opinion set to minus that); any later blow makes it unfriendly and lowers the opinion by the harm. Gifts only raise the opinion (10 points). A trespass makes it unfriendly while remembered: only the latest per character, forgotten after that time. A regard is never forgotten or pushed out by the 32-thing limit; older saves' gifts are counted into the opinion when read.
- Scent: `scent_strength(inventory)` (percent: `BASE_SCENT` 100 carrying nothing, plus 4 per kg worn and 1 per kg carried, up to `MOST_SCENT` 200; weight stands in for metal), `smells_for(acuity)` (action ticks on the weakest trail: poor 30 s, normal 2 min, keen 5 min, none 0), `can_smell(acuity, strength, laid, now)` (the nose's time × strength), `SCENT_KEPT_FOR` (keenest × strongest: 10 min; older marks can go).
- `EntityLook { id, kind, gender, name, appearance, feeling, x, y, worn, boss }`: all a player learns of a mob or another player. `boss`: a quest boss's `BossLook { boss, tier }` (its story `Boss`, named on hover, and its tier, a `Quality` it glows in), `None` for anyone else. Never stats. `gender` is for words about it later (pronouns). `name` is another player's character's name (`None` for mobs, which go by their kind). `worn` is what can be seen of what it wears (`Inventory::shown`), for its picture; `heading` the way it faces (`facing::Heading`).

## Notes

- Worked out apart from the level's shape, so changing them moves no walls and needs no generator version. Only the server calls it: the seed is secret.
- Which kinds live where, and how many, are first guesses: a few beggars in towns, animals on farms (chickens, horses and cattle), mostly kobolds elsewhere, with goblins, orcs, a few chickens and wolves, the odd giant and some lizardfolk (kobolds start without boots, as they stand on their toes). A number stays with its mob, even if a place couldn't be found for an earlier one. What each kind may start with (`gear_of`) is too, and a blade is then made for what the mob can lift and suits its size, on the server, or left out if none can be; what is worn is made at the mob's size and put straight on, other things are `Medium` and go in its pockets, and a test checks that whoever can have something can wear or carry it; animals have nothing. Nobody starts within `FROM_STAIRS` (6) cells of stairs, so waiting for a reset can't put one beside the way in.
