# shared: creature

Kinds of creature, the same for players and later mobs, and wearing things by the shape of the body.

## Files

- `crates/shared/src/creature.rs`: `Layer`, `Location`, `Size`, `BodyPart`, `CreatureDef`, `CreatureKind` (the table: `def()`). In `creature/`: `layer.rs` (`Layer`, `Location`), `size.rs`, `def.rs` (`BodyPart`, `CreatureDef`), `guesses.rs` (first-guess attribute medians: `PERSON`, `medians`), `names.rs` (`CreatureKind::random_name(rng)`: a beginning, middle and ending from each people's sounds, others a human's; never a letter three times running, `three_alike` crate-wide for `shared::story`), `kind.rs` (feelings and reactions), `senses.rs` (`Temper`, `Senses`, each kind's from the research), `bodies.rs` (each kind's parts: `def()`), `peoples.rs` (the first peoples from the research: halfling, dwarf, elf, orc, goblin, lizardfolk, minotaur, birdfolk, and the bodies for feet that aren't a person's and wing-arms), `taurs.rs` (drider and scorpion man: a person's upper body taking what a person's does from the hips up, nothing below; not playable), `animals.rs` (cattle and wolf: what they are like; four legs, a head, neck and tail, as a horse's body).
- `crates/shared/src/sheet.rs`: `Ability` (re-exported from `shared::ability`), `Trait`, `Appearance` (`suits`, `choice`, `says`, `skin`, `feathers`, `hair`, `hair_colour`), `Choice { name, colour, pattern }`, `Pattern::{Plain, Speckled(colour)}` (for pictures of a colour: a hen's speckles are a base colour with specks), `Load`, `Sheet` (`new` (Neutral), `built(kind, gender, build)`, `gender` (`shared::gender`; Neutral in older saves, their attributes kept with no bonus added), `alignment` (`shared::alignment`, an `Option`: a player's always, 0 from `built` and for older saves in `settled`; secret), `as_sent` (a copy without the alignment: every `Sheet` the server sends), `settled`, `capacity`, `load`; `vitals`: Health and Mana, `shared::vitals`, full in older saves; `true_form`, `shapeshift`, `own_kind`). Tests. In `sheet/`: `attributes.rs` (`Row`, `Attribute`, `Attributes`: the nine), `build.rs` (`Build`, `Dot`: the three triangles, `points`, `rolled`, an integer cosine table), `from_build.rs` (`Attributes::for_kind`, `SPREAD`, `POINT_WEIGHT`), `kind_bonus.rs` (`bonus_from`, `BONUS_POINTS`), `foes.rs` (`Foes`, `Foe { fought, defeated }`: `Sheet::foes`, the 5-second blocks spent fighting each kind (for `skill::familiarity`) and kills helped with, `of`, `fought`, `defeated`; saved, empty in older saves), `shapeshift.rs` (`TrueForm { kind, body }`, `Sheet::shapeshift(current, form)`: the form's Body row from `for_kind(form, gender, build)`, so its gender's bonus stays, Mind and Spirit untouched, its own kind and Body row kept in `true_form` and given back on changing into its own kind; `own_kind`), `looks.rs` (`Choice` and `Trait::choices_for(kind)`: each kind's choices for a trait, with the colour each is drawn in, from the research), `hair.rs` (each kind's hair styles and beards, as `body::HairStyle` / `body::Beard` and as choices in words; `Appearance::hair`, `Appearance::hair_colour`).
- `crates/shared/src/wear.rs`: `Fit`, `WearRefused` (`worth_trying`; serialised, sent in `ServerMessage::NotWorn`), `Remover`, `takes_one_place`, what an item covers; on `Made`: `fit`. On `Inventory`, in `wear/`: `check.rs` (`is_worn`, `covered_by` (shown faded), `worn_over` (everything over some items, for costs), `check_wear`, what clashes), `changes.rs` (`wear`, `worn_fit`, `change_body`, `may_remove`, `hand_over`), `secrets.rs` (`favour_found_out`, `magic_found_out`, `hide_secrets`), `swap.rs` (`wear_swapping`, `wear_new_swapping`, `Swapped`, with the energy it took); costs on `Inventory` are in `item/inventory/cost.rs` (`changing_cost`, `taking_off_cost`), `death.rs` (`drop_on_death(difficulty)`: what dying drops, everything carried and worn on Hard and Ironman but what `kept_on_hard` keeps on Hard: worn on the Skin layer alone, not padded, not jewellery; `shared::difficulty`). Tests for every rule below.

## Public API

- `CreatureKind::{ALL, def()}` (ordered as listed, for keys), `CreatureDef { name, weight (grams), size, inventory, body, speed, uses_stairs, playable, attributes (medians), bonus_set, skills (paths in the skill tree and practice, `skill::level_practice`), abilities, looks }` (`bonus()`) (`part`, `fingers`). To add a kind: the enum, `ALL` (alphabetical), `def`, `temper`, `senses`.
- `CreatureKind::temper() -> Temper::{Friendly, Neutral, Timid, Territorial, Hostile}`; `CreatureKind::senses() -> Senses { sight, dark, hearing, smell }` (`Acuity::{None, Poor, Normal, Keen}`, `DarkSight::{None, Low, Dark}`), `Senses::sight_range()` in cells (1 without sight, 6, 12, 20).
- Attributes (plan: `docs/context/abilities-skills.md`): `Row::{Body, Mind, Spirit}` (`ALL`, `name`, `attributes`: power, finesse, resistance), `Attribute` (the nine, `ALL`, `name`, `row`), `Attributes` (`all`, `get`, `set`, `for_kind(kind, gender, &build)`). `Build { dots: [Dot; 3], turn }` (`CENTRED`, `is_valid`, `dot(row)`, `points`, `rolled(rng)`), `Dot([u16; 3])` (`CENTRE`, `is_valid`), `WEIGHT_TOTAL` 1000, `TURN_STEPS` 360, `POINTS_TOTAL` 4000, `SPREAD` 30, `POINT_WEIGHT` 25.
- `BodyPart { location, layers, places }`: which layers a location takes, and how many things taking one place fit there.
- `Layer::{Skin, Middle, Outer, Over}`, `Location`, `Size::{Tiny..Giant}` (`name`, `quarters`, `radius`, `stepped`, `apart`); `radius`: a footprint's in `spot` units (`quarters` × 20: Medium 80, 2/3 of a cell across; see `clearance.md`). Quarters are each band's middle beside a person (owner, 2026-11-04): Tiny 1 (under 0.75 m), Small 3 (to 1.5 m), Medium 4 (to 2.1 m), Large 6 (to 3.2 m), Huge 9 (to 4.5 m), Giant 12 (to 7 m); items' sides scale by them and worn things' and blades' weights by their cube.
- `CreatureKind::field_of_view()`: the cone's width in degrees (`facing::Field`): 120 for people, 180 to 350 for kinds the research gives a wide field of view and for animals (horses 350, cattle 330, chickens 300). First guesses (owner, 2026-11-12).

## Notes

- An item (`item::WearDef`) has one layer and a list of locations (and may be padded, below), less any its form leaves bare (`Item::worn_locations`: a sleeveless shirt, footless or stirrup hose). It fits if every location is on the body and takes that layer, so a T-shirt needs arms, which a chicken hasn't; trousers need `Legs`, which a horse hasn't.
- Going on: nothing on the same layer may be at any of its locations (`WearRefused::Clash`). Taking off is `Inventory::move_item`. What is on a higher layer at the same location (`covered_by`) never refuses either (owner, 2026-11-09: refusing was more annoying than useful in play): it only takes longer, `energy::covered_cost` of the weight of everything over it (`changing_cost`, counted once for several things changed together), on top of `dressing_cost` for each. Only a player's own changes cost time: mobs dressed when made, changes of body and debug gifts don't.
- Swapping (owner, 2026-11-07): a player putting something on uses `wear_swapping` (or `wear_new_swapping` from the ground), which takes off everything on its layer that clashes, one after another (a full set of rings or hands gives up its first), into the inventory with `give`, so one place can take over from something on several (fingerless gloves for gloves). On a copy, changing nothing when refused: other reasons first (body, size, fingers), then `Stuck` if it won't come off (`may_remove` as the wearer; `god` skips it), `NoRoom` if it has nowhere to go. Mobs dressing still use `check_wear_new`, which refuses `Clash`.
- `one_place` items (rings) take one of a location's places, and clash only once all are taken or something whole is on that layer.
- Padding (`WearDef::padded`): armour with its own padding also takes the Middle layer at those of its locations, for clashing and covering, so Full Armour won't go over a gambeson but goes over trousers. A body must take Middle there. Rules go by `Item::padded`, which is the kind's list, or a torso form's own lining (lined cuirasses, padded jacks, and an armour coat with a lined body), so it can differ between items of one kind.
- `Back` has two places, Outer only, for a backpack and a cloak (both `one_place`). Armour for the back is on `Torso`, so it never stops a pack. Bipeds' `Head` and `Neck` take Skin to Outer (arming caps, coifs) and `Waist` takes every layer: Skin (braies, loincloths, petticoats, which hang from it and so list it alone; owner, 2026-11-09), Middle (belts), Outer (faulds, tassets) and Over.
- `Held` (owner, 2026-11-07) is where weapons are held to fight with: two places, Outer only, one per hand, neither left nor right, on every body with hands (bipeds, centaurs, the taurs' upper bodies; birdfolk's wing-hands too). Blades (`item::HELD`) take one place, unless their form is two-handed (`blade::Hands::Two`), which takes both: `wear::takes_one_place` is the item's answer, which the clash rule goes by. Held weapons can be worn a size either way of their own. Horses, wolves, chickens and the like have no `Held` and fight unarmed. Which is the main hand is `combat::Wielded`'s (the bigger weapon).
- `Over` is cloth over armour: a biped's Shoulders, Arms, Torso, Waist and Legs (surcoat, tabard), and a horse's Head, Neck and Torso (caparison over barding). Nowhere else takes it. A piece that only half covers a location doesn't list it (a knee-length surcoat isn't on the legs).
- Rings are Skin on `Fingers`. Gloves and padded gloves are Middle on `Hands` and `Fingers`, gauntlets Outer on both, so both lock rings underneath; fingerless gloves and demi-gauntlets are on `Hands` only, so rings stay free. Rings are always against the skin (owner's decision). Gloves being Middle differs from the owner's first example, so that rings can stay on under them.
- Fit is on the item itself (`item::Made`): the size it fits, the smallest and largest that can wear it, and finger count. `Made::fit` answers `Exact`, `Loose(n)` or `None`; `WearDef::adjustable` things are exact anywhere in their range; magical things are exact within `Quality::magic_reach` sizes of their own (uncommon 1 up to artifact 5), going by their usual range beyond that, whatever the fingers, but not on any body. Things covering whole fingers need exactly the body's count, except mitten gauntlets, which fit any (owner's decision; `wear::made_for_fingers`). Looseness has no penalty until stats have something to affect.
- Seen: `Inventory::shown()` is what a picture draws of what is worn, innermost first, as `WornLook { form, quality, fingers }` (nothing secret): every armour and footwear form (`ItemForm::drawn_worn`) except what something else drawn covers at every place it is. A surcoat isn't drawn yet, so what it covers shows. The server tells others it with each mob and player (`EntityLook::worn`).
- Taking off: `may_remove(id, Remover)` says who may, by what lies on it: blessed things only their wearer, cursed things nobody, and anything not worn anyone. It is for whoever acts to ask, not `move_item` or `hand_over`: a body falling or a future ability doesn't go by it. God mode skips it for the player's own attempts (`server`).
- Magic gives itself away when it was needed to fit (`magic_found_out`, called only for players). A curse gives itself away when taking it off fails. Nothing reveals a blessing yet. A client doesn't know what is secret, so it lets a player try anything refused only for size or fingers (`worth_trying`), and the server decides.
- Changing kind (`change_body`, owner 2026-11-04): what the new body still takes stays on, checked with the wear rules in the order it was put on (so what is under goes back first); what it can't wear, cursed or not, is given back to drop, as is anything carried that the new grid has no room for. The bin is kept. Whoever calls it puts what comes back on the ground.
- A creature's size comes from its kind, apart from its appearance's height, which has no effect on rules.
- The creature is kept on `Inventory` for now, as that is all that depends on it.
- A `Sheet` is one creature's attributes, the `Build` they came from, skills and appearance, starting from its kind. Abilities come from the kind alone (`ability::known`), with Attack for everyone. Nothing uses attributes or skills yet but carrying: capacity is strength × (size + 1) × 100 g (a median 100 carries 30 kg at Medium), times 1 + Weight Training's value (twice as much at 100%, `movement::MOVEMENT_SKILLS`), burdened past half and overloaded past all of it, which cut speed (`Sheet::speed`) to 75% and 40%. Appearance never affects rules, and `suits` is how the server checks a choice. Choices are per kind (goblins' skins are greens and greys, a minotaur's coat browns) and saved by place, so new ones go at the end; a person's skin list kept its old order. `skin` is the body's colour (skin, or coat for fur), `feathers` its feathers'. New traits go at the end of `Trait` (`HairStyle`, `Beard`), as messages carry one by its place. Every kind with a hair colour has a hair style (a test checks); beards for humans, beggars, giants, centaurs, scorpion men, dwarves (a long beard unless chosen otherwise) and orcs, not elves, driders or halflings (owner's plan, 2026-11-06). `hair` gives `body::Hair::NONE`'s parts for a kind without the choice.
- A creature's body weight comes from its kind, and `Inventory::weight_with_creature` adds what it carries and wears. Load only counts what is carried: a human's body alone is over twice what it can carry.
- `feeling_about` (`sheet::Feeling`) and `reaction` (`sheet::Reaction`) say what a kind thinks of another and does about it, by its temper: a kind likes its own, hostile kinds (kobolds, goblins, orcs, minotaurs) dislike everyone else, timid ones (chickens, horses) keep away from everyone, friends least (how far is the server's), territorial ones (lizardfolk, wolves) are neutral until guarding comes, cattle are neutral, friendly ones (halflings) come over to strangers, and anyone attacks what it dislikes. How a mob stands with one character can change from its kind's feeling: `mob::Memory`.
- Attributes from a build (`for_kind`): each row's dot leans its three from the kind's median: a corner gives that one median × (1 + 2 × `SPREAD`%) and the other two × (1 − `SPREAD`%), the centre the median. The turn shares the 4 points (`points`, by an integer cosine of each row's angle: a third of a turn apart, Body at the top at 0): each point over 1 raises its row by `POINT_WEIGHT`%. The kind's bonus and the gender's (`shared::gender`: 2 on each of its row's three) go on top, rounded once at the end; never below 1. Only the dots, turn and gender are sent: the server works the rest out, and the bonuses are from the kind it already checks and the gender. Mobs roll a build near the centre (`rolled`) from their spawn's seed.
- Peoples (all playable kinds) share a person's medians (100 each) and differ only by their bonus (owner, 2026-11-06); other kinds have their own medians. Every kind's bonus adds up to the same `BONUS_POINTS` (54, to balance; owner, 2026-11-06): `bonus_set` lists only what a kind sets (0 counts as set), and the rest is shared evenly among the others, leftovers one each to the earliest in grid order (`bonus_from`); a test checks every kind. Set bonuses and medians are placeholders from the old four stats: the owner sets real ones later. Saves from before the nine have no attributes and load with a centred build (`Sheet::settled`, called on joining).
- Kinds, bodies, attributes and clothes are first guesses to try the rules out. Skills are the tree in `shared::skill` (`Sheet::skills`); abilities are `shared::ability`, growing towards the plan in `docs/context/abilities-skills.md`. The first peoples (`peoples.rs`) are all playable, as all stand in one cell; centaurs and giants aren't, as they don't (owner, 2026-11-04; characters already made as one stay so); their bodies' looks are `shared::body::Biped`.
- Armour fit by body (owner, 2026-11-04): feet that aren't a person's (kobold and lizardfolk on their toes, minotaur hooves, birdfolk talons) take only Skin, so boots, shoes and sabatons don't fit; birdfolk arms and hands are wings and take only Skin. A closed helmet (its `Face::closed`, alone or in a set) won't go over a snout, a beak or horns (`Parts::of(kind)`): refused as `WrongBody(Head)`.
- Temper and senses come from the research tables (`docs/context/research-creatures.md`). Its "varies" (humans, giants) is `Neutral` until creatures of one kind can differ; `Territorial` (lizardfolk) guards its home (server `entities/territory.rs`). Sight is how far mobs notice, smell how long they can follow a scent trail (`shared::mob::smells_for`); dark sight waits for light to be a shared rule, hearing for noise.
