# shared: armour

What all armour shares, and the pieces for each body location. Multi-location sets come after, as single items built from those pieces (see `decisions.md`, "Armour: sets of pieces").

## Files

- `crates/shared/src/armour/mod.rs`: `Construction`, `Style`, `in_style`, `STANDOFF_MM`.
- `crates/shared/src/armour/finish.rs`: `Dye` and `Finish`, the one colour-and-finish type every location shares (agreed between the location chats).
- `crates/shared/src/armour/arms/`: arm pieces. `mod.rs` (`ArmsForm`, small queries: `kote`, `padded`, `rigid`, `bazubands`, `manica`), `parts.rs` (`Reach` with its span down the arm, `Sides`, `Couter`, `Spikes`), `tables.rs` (constructions by style, reaches, materials), `roll.rs`, `grid.rs` (`allowed`), `names.rs`, `weight.rs` (cells and grams). Tests in `tests.rs`.
- `crates/shared/src/armour/legs/`: leg pieces. `mod.rs` (`LegsForm`, small queries: `japanese`, `rigid`, `coiled`, `footed`), `parts.rs` (`LegsKind`, `Cover` with its span up the leg, `Knee`, `Reinforce`, `Ornament`), `tables.rs` (constructions by style and kind, covers, wraps, knees), `materials.rs`, `roll.rs`, `grid.rs` (`allowed`), `names.rs`, `weight.rs` (cells and grams). Tests in `tests.rs`.
- `crates/shared/src/armour/waist/`: waist pieces. `mod.rs` (`WaistForm`, small queries: `japanese`, `rigid`, `rows`), `parts.rs` (`WaistKind`, `WaistPiece`, `Length` with its edges on the frame, `Opening`), `tables.rs` (constructions by style, shapes, lengths), `counts.rs` (lames, culets, hangs, openings), `extras.rs` (front-only, mail beneath, groin plate, cloth), `materials.rs`, `roll.rs`, `grid.rs` (`allowed`), `names.rs`, `weight.rs` (cells and grams). Tests in `tests.rs`.
- `crates/shared/src/armour/feet/`: foot pieces. `mod.rs` (`FeetForm`, small queries: `japanese`, `bare_toes`, `rigid`), `parts.rs` (`FeetPiece`, `Toe`, point lengths), `tables.rs` (constructions by style, pieces, toes, lames), `materials.rs`, `roll.rs`, `grid.rs` (`allowed`), `names.rs`, `weight.rs` (cells and grams). Tests in `tests.rs`.
- `crates/shared/src/armour/neck/`: neck pieces. `mod.rs` (`NeckForm`, small queries: `japanese`, `rigid`, `over_breastplate`), `parts.rs` (`NeckKind`, `Plates`, `Front`, `Hem`, `Edging`), `tables.rs` (constructions by kind and style, plates, collars, lames, fronts, capes, hems, edgings, points, spikes, studs), `materials.rs`, `roll.rs`, `grid.rs` (`allowed`), `names.rs`, `weight.rs` (cells and grams). ## Public API

- `Construction::{Padded, Leather, Mail, Scale, Lamellar, Laminar, PlatesInCloth, MailAndPlate, Splint, Plate, Mirror, RingMail, Woven}` (`ALL`, `flexible`). Ring mail (rings sewn on leather) is made only in European and fantasy styles; woven rattan in Chinese and fantasy ones.
- `Dye::{Natural, Red, Blue, Green, Black, Brown, White, Yellow, Purple}` (`ALL`, `name`, `roll`, `roll_paint`): cloth and lacing. `Finish::{Bright, Blackened, Russet, Blued, Tinned, Painted(Dye), Lacquered(Dye), Gilt, Etched}` (`rare_only`, `allowed(quality)`, `roll(rng, style, quality)`): metal, and lacquer on leather. Gilt and Etched only at Rare and better. A set passes both to its pieces. (Shoulders' own `shoulders::Finish` is leather's plain, tooled or studded, older than these.)
- `Style::{White, Gothic, Milanese, Maximilian, Greenwich, Kastenbrust, Elven, Dwarven, Japanese, Chinese, Steppe, Classical, Byzantine, IndoPersian}` (`ALL`, `name`, `roll`, `european`, `allows(construction)`): one list for every location, shared by a set's pieces; saved by name, new ones at the end. `in_style(list, style)`: the constructions in a list a style allows (the whole list if none).
- `hands::roll(rng, piece, style)`: a `HandsForm` for a `HandsPiece::{Gauntlets, Demi, Padded}` (the item kinds `Gauntlets`, `DemiGauntlets`, `PaddedGloves`); a set passes its style, a piece alone rolls one.
- `HandsForm { construction, fingers, cuff, knuckles, claws, locking, material, cloth, trim, style }`: `allowed(piece)`, `name`, `generic_name`, `specific_name`, `grams`, `grams_at(size)`. `Fingers::{Fingered, Mitten, Open}`, `Cuff::{Wrist, Hourglass, Bell, Pointed, Long}` (`reach(frame)`, `flare`), `Knuckles::{Plain, Ridged, Spiked, Rondel}`.
- `shoulders::roll(rng, kind, style)`: a `ShouldersForm` of a `ShouldersKind::{Pauldrons, Spaulders, Guards, Cape, Discs, Ailettes, Wing}`, or any kind (item kind `ShoulderArmour`).
- `ShouldersForm { piece, construction, adornment, finish, material, cloth, trim, style }`: `kind`, `allowed`, `name`, `generic_name`, `specific_name`, `shape` (cells), `grams`, `grams_at(size)`, `roman`, `dendra`, `japanese`. `ShouldersPiece::{Pauldrons { cut, wings, lames, upper_lames, haute_piece, gardbrace, besagews }, Spaulders { lames, besagews, pteruges }, Guards { size, shape, rows, lacing }, Cape { cut, length }, Discs { diameter, boss }, Ailettes { shape, size, heraldry }, Wing}`. `Cut`, `Wings`, `HautePiece` (`height`), `Span` (`guard`, `ailette`), `GuardShape`, `Lacing`, `CapeCut`, `Length`, `AiletteShape`, `Adornment::{None, Spikes(n), Horns, BeastHead, Skull}`, `Finish::{Plain, Tooled, Studded, RingMail}`.
- `torso::roll(rng, kind, style, quality)`: a `TorsoForm` for a `TorsoKind::{Armour, ArmingVest}` (item kinds `TorsoArmour`, `ArmingVest`).
- `TorsoForm { body, construction, style, material, back, over_mail, lined, vest, finish, cover, lacing, trim }`: `kind`, `allowed`, `padded` (takes Middle too: lined, or padding itself), `breast`, `sleeves`, `rigid`, `name`, `generic_name`, `specific_name`, `shape` (cells), `grams`, `grams_at(size)`. `TorsoBody::{Plate { breast, plackart, ridge, fluting, lance_rest }, Leather { breast, studded }, Mail { links, weave, rings, sleeves }, RingMail { sleeves }, Padded { quilting, sleeves }, Scale { shape, size, backing, sleeves }, Lamellar { cut, size, lacing }, Laminar { lames, riveted }, PlatesInCloth { size, rivets, leather }, MailAndPlate { rows, sleeves }, Mirror { mirrors }, Splint { rods }, Woven}`. `Rings::outside` and `PlateSize::height` (mm).
- `arms::roll(rng, style, quality)`: an `ArmsForm` (item kind `ArmArmour`).
- `ArmsForm { construction, style, reach, sides, couter, closed, spikes, studded, material, finish, cover, trim }`: `allowed`, `name` (a `String`: spikes, studs and an unusual material first), `generic_name`, `specific_name`, `shape` (cells), `grams`, `grams_at(size)`, `kote`, `padded`, `rigid`, `bazubands`, `manica`. `Reach::{Wrist, Forearm, Elbow, ForearmAndElbow, UpperAndElbow, Whole}` (`ALL`, `forearm`, `elbow`, `upper`, `span(frame)`: millimetres down the arm), `Sides::{Both, Left, Right}` (`sides`, `count`), `Couter::{None, Cup, Winged}`, `Spikes::{None, Elbow, Ridge}`.
- `legs::roll(rng, kind, style, quality)`: a `LegsForm` for a `LegsKind::{Armour, Chausses, Padded}` (item kinds `LegArmour`, `Chausses`, `PaddedChausses`).
- `LegsForm { kind, construction, style, cover, closed, knee, reinforce, sides, ornament, studded, material, finish, cloth, trim }`: `allowed`, `name` (a `String`), `generic_name`, `specific_name`, `shape` (cells), `grams`, `grams_at(size)`, `japanese`, `rigid`, `coiled`, `footed`. `Cover::{Knee, Shin, Thigh, ThighAndKnee, KneeAndShin, Whole, Ankle}` (`ALL`, `thigh`, `knee`, `shin`, `span(frame)`: heights from the ground), `Knee::{None, Cop, Winged, Tall}`, `Reinforce::{None, Poleyns, PoleynsAndSchynbalds, QuiltedThighs}`, `Ornament::{None, KneeSpikes, ShinFins}`; `sides` is `arms::Sides`.
- `STANDOFF_MM` (15): skin to the inside of any Outer armour, agreed by the locations.
- `waist::roll(rng, kind, style, quality)`: a `WaistForm` for a `WaistKind::{Armour, Cuirassier}` (item kinds `WaistArmour`, `CuirassierTassets`; cuirassier tassets roll a European or fantasy style if not given one that makes plate).
- `WaistForm { kind, construction, style, piece, length, lames, culet, hangs, front, opening, mail_beneath, groin, studded, material, finish, cloth, trim }`: `allowed`, `name` (a `String`: studs and an unusual material first), `generic_name`, `specific_name`, `shape` (cells), `grams`, `grams_at(size)`, `japanese`, `rigid`, `rows`. `WaistPiece::{Girdle, Fauld, Tassets, Panels, Pteruges, Skirt, Breeches}` (`ALL`), `Length::{Band, Hip, Thigh, Knee}` (`top(frame)`, `bottom(frame)`: heights from the ground), `Opening::{Closed, Sides, FrontAndBack}`.
- `feet::roll(rng, style, quality)`: a `FeetForm` (item kind `FootArmour`).
- `FeetForm { piece, construction, style, toe, point, detachable, width, lames, heel, high, fluted, toes, soled, studded, material, finish, cloth, trim }`: `allowed`, `name` (a `String`: studs and an unusual material first), `generic_name`, `specific_name`, `shape` (cells), `grams`, `grams_at(size)`, `japanese`, `bare_toes`, `rigid`. `FeetPiece::{Sabatons, MailShoes, FootGuards}` (`ALL`), `Toe::{Round, Pointed, DuckBill, BearPaw, CowMouth, Claw}` (`ALL`, `broad`, `widths`), `POINT_MM`, `DETACHABLE_POINT_MM`.
- `neck::roll(rng, kind, style, quality)`: a `NeckForm` for a `NeckKind::{Collar, Gorget, Bevor, Aventail, Mantle}` (item kinds `Collar`, `Gorget`, `Bevor`, `Aventail`, `Mantle`).
- `NeckForm { kind, construction, style, plates, high, lames, pointed, front, cape, hem, edging, spikes, studded, material, finish, cloth, trim }`: `allowed`, `name` (a `String`: spikes, studs and an unusual material first), `generic_name`, `specific_name`, `shape` (cells), `grams`, `grams_at(size)`, `japanese`, `rigid`, `over_breastplate`. `Plates::{Collar, Bib, Plates, Shoulders}`, `Front::{Open, Chin, Closed, Ventail}`, `Hem::{Straight, Vandyked, Scalloped}`, `Edging::{None, Brass, Patterned}`.
- `head::roll(rng, kind, style, quality)`: a `HeadForm` for a `HeadKind::{Cap, Coif, Helmet, BevoredHelm}` (item kinds `ArmingCap`, `Coif`, `Helmet`, `BevoredHelm`).
- `HeadForm { kind, construction, style, material, bowl, skull, face, cheeks, back, brim, top, ornament, studded, finish, cover, lacing }`: `allowed`, `name` (a `String`: ornament and unusual material first), `generic_name`, `specific_name`, `shape` (cells), `grams`, `grams_at(size)`, `helmet`, `tusks`, `laced`, `skull_cap`, `edge(frame) -> Edge { front, side, back }` (`Edge::height(degrees from the front)`), `rim(frame)`: 16 points round its lower edge in mm, from the front towards the left, for aventails to hang from. `OFF_HEAD`: shells stand 10 mm off the head. `Face::closed`, `Brim::round`, `Ornament::prefix`.
- `Heraldry { field, charge, colour }` (`allowed`, `roll`), `Tincture` (`metal`), `Charge`: a simple coat of arms, for ailettes now and shields and surcoats later.
- `hands::Hand::of(frame, side, digits)`: wrist, tip, length, palm, breadth, thickness, thumb, number of fingers, wrist radius, in millimetres. `Hand::digits(fingers)`: digits per hand from a body's finger count.

## Notes

- Every piece is built in millimetres on `body::Frame`, in place on the body, so a set and a character's picture can use it unchanged.
- A set's pieces share its construction, material, quality, size and style. A style narrows plate (owner's decision): Kastenbrust is mittens with long cuffs, Gothic pointed cuffs, Maximilian fingered with bells, Milanese mostly mittens, Greenwich bells, Dwarven never long. Each construction is made only in some styles (owner's decision; `Style::allows`): European harnesses in leather, mail, scale, plates in cloth, splint and plate; Japanese without scale or mirrors; Chinese, Steppe, Classical, Byzantine and Indo-Persian in their own; Elven and Dwarven in anything but mirrors; padding in any. So the style is rolled first, then a construction it allows.
- Weight grows with the square of size (owner's decision): area grows, thickness stays (`grams_at`).
- Hands: everything is rolled from what the earlier choices allow (construction, fingers, cuff, knuckles, claws, locking, material, cloth, trim), so nothing is thrown away; `allowed` is that grid, checked by a test. Weights are worked out from area × thickness × density, scaled by the material's density against steel (or leather, linen).
- Cuffs reach up the forearm over the arms' vambraces, 28 mm out from the skin, up to 200 mm from the wrist (agreed with the Arms work, whose pieces stay within 20 mm and end at the wrist).
- Claws and knuckle spikes will give unarmed damage bonuses once combat has them (owner's decision).
- Shoulders: one item is a pair, and a pair may be uneven (Milanese great left, lance cut on the right). Wings and sode reach over chest, back and upper arm but count only as Shoulders. Besagews and gardbraces are parts, not items. Classical plate spaulders are bronze and Mycenaean (Dendra), Classical laminar ones Roman; Japanese guards are sode, named by size; yokes and doublings are Classical. A style leans the kind: ailettes only European or fantasy, the wing only Steppe, guards more in regional styles. Adornments sit on top of any style. Edges agreed with Torso, Arms and Neck: rigid pieces lie outside the breastplate and gorget, lames end about 130 mm down the arm over the rerebrace.
- Torso: one item per piece, Outer on Torso; the backplate is part of it (`back`), so Back stays free for packs. Lined pieces and padded jacks also take Middle, through `Item::padded` (form, not kind); the Arming Vest is Middle itself. A rigid piece may be `over_mail`: its own mail shirt under it, one item (owner's decision, no fourth layer). Plate's breast comes from the style (Milanese globose, Gothic waisted with a pointed plackart, Kastenbrust boxed, Greenwich peascod, Maximilian fluted, Classical muscle, bell or discs, Japanese tankō, Elven leaf, Dwarven panels); names follow the breast and back (Cuirass or Breastplate, Kardiophylax a disc without a back). The many-plate constructions name themselves by style: Lorica Squamata and Plumata (Classical scale), Dō-maru, Haramaki and Ō-yoroi Dō (Japanese lamellar), Mountain Pattern Cuirass (Chinese), Klivanion (Byzantine), Lorica Segmentata and Okegawa-dō (laminar), Banded Mail (laminar over mail), Coat of Plates, Jack of Plate (stitched plates, always lined), Leather Brigandine, Dingjia, Tatami Dō, Yushman and Bakhterets (Steppe), Zirah Bagtar and Char-aina (Indo-Persian), Slat Armour, Rattan Cuirass, Coin Armour, Hide Armour. Plated mail and mirrors include their mail and take mail's finishes; lacquer goes on leather, horn and bone plates. Weights from area × thickness × density, calibrated to a steel cuirass of about 5.3 kg. Edges agreed with Shoulders, Waist and Neck: rigid shells 25 mm off the skin, the hem at the waist with a flange 20 mm below, shoulder straps under pauldrons.
- Head: every helmet is rolled from a row of `table.rs` made in its style (all rows of its kind if none are), each property from that row's lists, and named by the first named row it fits, so narrower rows go first; a grotesque visor is always a Skull Helm. Rows must stay distinguishable: the tests check every name turns up. Generic names: Helmet, Helm (closed face), Skull Cap or Cap, War Hat (brim all round), with the material first unless iron or steel (only mithril and adamantine on specific names), and an ornament first of all ("Horned Great Helm"). Caps are Skin (Head's Skin also takes glasses and masks later), coifs Middle on Head and Neck, helmets Outer; bevored helms (frog-mouth, great bascinet) Outer on Head and Neck. Edges agreed with Neck: aventails, gorgets and separate bevors are Neck items, hanging from `rim`; chin plates built into a helmet are Head. Weights from parts' areas (bowl to the ears about 900 cm²) × thickness × density, plus a 200 g lining: a steel great helm about 2.9 kg, a frog-mouth about 7.7. A Conical leather helmet is the boar's tusk helmet (`tusks`).
- Arms: one item covers both arms (Arms has one place); only an archer's bracer (usually the left, bow arm) and a Classical manica (usually the right, sword arm) may be one arm, still taking the whole place (owner's decision). Construction is rolled from the style's own list, then reach, then material. Specific names: Kote and Han Kote (Japanese plates on cloth), Kusari Gote, Manica/Manicae, Bazubands (Indo-Persian plate rising to a point over the elbow), Archer's Bracer; otherwise Bracers, Vambraces, Couters, Rerebraces, Arm Harness (whole plate), or construction sleeves. Whole kote also take Middle (`padded`). Every piece stops 10 mm above the wrist and rerebraces start 100 mm down, under the pauldron's lames; rigid pieces stand 18 mm off the skin so gauntlet cuffs go over them (agreed with Hands and Shoulders). Besagews are Shoulders' parts. Weights from area per arm × per-area weight of the construction × density: a steel arm harness about 3.5 kg, leather bracers about 0.3 kg, mail sleeves about 2.9 kg.
- Legs: one item covers both legs (Legs has one place); only a Classical greave may be one leg (1 in 20, usually the left). Three kinds: `LegArmour` (Outer on Legs), `Chausses` (footed mail, Outer on Legs and Feet), `PaddedChausses` (Middle on Legs, in place of trousers). Plates strapped over mail are the chausses' own `reinforce`, not a second Outer item (owner's decision). Japanese pieces are suneate (shins) and haidate (thighs, an apron from the hip), on cloth and tied, front only except tsutsu-suneate. Classical plate is bronze: knemides (knee and shin, sprung shut), ocreae (front of the shin) or ring greaves (closed coils, kept here for want of a Southeast Asian style), perimeridia, episphyria (ankle guards, given to Legs by the owner); Indo-Persian mail and plate on the thigh and knee is the dizçek. Edges agreed with Waist and Feet: cuisses start at the crotch (`hip - thigh`) and rise to the belt outside, rigid pieces stand 15 mm off the skin under Waist's 35 mm tassets, greaves come down to the ankle over a sabaton's top. Weights from area per leg × per-area weight × density: a steel leg harness about 4 kg, greaves 1.7, poleyns 0.65, footed chausses 4.5, padded chausses 1.2.
- Waist: everything hung from the waist is Waist however far down it reaches; only cuirassier tassets (plate laminated to the knee, wrapping the thigh) also take Legs (owner's decision). Never Middle: Waist's Middle is the belt's. A style's own list of constructions (never mirrors), then the shape of piece: war girdles (bronze: Mitra with a belly plate, Italic Belt without), faulds (2 to 6 hoops, maybe a culet), tassets (single plates, pointed in Gothic and Elven, or laminated: Almain; knee-long in later styles; small side ones in 15th-century styles; the Dendra's three pairs), hip panels (Kusazuri, 4 to 8; steppe and Chinese flaps, 2 or 4), pteruges (1 to 3 rows; the Cingulum Apron over the front; War Kilt in studded leather), skirts (Tonlet, Steel Bases in Maximilian and Greenwich, mail, scale, lamellar, brigandine, Kremasmata), mail breeches. Plate faulds and tassets may have mail beneath (one item; mail skirts are also their own) and a plain groin plate, never a codpiece's shape (owner's decisions). Edges agreed with Torso and Legs: pieces start at `waist + 30` under the torso's hem; hanging tassets stand 35 mm off the thigh over the cuisses; a coat's skirt is a waist skirt in a set. Weights from area × per-area weight × density: a steel tonlet about 5.5 kg, a mail skirt to mid-thigh about 3 kg, a fauld about 1 kg.
- Feet: one item covers both feet, Outer on Feet, over shoes or boots (boots are Middle, as shoes). A style's own list of constructions, then the piece (sabatons in plate, leather, scale or splint; mail or ring mail shoes; foot guards in plate, leather, plates in cloth or mail and plate), then a toe the style gives (Gothic pointed, Milanese round, Maximilian bear-paw or cow-mouth, Greenwich duck-bill or cow-mouth, Kastenbrust pointed or round, Elven short points curling up, Dwarven cow-mouth or claws) that the construction can be shaped to. Points 20-130 mm, detachable ones 80-250 (owner). Regional styles with no foot armour of their own (Chinese, steppe, Byzantine, Indo-Persian) make mail shoes and leather foot guards. Names: Gothic, Duck-bill, Bear-paw, Cow-mouth and Clawed Sabatons, Kusari Tabi, Kogake; Armoured Boots for any rising over the ankle (owner); Sabatons, Mail Shoes, Foot Guards otherwise. Edges agreed with Legs: the line is the ankle; the ankle plate at `ankle_radius + 10` under the greave's flared edge. Weights from area × per-area weight × density, checked against a 200 g sabaton (Higgins Armory): a plain steel pair about 430 g, with heel caps about 730.
- Neck: five item kinds. Collars are Middle (padded, the mail standard, the Japanese kikko guruwa), so a standard goes under a bevor or aventail as it was worn; a character wears a standard or a padded collar, not both (owner). Gorgets, bevors and aventails are Outer on Neck, so only one of them at a time; the mantle (bishop's mantle) is Outer on Neck and Shoulders, so no pauldrons with it (owner). An aventail needs no helmet: without one it hangs from a plain band (owner). A gorget is the collar alone, a nodowa's bib, plates over chest and back, or those with shoulder lames (Colletin); a tall collar of lames goes under the breastplate, a low rim over it (`over_breastplate`). Aventails and mail standards may reach over the shoulders but count as Neck only (half cover). Mail hems may be vandyked, scales and lamellae scalloped; Indo-Persian mail may be patterned with brass and copper rings. Collars and plate collars take 2 × 1 cells, the rest 2 × 2 (owner). Edges agreed with Head (aventails hang from `HeadForm::rim`, 10 mm off the head; chin plates built into a helmet are Head), Shoulders (gorget lames to `shoulder_half - 40` under pauldrons; hanging pieces under shoulder pieces) and Torso (a tall-collared gorget's plates under the breastplate, a low one over it). Weights from area × per-area weight × density: a steel gorget with plates about 1.2 kg, a bevor about 1.1, a mail aventail about 1.2.
