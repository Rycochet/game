# shared: dungeon

Dungeons and buildings as rules: a grid of cells (floor, wall, doors, windows, outside ground, water, lava) with the room each belongs to, the thin shape walls take, and what can be seen (or lit) from a cell. No drawing (that is client `dungeon_map`); levels are generated by `shared::site`.

## Files

- `crates/shared/src/dungeon.rs`: `Cell`, `WallShape`, `RoomId`, `Level`, `Sight` and the private line checks (`clear_line`). Golden tests (test room layout, sight from its centre), wall shapes, sight past columns and through openings, mutual sight.

## Public API

- `Cell` (`Floor`, `Wall`, `Door`, `SecretDoor`, `Ground`, `Water`, `Lava`, `Window`; serde, sent in `protocol::LevelChunk`): `is_walkable()` (floor, door, ground), `is_open()` (sight and light pass the whole cell: those plus water, lava and windows), `is_wall()` (wall, secret door), `is_opening()` (door, window). Doors are open for now; secret doors act as wall until finding them exists.
- `WallShape { north, east, south, west }`: which sides a wall runs out to; `reach()` how far it goes from the centre each way, in `CELL_UNITS` (40 a cell): `WALL_HALF_UNITS` (2: it covers 45–55% of the cell), to the edge where joined, `WALL_END_UNITS` (4: stopping 60% across) past the centre if joined on one side only. A column has no sides.
- `RoomId(u16)`: the planned room a cell is in.
- `Level`: rectangle of cells (`new(min_x, min_y, width, height)` all wall, `set`, `cell`, `is_open`, `is_walkable`, `wall_shape`, `opening_runs_east_west`, `room`, `set_room`, `contains`, `bounds`). Outside the rectangle counts as wall, in no room. `test_room()`: 15 × 7 floor cells (east–west × north–south) walled round, centred on `(0, 0)`, with a 1 × 3 wall at `x = -2`, `y = -1..=1` (for tests, and shown before any site is entered).
- `Sight`: cells seen, sized to a level. `add_view(level, x, y, radius)` adds what is seen from a cell (`radius` in cells, straight-line between centres, or `None`); calls combine (shared sight, several lights). `add_glimpses(...)`: the same, but open cells count when a line reaches near one of their corners too; not symmetric, so for drawing only, never game rules. `is_seen(x, y)`, `insert(x, y)` (count a cell as seen: used as a set of cells, e.g. remembered or known ones).

## Depends on

- Nothing.

## Used by

- `shared::site` (generators), `server` (`sites`: sight and preloading), client `common` (`CurrentDungeon`), `player` (only walkable cells can be stepped on), `dungeon_map` (sight, light reach, cell colours), `site` (ground leads out).

## Notes

- Three kinds of cell so far: walkable and see-through (floor, door, ground), not walkable but see-through (water, lava, window), not walkable and blocking sight (walls, in the thin shape only). Walkable cells that block sight are planned (effects), not built. Light uses the same `Sight` with a radius, so it is blocked by exactly the same rule.
- A wall blocks movement in its whole cell, but sight and light only where the wall stands: they pass beside a column or a wall's end. Doors and windows don't block sight anywhere in their cell, even at the frame drawn at their edges (that frame and how it looks are the client's).
- Sight is exact straight lines in integers: deterministic, no floats, and mutual, since a line is the same both ways. Open cells are seen when the line to their centre misses every wall; a wall is seen when an open cell touching it (corners too) is. Cost grows with the open cells times the distance a line gets, so the server only looks from cells it hasn't looked from yet.
- Walls join walls, and doors and windows in line with them (`opening_runs_east_west`: the way with more walls beside the opening); touching only at corners, they don't join, so sight passes between them.
- Changing the algorithm changes what players can see: update the golden test deliberately.
