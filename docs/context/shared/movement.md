# shared: movement

How characters move: one step at a time to one of the 8 neighbouring grid points, with the rules the client checks before showing a step and the server checks again before accepting it.

## Files

- `crates/shared/src/movement.rs`: `Step`, `step_on_level`, `mob_step_on_level`, `mob_can_stand`, `step_cost`, `WorldGround`, `step_on_world_map`, `steps_between`. In `movement/`: `gait.rs` (`Gait`, `MOVEMENT_SKILLS`: Climbing, Running and Weight Training's nodes), `training.rs` (practice from moving), `world_step.rs` (`world_step_cost`, `world_step_time`, `climbed`), `terrain.rs` (`Terrain`, `Footing`, `PIONEER_SAVING_PER_MILLE`, `TERRAIN_STEP`, `WorldGround::footing`, `terrain`, `land_terrain`, `terrain_square`), `wading.rs` (`wades`, `footing_on`, `footing_of`, `level_step_cost`), `swimming.rs` (`step_on_level_by`, `step_on_world_map_by`, `WorldGround::sea_depth` and `swimmable`), `wild_heights.rs` (`WildHeights`, `wild_step_cost`, and `WorldGround`'s `broad_elevation` and `low_water_elevation` for them), `flat_ground.rs` (`FlatGround`). Tests: only single steps exist (also when decoded from a message), steps on a level need walkable cells, only players step onto World Gates, one cell of liquid is jumped, costs follow distance, water stops world map steps but not out of it, slopes cost extra, steps between points add up.

## Public API

- `Step` (serde, as `(i8, i8)`): `dx` and `dy` each -1, 0 or 1, not both 0. `Step::new(dx, dy) -> Option<Step>` is the only way to make one; decoding anything else from a message fails (the link reports it garbled). `.dx()`, `.dy()`, `.from((x, y))`.
- `step_on_level(level, from, step) -> Option<(x, y)>`: onto a walkable cell (`Level::is_walkable`), or over one liquid, hole or fence cell onto a walkable one beyond it (a jump: two cells away, one `Step`, nobody stops there); what can't be crossed can't be stepped onto. Cutting a wall's corner diagonally is allowed.
- `mob_step_on_level` / `mob_can_stand(level, x, y)`: the same for mobs, but never onto (or landing a jump on) a `Cell::WorldGate`: only players go through gates (owner). Every mob step, guard spot, trail and distance map uses them (site spawns need no check: only the wilderness and the Hall have gates).
- `STRAIGHT_COST` (70), `DIAGONAL_COST` (99), `step_cost(from, to)`: the time a step takes, by the distance it covers (a jump is two cells' worth). Pathfinding uses it now; walking speed and terrain costs are to use it too.
- `WorldConditions { water, ice }`: what decides where can be walked now, as the server says (the hour's `WorldParams::water_level`; `ice`: whether frozen sea counts as ground, true for walking, false for laying roads).
- `past_the_ice(width, y)` (the same without a `WorldGround`, for `wilderness`), `WorldGround::past_the_ice(y)`: past `climate::ICE_LATITUDE` (80°, 2/9 of the width from the equator), where nobody may step, even from open water, and no poi stands (owner: the map stretches badly near the poles); not in the showcase plain. `step_on_world_map` and `pathfinding::find_world_path` refuse it.
- `WorldGround::new(&WorldParams)`, `.elevation(x, y, conditions) -> Option<i32>` (`None` past the ice too): in whole quarters of `Surface::broad_height`, `None` for open water (sea frozen by the climate, `SurfaceSample::is_frozen`, counts as ground when `ice`).
- `step_on_world_map(ground, conditions, from, step) -> Option<(x, y)>`: onto dry ground or ice; anyone standing in open water may step anywhere, so nobody caught by the tide, a thaw or put there is stuck.
- Terrain (owner, 2026-11-08): `Terrain::{Grassland, Forest, Jungle, Snow, Desert}` from the biome at the corner of the point's `TERRAIN_STEP` (4) square (biomes change slowly and the climate is slow to sample) (`of_biome`: grassland and savanna, the forests, tropical rainforest, tundra, desert; no marshes, as the climate has no wetlands), slowing steps off roads and water to `per_mille` (1.2, 1.3, 1.5, 1.8, 2 times) of open ground, less by its Pioneering skill (`skill()`: `survival/pioneering/<key>`; `Gait::cross` takes off two thirds of the extra at 100%). `Footing { road, wet, terrain }`: what a step lands on, counting in that order (`wades`, `slowed_by`). `WorldGround::footing(to, conditions, road, on_river)` builds a world map step's; `footing_on(level, to, terrain)` / `footing_of(cell, terrain)` a level step's, the terrain (the wilderness's, `WildHeights::terrain`, its nearest point's) only on `Ground` or `Grass` cells.
- `world_step_cost(from, to, elevations, footing, gait) -> u32`: `step_cost` plus `SLOPE_COST` (45) per elevation climbed or descended (`climbed`), water counting as flat, the slope cut by `gait`'s Climbing, then by its `Footing`: divided by the road's speed along one, waded (`Gait::swim`) in water (a `river::RiverPoints` point, or the sea), otherwise slowed by its terrain (`Gait::cross`). The road comes from `road::RoadSteps::kind_of`. What routes are found by.
- `world_step_time(...)`: the same, quicker by `gait`'s Running unless wading: what a step takes, charged by the server and paced by the client. Running is left out of routes, as it speeds every step alike and would push the search's estimate above the real cost.
- `Gait` (`UNSKILLED`, `of(&Skills)`, `climb(slope_cost)`, `run(cost)`, `swim(cost)`, `swim_per_mille`): Climbing, Running and Swimming's values, up to `FULL` (100%; specialised past it changes nothing more). 100% Climbing takes `CLIMB_SAVING_PER_MILLE` (900) off slopes, so routes go almost straight over mountains; 100% Running is `RUN_TENTHS` (4.1) times as fast as walking on the world map, a marathon record's 350 m a minute against walking's 85 (owner, 2026-11-08). Wading takes `SWIM_SLOW_PER_MILLE` (1.7×, an average swimmer's 50 m a minute) at 0% to `SWIM_FAST_PER_MILLE` (0.708×, a record's 120 m a minute, quicker than walking) at 100%. `MOVEMENT_SKILLS`: where they are in the tree.
- Deep water (owner): from `DEEP_WATER_FROM` (50%) Swimming, `step_on_level_by(level, from, step, gait)` steps into `Cell::Water` instead of jumping it (never lava), and `step_on_world_map_by(ground, conditions, from, step, gait)` into the sea where `WorldGround::sea_depth` is at most `Gait::deepest_sea` (0 at 50% up to `DEEPEST_SEA`, 2 height units or 200 m, the continental shelf, at 100%; never the deep ocean). Players' steps and routes use these; mobs keep the plain rules.
- Jumping: `is_jump(from, to)` (landing two cells away); `Gait::jump` takes a jump from all of its distance at 0% to `JUMP_BEST_PER_MILLE` (700, a setting to tune) at 100%, in `level_step_cost`. `Gait::cheapest_per_mille`: the least a level step can cost against walking (wading or jumping), which `pathfinding::find_path_by` scales its estimate by.
- `wades(level, to)` (onto `Cell::Shallows` or `Cell::Water`, on any level), `level_step_cost(from, to, heights, wading, gait)`: a level step in `step_cost` units: `wild_step_cost` when the wilderness's heights are given, else its distance, waded if `wading`; can be less than `step_cost` for a good swimmer (`pathfinding::find_path_by`'s `cheapest`).
- `WildHeights::new(&WorldGround, bounds)`, `.at(x, y)`, `.covers(x, y)`: how high wilderness cells count as for walking, in 16ths of an elevation: the world map points' elevations (whatever covers them) blended between the four round each cell's middle, as the wilderness blends its ground, never below the lowest tide (the sea is flat). Each point is sampled once per rectangle. `.with_flats(Vec<FlatGround>)`: ground kept level, which wins over the slopes: the server passes its window's cutouts, the client those it has been told of (`ServerMessage::FlatGround`, sent with a cutout's first cells, so a secret poi's flat is learnt no sooner than its cells). Also what the client draws the wilderness's heights by.
- `FlatGround { bounds, height }` (serde): a rectangle of cells at one height; `of_cutout(&WorldGround, bounds)` levels a cutout's rectangle at its middle cell's height; `.contains(x, y)`.
- `wild_step_cost(from, to, heights, gait) -> u32`: a wilderness step in `step_cost` units: its distance plus `SLOPE_COST` per 16th climbed or descended, less by Climbing. 16 cells across a point cost 16 times its world map step (tested). `energy::wild_step_energy` is it in a site's energy.
- Training: `RUN_PRACTICE` (17) per world map step, `climb_practice(elevations)` (`CLIMB_PRACTICE_PER_ELEVATION`, 6, per elevation beyond the first, so ground within 25 m a step teaches nothing; at most `MOST_CLIMB_PRACTICE`, 40), given at most once per skill every 5 seconds by the server. `PIONEER_PRACTICE` (17) for a terrain's Pioneering skill per step slowed by it, on either map. `SNEAK_PRACTICE` (17) for Sneaking (`MOVEMENT_SKILLS.sneaking`, `stealth/sneaking`) per wilderness step at a sneaking pace while something wary watches (the server decides both). `JUMP_PRACTICE` (40) per jump: slow, about 50% after 3,000 jumps and 100% after 25,000 (owner: takes a lot of practice). `SWIM_PRACTICE` (17) per waded cell, `RIVER_SWIM_PRACTICE` (4) per world map river step (owner: simple, not very effective). `weight_practice(load)`: `WEIGHT_PRACTICE` (17) for Weight Training per step on any map while `Burdened`, twice while `Overloaded` (its effect is in `Sheet::capacity`). In the wilderness Climbing goes by 16ths a cell (elevations per point, the world map's steepness), and Running isn't practised. A test prints the curves (`movement_practice_curve`, `--nocapture`): 50% after about 10 hours of travel, 100% after about 80.
- `steps_between(from, to) -> Vec<Step>`: diagonal first, then straight (dev tools, tests).

## Notes

- Time for steps is `shared::energy`, using the costs here: on the world map every step, by keyboard or click travel, is charged its `world_step_time`, slopes and all. Wilderness steps add their slope (`wild_step_cost`); site and Hall steps are flat (`energy::site_step_cost`), and so are mobs' steps, even in the wilderness, for now.
- Walkable ground follows the tide, by whole game hours, matching what the client draws. If an `Hour` message crosses a step in flight, that step may be refused and put right.
- Climbs count on broad height, not the full surface: tried on real mountains, a higher flat cost or one growing with steepness on the full surface barely changed routes, because small bumps cost the same on any route and drown out the mountain. Tune with `SLOPE_COST` (its comment records what was seen at each value).
