# shared: poi

World map pois: the world grid is cut into chunks, and each chunk has at most one poi (a cave, house, castle, crypt...), each leading to its own map (dungeon, building, town) later. Everything about a poi comes from its chunk's position alone, so client and server agree and it doesn't matter which chunks were looked at first. Pois stand only on dry land (ground above high tide). Its map (a site) is generated by `shared::site` from its kind and seed.

## Files

- `crates/shared/src/poi.rs`: `PoiMap`, `Poi`, `PoiKind`, `ChunkPos`, constants. Golden and invariant tests. In `poi/`: `kind.rs`, `chunk.rs` (`ChunkPos`, `Poi`), `poi_map.rs`, `in_chunk.rs` (a chunk's poi, towns), `area.rs`, `enter.rs` (`ENTER_DISTANCE`, `can_enter_from`).

## Public API

- Constants: `CHUNK_SIZE` (64 grid points), `EDGE_MARGIN` (8: pois stand at least this far inside their chunk, so two are at least 16 apart, a token's width), `POI_CHANCE_PERCENT` (80, the owner's choice), `LAND_TRIES` (8 random spots tried for dry land; a chunk with none has no poi, so seas and coasts have fewer). In desert (`Biome::Desert` where it stands) only one chunk in `DESERT_KEEPS_ONE_IN` (4, owner) keeps its poi, by a hash of its own; no poi stands past the ice (`WorldGround::past_the_ice`, 80°).
- `PoiKind`: 40 kinds: 34 (`Boulders` … `Mausoleum`) named after the tiles `l2_terrain066`–`099` and in their order, all equally likely, then the towns (`Hamlet`, `Village`, `Town`, `City`; `is_town()`) and `Farm` and `HerbalistHut`, with their own odds; `ALL`.
- `ChunkPos { x, y }`: chunk `x` is always `0..columns()` (wraps), `y` unbounded. `PoiKind`, `ChunkPos` and `Poi` are serde-serialisable (sent in `protocol::WorldChunk`).
- `ENTER_DISTANCE` (11) and `can_enter_from(&poi, (x, y), width)`: how near a poi's middle a player must stand on the world map to go into it, by Down or a click (a straight line, the near way round): the player's and pois' tokens are 16 points across, and at 11 apart about a fifth of their area overlaps (owner). The server checks it; Down goes into the nearest such poi the player knows of.
- `Poi { kind, chunk, x, y, seed, desert }`: `x, y` its exact world grid position; `seed` is what its map is generated from; `desert` whether it stands in desert, where more are ruined (`Generator::for_site`).
- `PoiMap::new(&WorldParams, pois_seed)` (the secret `WorldSeed::pois`; keeps its own `Surface`, and finds the world's World Gates with it), `.world_gates()` (their chunks have no other poi), `.width()`, `.columns()`, `.chunk_at(x, y)`, `.in_chunk(chunk) -> Option<Poi>` (`x` in `0..width`), `.in_area(min, max) -> Vec<Poi>` (inclusive, any planet-width frame; results in the caller's frame, sorted by `(y, x)`), `.chunks_in_area(min, max) -> Vec<ChunkPos>` (every chunk touching the area, wrapped, sorted).

## Depends on

- `shared::world_gate`, `shared::rng`, `shared::climate` (where farms and huts stand), `shared::surface` (ground height), `shared::world` (`high_water`).

## Used by

- `shared::road` (towns), `shared::story` (`PlaceKind::of_poi`, `CHUNK_SIZE` for distance bands), `shared::site` (`PoiKind` picks the generator), `shared::protocol` (`WorldChunk`), `server` (`ServerWorld::pois`: what it sends); client `common` (`KnownPois`, `Portal`), `world_map` (poi tokens), `site` (entering).

## Notes

- Towns replace what a chunk rolled, by a separate hash (`town_at`, `TOWNS_PER_THOUSAND`: 30, 15, 5, 2 per 1000 by size), so other chunks keep their poi and the kind says the town's size before any visit.
- Farms replace what a chunk rolled in the same way (`kept_to_land`, `FARMS_PER_THOUSAND`: 60), if the poi's spot is grassland, woodland, seasonal forest or savanna; a town wins. So do herbalists' huts (`HUTS_PER_THOUSAND`: 40) in any kind of forest; a farm wins. New kinds should do this too: adding to the plain roll would move every poi. Oases (owner's design) stand only where the spot is desert: `OASES_PER_THOUSAND` (100) of desert chunks with a poi, by a hash of their own; a town wins, and an oasis is never dropped by the desert's quarter rule.
- Per chunk: `Seed::new(pois_seed.at(&[cx, cy])).rng()`, then in order: the chance roll, the kind, the map seed, then up to `LAND_TRIES` `x`/`y` spots until one is above `WorldParams::high_water()`. Changing that order or any constant changes every world (golden test).
- East–west the world wraps at `width`, rarely a whole number of chunks, so the last column takes the remainder (wider). North–south there's no wrap.
- Land is judged by the float surface height against `high_water`, as the server will; a spot exactly at the threshold could in theory differ across platforms if the noise isn't bit-identical (the surface golden tests guard that).
