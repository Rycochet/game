# Leg armour

Leg armour is one item for both legs, over trousers or padded chausses. It runs from the top of the thigh, under any tassets hanging from the waist, down to the ankle, where sabatons go under its edge. A Classical greave may be worn on one leg only. See [Armour](index) for layers, styles and materials.

## Kinds

| Kind | Covers | Layer | Made as | Named |
| ---- | ------ | ----- | ------- | ----- |
| Leg armour | Legs | Outer | Every construction but mirror | By what it covers (below) |
| Chausses | Legs, feet | Outer | Mail, ring mail | Chausses, Reinforced Chausses |
| Padded Chausses | Legs | Middle | Padded | Padded Chausses |

Leg armour covers the knee, the shin, the thigh, the thigh and knee, the knee and shin, the whole leg, or the ankle. It is closed all round or covers the front only. Knees may have a cop, a cop with a wing, or a tall Japanese plate.

| Name | What it is |
| ---- | ---------- |
| Poleyns, Knee Guards | The knees |
| Greaves, Demi-greaves, Schynbalds | The shins, all round or the front only |
| Cuisses, Thigh Guards | The thighs |
| Leg Harness | Plate over the whole leg |
| Gamboised Cuisses | Quilted thighs |
| Leggings | Soft armour over the legs |
| Chausses, Footless Chausses, Demi-chausses | Mail over the legs and feet, the legs alone, or the front |
| Reinforced Chausses | Mail with poleyns, and maybe schynbalds, over it |
| Suneate, Tsutsu-suneate, Haidate | Japanese shin guards, closed shin guards, and a thigh apron from the hip |
| Knemides, Ocreae, Ring Greaves, Perimeridia, Episphyria, Leg Manicae | Greek and Roman bronze greaves, coiled greaves, thigh guards and ankle guards, and strips on the leg |
| Dizçek | Indo-Persian mail and plate over the thigh and knee |

Spikes on the knees, fins on the shins, studs and unusual materials come first in the name.

## Materials

- Plate: iron or steel; bronze for Classical greaves; mithril and adamantine.
- Mail and ring mail: iron, steel or brass.
- Leather: leather or hardened leather.
- Scale, lamellar, splints: iron, steel, hardened leather or horn.
- Padded: linen, wool or cotton.
