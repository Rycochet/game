# Head armour

Head armour runs from a padded cap to a closed helm. An arming cap sits next to the skin, a coif goes over it, and a helmet goes over both. A few helms also close over the throat and take the neck as well. See [Armour](index) for layers, styles and materials.

## Kinds

| Kind | Covers | Layer | Made as | Named |
| ---- | ------ | ----- | ------- | ----- |
| Arming Cap | Head | Skin | Padded | Arming Cap |
| Coif | Head, neck | Middle | Mail, ring mail, padded | Coif, Mail Coif |
| Helmet | Head | Outer | Plate, lamellar, leather, rattan | By its shape (below) |
| Bevored Helm | Head, neck | Outer | Plate | Frog-mouth Helm, Great Bascinet |

A helmet is shaped by its bowl, its skull (round, low, conical, pointed, flat, onion), its face (open, a nasal bar, spectacles, bars, an opening, a faceplate, a sight slit, a visor, a mask), cheek pieces, what guards the back of the neck (a lip, a broad plate, a tail, lames, flaps), a brim, and what sits on top (a knob, a spike, a comb, a crest, a plume). It is named after the shape it fits:

| Name | What it is |
| ---- | ---------- |
| Skull Cap | A plain round cap |
| Helmet, Helm, War Hat | Any other open-faced helmet; a closed one; one with a brim all round |
| Nasal Helm, Spangenhelm, Ridge Helmet, Spectacle Helm | Early helmets: a bar down the nose, a frame of bands, two halves on a ridge, guards round the eyes |
| Great Helm, Sugarloaf Helm | A closed helm with a faceplate, flat or pointed on top |
| Bascinet, Hounskull, Klappvisor Bascinet | A pointed open helmet, then with a pointed or a small visor |
| Kettle Hat, Cabasset, Morion | Brimmed helmets: wide, narrow, curving up fore and aft |
| Sallet, Barbute, Armet, Close Helmet, Burgonet | Later European helmets: a long tail, a T-shaped opening, rounded and bellows visors, a peak and high comb |
| Lobster-tailed Pot | Bars over the face and a tail of lames |
| Chichak, Turban Helmet, Kulah Khud | Ottoman, onion-shaped and Indo-Persian helmets with a spike |
| Lamellar Helmet, Steppe Helmet | Laced plates, or many plates with flaps and a spike |
| Kabuto, Jingasa | A Japanese helmet with a neck guard of lames; a flat wide hat |
| Corinthian, Attic, Chalcidian, Boeotian, Illyrian, Phrygian Helmet, Pilos Helmet | Greek bronze helmets |
| Montefortino Helmet, Galea, Mask Helmet | Roman helmets |
| Boar's Tusk Helmet, Leather Helmet | Leather, with or without rows of tusk plates |

Rare helmets carry horns, wings, a crown, a front crest, antlers or a dragon, and a grotesque visor makes a Skull Helm. These come first in the name: Horned Great Helm.

## Materials

- Plate: iron or steel; bronze for ancient shapes; mithril and adamantine.
- Lamellar: iron, steel, hardened leather or horn.
- Leather: leather or hardened leather.
- Mail coifs: iron, steel or bronze.
- Padded caps and coifs: linen, wool or cotton.
- Rattan helmets: rattan.
