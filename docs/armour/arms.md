# Arm armour

Arm armour is one item for both arms, over the sleeves of a gambeson. It stops just above the wrist, where a gauntlet's cuff goes over it, and starts under the shoulder armour. An archer's bracer or a Classical manica may be worn on one arm only. See [Armour](index) for layers, styles and materials.

## Kinds

| Kind | Covers | Layer | Made as | Named |
| ---- | ------ | ----- | ------- | ----- |
| Arm armour | Arms | Outer | Every construction but mirror | By what it covers (below) |

It is named by how far it reaches and how it is built, or by its own name where it has one:

| Name | What it is |
| ---- | ---------- |
| Bracers | Light guards on the forearms |
| Archer's Bracer | A bracer on one arm, the bow arm |
| Vambraces | Plate tubes on the forearms |
| Couters | Cups over the elbows, plain or winged |
| Rerebraces | Plate on the upper arms |
| Arm Harness | Plate over the whole arm |
| Splinted Arms | Strips down the arm over a backing |
| Mail, Ring Mail, Scale, Lamellar, Padded, Brigandine Sleeves | Whole sleeves of that construction |
| Manica, Manicae | Roman strips over the arm, often one sword arm |
| Kote, Han Kote, Kusari Gote | Japanese sleeves of plates and mail on cloth |
| Bazubands | Indo-Persian forearm plates rising to a point over the elbow |

Spikes on the elbows or along the arm, studs, and unusual materials come first in the name: Spiked Vambraces. Whole kote also take the Middle layer, as they are padded.

## Styles

| Style | What it makes here |
| ----- | ------------------ |
| White, Gothic, Milanese, Maximilian, Greenwich, Kastenbrust | Plate vambraces, couters, rerebraces and arm harness; mail and splinted sleeves |
| Elven, Dwarven | Any European piece, slender or heavy |
| Japanese | Kote |
| Chinese | Lamellar, scale and brigandine sleeves |
| Steppe | Mail sleeves, bracers, lamellar |
| Classical | Manicae, bronze guards, linen and leather |
| Byzantine | Lamellar, scale and splinted sleeves |
| Indo-Persian | Bazubands and mail sleeves |

## Materials

- Plate and splints: iron or steel; bronze for Classical guards; mithril and adamantine.
- Mail and ring mail: iron, steel or brass.
- Leather: leather or hardened leather.
- Scale, lamellar, laminar: iron, steel, hardened leather or horn.
- Padded and cloth: linen, wool, cotton or silk.
