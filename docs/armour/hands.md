# Hand armour

Hand armour is one item for both hands. Gauntlets go over gloves or padded gloves, and their cuffs over the ends of the arm armour. Rings go on first: anything over the fingers locks them on until it comes off. See [Armour](index) for layers, styles and materials.

## Kinds

| Kind | Covers | Layer | Made as | Named |
| ---- | ------ | ----- | ------- | ----- |
| Gauntlets | Hands, fingers | Outer | Plate, mail, ring mail, scale, leather, plates in cloth, mail and plate | By its make (below) |
| Demi-gauntlets | Hands | Outer | Plate, mail and plate | Demi-gauntlets, Tekkō |
| Padded Gloves | Hands, fingers | Middle | Padded | Padded Gloves |

Gauntlets have fingers each covered on their own, or are mittens with the fingers together and the thumb apart. Demi-gauntlets cover only the back of the hand and the wrist, so rings stay free. Fingered gauntlets must be made for your number of fingers; mittens and demi-gauntlets fit any hand.

The cuff is a wrist band, an hourglass, a bell, a point, or long to mid-forearm. The knuckles are plain, ridged, spiked, or have a disc on the back. Rare plate mittens lock shut on a grip, and some fingered gauntlets have claws.

## Materials

- Plate: steel or iron; bronze, rarely; mithril and adamantine.
- Mail and ring mail: steel, iron or brass, with a leather palm.
- Scale: steel, iron, bronze, horn, hardened leather, dragon scale or chitin.
- Leather: buff leather, hardened leather or leather.
- Plates in cloth: steel or iron plates under linen, wool or silk.
- Padded: linen, wool or cotton.
- Brass trim on some plate.
