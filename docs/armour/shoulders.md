# Shoulder armour

Shoulder armour is one item for both shoulders, worn over a gambeson and over the top of the breastplate. Its wings and hanging guards reach over the chest, back and upper arm, but it counts only for the shoulders, so arm armour still goes on. See [Armour](index) for layers, styles and materials.

## Kinds

| Kind | Covers | Layer | Made as | Named |
| ---- | ------ | ----- | ------- | ----- |
| Pauldrons | Shoulders | Outer | Plate, leather, scale, mail and plate | Pauldrons, Great Pauldrons, Gothic Pauldrons, Plated Mail Pauldrons |
| Spaulders | Shoulders | Outer | Plate, leather, laminar | Spaulders, Dendra Shoulder Guards, Lorica Shoulder Guards |
| Shoulder guards | Shoulders | Outer | Lamellar, scale, laminar, plates in cloth, leather, mail | Ko-sode, Chū-sode, Ō-sode, Lamellar Shoulder Guards |
| Shoulder cape | Shoulders | Outer | Mail, scale, padded, leather, ring mail | Mail Doubling, Scale Doubling, Ring Mail Cape, Linothorax Yoke |
| Shoulder discs | Shoulders | Outer | Mail and plate, plate, mirror | Naplechniki, Mirror Shoulder Plates |
| Ailettes | Shoulders | Outer | Leather, plate, wood | Ailettes |
| Wing | Shoulders | Outer | Laminar | Chukchi Wing |

- **Pauldrons**: a dome over the shoulder with lames down the arm, small or large wings over chest and back, and sometimes a raised guard at the neck. A pair may be uneven: a great left pauldron, or the right cut away for a lance.
- **Spaulders**: a plate with lames below, leaving the armpit open. Round discs may guard the armpit.
- **Shoulder guards**: flat hanging guards of rows of plates, small, medium or large. The Japanese ones are sode.
- **Shoulder cape**: a round doubling of mail or scale, or a yoke of linen or leather tied down on the chest.
- **Ailettes**: flat boards tied on the shoulders, painted with simple heraldry.
- **Wing**: a broad board of wood or leather strips over the left shoulder, used as a shield.

Rigid pieces may carry spikes, horns, a beast's head or a skull. Leather may be plain, tooled, studded or covered in rings.

## Materials

- Plate: iron or steel; bronze for Dendra guards; mithril and adamantine.
- Leather: leather or hardened leather.
- Scale, lamellar, laminar: iron, steel, hardened leather or horn, laced with silk or leather.
- Plates in cloth: iron or steel under linen, wool or silk.
- Ailettes and the wing: wood, hardened leather or plate.
- Brass trim on plate and mail.
