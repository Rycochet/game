# Neck armour

The neck takes a collar underneath and one piece of armour over it: a gorget, a bevor or an aventail. A mantle also covers the shoulders. See [Armour](index) for layers, styles and materials.

## Kinds

| Kind | Covers | Layer | Made as | Named |
| ---- | ------ | ----- | ------- | ----- |
| Collar | Neck | Middle | Padded, mail, plates in cloth | Padded Collar, Mail Standard, Guruwa |
| Gorget | Neck | Outer | Plate, leather, scale, laminar, lamellar | Gorget, Colletin, Nodowa |
| Bevor | Neck | Outer | Plate, leather | Bevor |
| Aventail | Neck | Outer | Mail, ring mail, mail and plate, lamellar, scale, quilted, leather | Aventail, Camail |
| Mantle | Neck, shoulders | Outer | Mail, scale | Bishop's Mantle |

- **Collar**: a padded collar, a standing mail collar (a standard) or a Japanese collar lined with small plates. Worn alone or under a bevor or aventail.
- **Gorget**: the collar alone, a bib over the chest (the Japanese nodowa), plates over chest and back, or those with lames over the shoulders (a colletin). A tall collar of lames goes under a breastplate; a low rim goes over it.
- **Bevor**: a plate over the chin and throat, up to the mouth or the chin, with lames below. Pointed and ridged in Gothic style.
- **Aventail**: a curtain of mail or plates hung from the helmet's lower edge, or from a plain band without one. It may be open at the front, close round the chin, close up to the nose, or have a flap over the mouth.
- **Mantle**: a short cape of mail or scale over the neck and shoulders, so no shoulder armour goes with it.

Mail hems may be straight or cut in points, scales and lamellae scalloped. Mail may be edged in brass, or patterned in brass and copper rings in Indo-Persian style.

## Styles

| Style | What it makes here |
| ----- | ------------------ |
| White, Gothic, Milanese, Kastenbrust | Plate gorgets and bevors, mail standards and aventails |
| Maximilian | Fluted plate gorgets |
| Greenwich | Etched plate gorgets |
| Elven, Dwarven | Any European piece, slender or heavy |
| Japanese | Nodowa and lined collars |
| Chinese, Steppe, Byzantine | Lamellar, scale and leather aventails, leather gorgets |
| Classical | Tall bronze neck guards and scale |
| Indo-Persian | Aventails of mail, or mail and plate, often closed to the nose |

## Materials

- Plate: iron or steel; bronze for Classical guards; mithril and adamantine.
- Mail: iron, steel or bronze, with brass edging.
- Leather: leather or hardened leather.
- Padded and lined collars: linen, wool, cotton or silk.
