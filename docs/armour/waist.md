# Waist armour

Waist armour hangs from the waist, under the hem of the torso armour, however far down it reaches. Your belt stays underneath it. A coat's skirt is waist armour too, made with the coat as a [set](sets). See [Armour](index) for layers, styles and materials.

## Kinds

| Kind | Covers | Layer | Made as | Named |
| ---- | ------ | ----- | ------- | ----- |
| Waist armour | Waist | Outer | Every construction but mirror | By its shape (below) |
| Cuirassier Tassets | Waist, legs | Outer | Plate | Cuirassier Tassets |

| Shape | What it is | Named |
| ----- | ---------- | ----- |
| War girdle | A broad belt of bronze, maybe with a plate over the belly | War Girdle, Mitra, Italic Belt |
| Fauld | Two to six hoops round the hips, maybe with hoops over the rump | Fauld |
| Tassets | Plates hanging over the thighs, single or of lames, to the hip, mid-thigh or knee | Tassets, Almain Tassets, Dendra Hip Plates |
| Hip panels | Four to eight hanging panels, or flaps | Kusazuri, Lamellar Hip Panels |
| Pteruges | One to three rows of strips | Pteruges, Cingulum Apron, War Kilt |
| Skirt | A skirt all round, to the hip, mid-thigh or knee | Tonlet, Steel Bases, Mail Skirt, Brigandine Skirt, Kremasmata |
| Mail breeches | Mail over the hips and between the legs | Mail Breeches |

Cuirassier tassets are lames to the knee, wrapping the thigh, so they also take the legs. Plate faulds and tassets may have a mail skirt beneath, as one item, and a plain groin plate. The Cingulum Apron is pteruges over the front only.

## Styles

| Style | What it makes here |
| ----- | ------------------ |
| White, Milanese | Faulds and tassets, mail skirts |
| Gothic | Faulds with pointed tassets |
| Maximilian, Greenwich | Fluted or etched tassets, and steel bases |
| Kastenbrust | Tonlets and long faulds |
| Elven, Dwarven | Any European piece, leaf-pointed or heavy |
| Japanese | Kusazuri |
| Chinese, Steppe, Byzantine | Lamellar and scale skirts, hip flaps, brigandine skirts |
| Classical | Pteruges, the cingulum apron, war girdles, Dendra hip plates, kremasmata |
| Indo-Persian | Mail skirts, mail and plate |

## Materials

- Plate: iron or steel; bronze for Classical pieces; mithril and adamantine.
- Mail and ring mail: iron, steel or brass.
- Leather: leather, hardened leather or buff leather.
- Scale, lamellar, laminar: iron, steel, hardened leather or horn, laced with silk or leather.
- Pteruges and padded skirts: linen or leather.
