# Torso armour

Torso armour covers the body from the shoulders to the waist, back included, so a pack and a cloak still go on your back. An arming vest or gambeson goes underneath, unless the piece is lined or is padding itself. A breastplate without a back also counts as torso armour. Longer coats are [sets](sets). See [Armour](index) for layers, styles and materials.

A rigid piece may have its own mail shirt underneath, as one item. A lined piece, and padded jacks, take the Middle layer as well, so no vest goes under them. Plated mail and mirror armour include their mail.

## Styles

The style shapes a plate breast:

| Style | What it makes here |
| ----- | ------------------ |
| White | Round breasts and munition cuirasses |
| Gothic | Waisted breasts with a pointed plate over the belly and fanning ridges |
| Milanese | Rounded breasts with a half plate over the belly |
| Maximilian | Fluted breasts |
| Greenwich | Peascod breasts, pointed at the belly |
| Kastenbrust | Boxed breasts |
| Elven, Dwarven | Leaf-shaped breasts; breasts of panels |
| Japanese | Tankō, dō-maru, haramaki and ō-yoroi dō, okegawa-dō, tatami dō |
| Chinese | Mountain pattern cuirasses, dingjia, rattan, lamellar and scale |
| Steppe | Yushman, bakhterets, lamellar, scale and leather |
| Classical | Muscle, bell and triple-disc cuirasses in bronze, linothorax, mail and scale, segmentata |
| Byzantine | Klivanion, lamellar and scale over mail |
| Indo-Persian | Zirah bagtar, char-aina, mail |

## Materials

- Plate: iron or steel; bronze for Classical cuirasses and the tankō's iron; mithril and adamantine.
- Mail and ring mail: iron, steel or bronze; mithril.
- Leather: leather, hardened leather, buff leather or rawhide.
- Scale and lamellar: iron, steel, bronze, hardened leather, horn, bone, dragon scale, chitin; brass coins.
- Padded: linen, wool or cotton; coir for Kiribati armour.
- Plates in cloth: iron or steel under linen, wool, silk or leather.
- Splints and slats: iron, wood or bone. Woven: rattan.
