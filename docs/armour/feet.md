# Foot armour

Foot armour is one item for both feet, worn over [shoes or boots](../footwear). It reaches up to the ankle, under the edge of the leg armour. See [Armour](index) for layers, styles and materials.

## Kinds

| Kind | Covers | Layer | Made as | Named |
| ---- | ------ | ----- | ------- | ----- |
| Foot armour | Feet | Outer | Plate, leather, scale, splint, mail, ring mail, plates in cloth, mail and plate | By its make (below) |

| Name | What it is |
| ---- | ---------- |
| Sabatons | Plate, leather, scale or splints over the foot: a toe cap, lames and an ankle plate, maybe a heel cap |
| Gothic, Duck-bill, Bear-paw, Cow-mouth, Clawed Sabatons | Sabatons named by their toe |
| Armoured Boots | Sabatons with a cuff rising over the ankle |
| Mail Shoes, Kusari Tabi | Mail over the foot; Japanese mail socks |
| Foot Guards, Kogake | Plates over the top of the foot; the Japanese armoured cover |

Toes are round, pointed, duck-billed, bear-pawed, cow-mouthed or clawed. Points reach well past the toes; some are long and come off for walking.

## Styles

| Style | What it makes here |
| ----- | ------------------ |
| White | Round-toed sabatons |
| Gothic | Long pointed sabatons |
| Milanese | Round-toed sabatons |
| Maximilian | Bear-paw or cow-mouth, fluted |
| Greenwich | Duck-bill or cow-mouth, very wide |
| Kastenbrust | Pointed or round |
| Elven | Short points curling up |
| Dwarven | Cow-mouths or claws |
| Japanese | Kogake and kusari tabi |
| Classical | Bronze foot guards |
| Chinese, Steppe, Byzantine, Indo-Persian | Mail shoes and leather foot guards |

## Materials

- Plate and splints: iron or steel; bronze for Classical guards; mithril and adamantine.
- Mail and ring mail: iron, steel or brass.
- Leather: leather or hardened leather.
- Scale: iron, steel, hardened leather or horn.
