# Sets

A set is one item made of pieces for several places, all in one style, of one material and one quality, and made for one size. It goes on and comes off whole. Sets come with their own padding where they need it. See [Armour](index) for layers, styles and materials.

## Kinds

| Kind | Covers | Layer | Made as | Named |
| ---- | ------ | ----- | ------- | ----- |
| Armour Coat | Arms, torso, waist | Outer | Any construction but padding | Hauberk, Buff Coat, the torso's own name, or a coat of its construction |
| Corslet | Neck, torso, waist | Outer, with padding on the torso | Rigid constructions | Corslet |
| Half Armour | Head, neck, shoulders, arms, torso, waist, hands | Outer, with padding on the shoulders, arms and torso | Any construction but padding | Half Armour |
| Full Armour | All nine places | Outer, with padding on the shoulders, arms and torso | Any construction but padding | A harness, by its style |

- **Armour Coat**: a body with sleeves and a skirt of the same make: a mail hauberk, a buff coat, a lamellar coat. A gambeson goes under a mail coat.
- **Corslet**: a breast and back with a gorget and tassets.
- **Half Armour**: everything above the legs, helmet and gauntlets included.
- **Full Armour**: everything, from helmet to sabatons.

A set's pieces are the same kinds as on the other pages: its helmet is a helmet, its neck a gorget on rigid armour or an aventail hung from the helmet on mail. Every piece takes the set's material where it can be made of it. Padding and some pieces keep their own.

A set is called by its kind and how it is made: Hauberk, Buff Coat, Lorica Segmentata, Dō-maru, Brigandine Coat, Corslet, Half Armour, Mail Half Armour, Gothic Harness, Milanese Harness, Elven Plate, Dwarven Plate, Gusoku, Full Mail, Full Lamellar Armour. A rare material comes first: Mithril Hauberk.

You can wear a surcoat or tabard over a set.

## Styles

| Style | What it makes here |
| ----- | ------------------ |
| White, Gothic, Milanese, Maximilian, Greenwich, Kastenbrust | Harnesses of plate, named by their style; hauberks and mail |
| Elven, Dwarven | Elven and Dwarven Plate, and coats |
| Japanese | Gusoku and dō-maru |
| Chinese, Steppe, Byzantine | Lamellar, scale and brigandine coats and sets |
| Classical | Segmentata, linothorax, scale and muscle cuirass sets |
| Indo-Persian | Mail and plate, mirrors |

## Materials

The torso piece's material is the set's: iron, steel, bronze, leather, horn and the rest, as on the torso page. Every other piece is made of it too, unless it can't be: padding stays cloth, and laces stay silk or leather.

## Weights

A set weighs what its pieces weigh together:

| Set | Weight |
| --- | ------ |
| Mail hauberk | about 9 kg |
| Plate corslet | about 8 kg |
| Plate half armour | about 15 kg |
| Plate full armour | 18 to 25 kg |
