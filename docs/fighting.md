# Fighting

Every creature can fight, with weapons or with its bare hands. You fight with **Attack**, an ability every creature knows, and can mend wounds with **Heal**, the first spell. New characters start with Heal in the first slot of the action bar and Attack in the second.

## Attacking

Target a creature (right-click it, hold a finger on it, or press Tab), then use Attack: press its key, click it or tap it. Your character swings at the target if it is close enough and in sight. Most weapons reach the next cell; polearms and spears reach two cells away, but not through walls.

Each attack winds up for a short moment before it lands. If your target has moved out of reach or out of sight when it lands, the attack misses and the time is lost. You can call off an attack that is still winding up with Esc, or by moving, and you can act again almost at once.

Right-click Attack on the action bar (or hold a finger on it) to have it used by itself: you then attack your target whenever it is an enemy in reach and you are ready. A creature that attacks you becomes your target if you have no target in sight.

## Healing

**Heal** turns 6 Mana into Health. Use it like Attack: it heals your target if they aren't an enemy, otherwise (or with no target) it heals you. Others can be healed from up to 6 cells away, in sight. It always spends its 6 Mana, even on someone already whole; healing beyond their most is lost. At first each point of Mana gives 2 Health, rising with the Healing skill to 20 for a master.

Made automatic, Heal heals you whenever your Health is at a quarter or less and you have the Mana.

## Lining abilities up

Abilities you use yourself wait their turn: press several while you are still busy and they are used one after another, in the order you pressed them. Each one waiting has yellow and black stripes running round it on the action bar; press it again to take it out of the line. One that can't be done when its turn comes is passed over. Moving clears the line.

What you do yourself always comes first. While you walk somewhere or have abilities lined up, automatic ones wait, and start again once you have arrived and the last has landed.

## Automatic abilities take turns

When several abilities are automatic, they are tried from left to right along the action bar (keys 1 to 0), and the first that can be done is. Attack can be done whenever an enemy is in reach, so anything to its right waits until it can't: put Heal before Attack to stop and heal when you are low, or after it to keep fighting.

## How quick you are

After every action you wait before you can act again. On the action bar, a shade over your abilities drains away as the wait ends. An ability that winds up for a second or more also shows a gold bar under the token, so you can see it coming, from enemies too.

How long an attack takes depends on what you hold:

| Weapon | Roughly |
| --- | --- |
| Bare hands | Two attacks a second |
| Knife or dagger | One and a half a second |
| Sword | One a second |
| Polearm | One every two seconds |

Long and heavy weapons are slower, and agility makes them quicker. Skill with the weapon makes every attack a little quicker. Heavier loads slow everything you do.

## Harm

Every attack that lands does some harm; there are no misses. How much depends on:

- **The weapon:** its quality matters most, then the metal it is made of, then its weight. A slow, heavy weapon hits harder, so it does about as much harm over time as a quick, light one; weapons with an edge or a point do more than fists.
- **Strength:** stronger creatures hit harder.
- **Skill against Defence:** your skill with the weapon against the other's skill at defending. Two even fighters each take half of each blow; the better you are, the more of it lands. Defending is always at work, with nothing to press.
- **Luck:** each blow can be up to half as hard again, or half as soft.

Two even beginners fighting with fists last about 20 seconds; with weapons, a little less. Light weapons give steady fights; heavy ones are swingier, as each blow counts for more.

Armour doesn't protect you yet. Parrying and blocking with a shield are planned.

## Health

Your Health is the red half of the circle at the bottom of the screen, and comes mostly from Toughness. Anyone who is hurt shows a red bar under their token until they are whole again. You can't take stairs or leave a place in a fight. Out of a fight for 10 seconds, Health refills, from empty to full in about five minutes. Mana, the blue half, comes mostly from Will and refills all the time, fighting or not, at the same pace.

## Holding weapons

Weapons are held, not worn: drag one onto the Held row of the clothes panel in the Character panel. You have two hands. A two-handed weapon takes both. Creatures without hands always fight with their bodies.

With a weapon in each hand you **fight with two weapons**: your hands take turns, the bigger weapon first. Your other hand hits softer, and you get quicker as you learn the Dual Wielding skill, until a master attacks about a quarter faster than with one weapon. Untrained, fighting with two weapons is worse than with one.

## Getting better

You learn by doing. Every blow that lands teaches you the skill of the weapon you used (and fighting with two weapons, if you are), and being attacked teaches you to defend with what you hold. Each skill learns from at most one blow every five seconds, so a quick weapon learns no faster than a slow one. Skills come quickly at first and ever more slowly towards mastery, which takes a great deal of fighting. Creatures you have fought a lot teach you less, until your skill catches up with how well you know them, so you learn most from fighting many kinds. Your skills are on the Skills page (K).

## Dying

When your Health runs out you are killed, and told so. You come back on the world map outside the place where you died (or outside the wilderness), whole again and with everything you carried.
