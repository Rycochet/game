Web based, preferably on Facebook


Town, buildings in it, support a party who explore the dungeons under it
Party gets raw materials (ore, skins and gems) for making equipment by exploring
Make equipment and possibly have wizards enchant it in the town
PvP arena, doesn't damage target, but your own party suffers damage
Global market for selling equipment






Town
====
Buildings can level up to greater forms allowing better contents - costs gold


Bar -> Tavern -> Inn
--------------------
Recruit party members here
Party members rest here to heal, takes 12 hours to heal to full from minimum health
Selection to recruit depends on building level
Costs gold to hire characters


Resurrection Shrine
-------------------
Party members who die come back here, otherwise they stay dead
Takes 24 hours to recover, but no loss of equipment / levels / exp


Armoursmith
-----------
Creates metal armour
Higher levels allow higher level ores to be worked


Weaponsmith
-----------
Creates metal weapons
Higher levels allow higher level ores to be worked


Tailor
------
Creates leather armour, robes and cloaks
Higher levels allow higher level skins to be worked


Jeweller
--------
Creates rings and polishes gems
Can insert gems into weapons and armour, one gem, level matches (or lower than) the plus on it
Higher levels allow higher level gems to be worked


Arena
-----
Gives access to PvP (?) fights with your party
Your party suffers real damage that requires time to heal
You get gold and experience as rewards for winning
Party members can only fight arena battles with similar levels of other party members


Workshop
--------
Can assign a wizard here and they can enchant a weapon
Time is 4 hours per plus to enchant, can only go up one level at a time (so 12 hours total to +2)
Can only enchant to wizard level / 10 rounded down (so level 10 for +1), max level +5


Chapel
------
Can assign a cleric here and they can create potions from raw ingredients


Monastary
---------
Increases heal rate in Tavern etc


Black Market
------------
Allows trade between players


Fletcher
--------
Creates ranged weapons








Heroes
======
Each class has different strengths, weaknesses and equipment limits
Heroes are assigned a secondary profession, in one of the other buildings, and they gain exp in it while not adventuring
* Must remember to assign a hero to a building to man it and to gain exp
* Can only heal while in Tavern
Can only work in certain buildings depending on class (stats?)
Stats: Str, Dex, Int, Con (start with a single 3, a single 2 and two at 1)
* Strength = hit hard
* Dexterity = dodge + hit often
* Intelligence = use magic + spot stuff
* Constitution = health + resistences
* Every level get one stat point to spend
* Health = 10 + (2 x con)
Magic: Divine, Wild, Arcane
* Divine = healing + curing
* Wild = animal control + alteration
* Arcane = damage + protection
Combat:
* attack = rand(dex) + weapon_plus
* defense = rand(dex + armour) + armour_plus
* damage = (rand(str) + weapon) * (attack / defense)


Fighter 3,1,1,2
-------
Heavy armour, all weapons, no magic


Thief 1,3,2,1
-----
Light armour, small weapons, no magic, traps + chests


Paladin 3,1,1,2
-------
Heavy armour, sharp weapons, no jewelry, divine magic / 2


Cleric 2,1,3,1
------
Medium armour, blunt weapons, divine magic


Ranger 1,3,1,2
------
Medium armour, ranged weapons, wild magic / 2


Druid 1,1,3,2
-----
No armour, no weapons, wild magic


Wizard 1,2,3,1
------
No armour, no weapons, arcane magic


Spellsword 3,1,2,1
----------
Medium armour, sharp weapons, arcane magic / 2






Dungeons
========
Dungeons are in level bands, one building per band
Each dungeon is 10 (???) "levels" deep, each has 5x4 (20) (???) rooms, random room has stairs down
Once a floor is cleared it will stay cleared for 2 hours
Each room has a random chance of a monster encounter (level appropriate)
- Winning an encounter gives a chance of a treasure, but always gives gold and party experience
Each room has a chance of ores
Roguelike exploration - click room or explore?
